short_description: 'Saarland University (German: Universität des Saarlandes, pronounced
  [univɛʁziˈtɛːt dɛs ˈzaːɐ̯landəs]) is a public research university located in Saarbrücken,
  the capital of the German state of Saarland. It was founded in 1948 in Homburg in
  co-operation with France and is organized in six faculties that cover all major
  fields of science.  In 2007, the university was recognized as an excellence center
  for computer science in Germany.

  Thanks to bilingual German and French staff, the university has an international
  profile, which has been underlined by its proclamation as "European University"
  in 1950 and by establishment of Europa-Institut as its "crown and symbol" in 1951.

  Ten academics have been honored with the highest German research prize, the Gottfried
  Wilhelm Leibniz Prize, while working at Saarland University.'
