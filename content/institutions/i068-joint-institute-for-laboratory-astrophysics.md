short_description: JILA is a world-leading atomic, molecular and optical (AMO) physics
  institute in Boulder, Colorado, jointly operated by the National Institute of Standards
  and Technology (NIST) and the University of Colorado Boulder. Originally founded
  in 1962 as the Joint Institute for Laboratory Astrophysics, it now goes by the acronym
  JILA alone. Its faculty — including Nobel laureates Eric Cornell, Carl Wieman and
  Jan Hall — pioneered Bose–Einstein condensation, the fermionic condensate, optical
  frequency combs and the world's most precise optical atomic clocks, and today lead
  research on ultracold molecules, Rydberg-atom arrays and quantum simulation.
