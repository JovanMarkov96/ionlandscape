The Quantum Hub is a central facet of the Israeli quantum ecosystem, functioning not as a hardware or software developer, but as a crucial nexus for collaboration. The Hub fosters relationships between commercial enterprises, academic researchers, startup founders, and government entities to accelerate the maturation and commercial deployment of Israeli quantum technologies globally.
