Ioan Pop is an experimental physicist at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) working on protected superconducting qubits. His group develops fluxonium qubits and high-kinetic-inductance superinductors made from granular aluminum, pursuing low-loss materials, long coherence, and intrinsically protected qubit designs.
