Robert McDermott is an experimental physicist at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. His group works on superconducting qubit readout and control — including single-flux-quantum (SFQ) digital control of qubits — and on understanding materials defects, quasiparticles and radiation as sources of qubit decoherence.
