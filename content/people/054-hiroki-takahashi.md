Hiroki Takahashi is an Associate Professor at OIST, where he leads the Experimental Quantum Information Physics Unit. His research focuses on trapped-ion quantum networks and cavity QED with calcium and barium ions.

He received his PhD in Quantum Optics from the University of Tokyo. He spent over 8 years in the UK at the University of Sussex working with Matthias Keller on ion-cavity quantum interfaces, producing the landmark 2020 PRL paper demonstrating strong coupling of a single ion to an optical cavity. He joined OIST in 2019.
