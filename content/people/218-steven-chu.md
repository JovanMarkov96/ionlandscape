Steven Chu shared the 1997 Nobel Prize in Physics for the laser cooling and trapping of atoms. A professor at Stanford, he later served as the 12th U.S. Secretary of Energy (2009-2013) and mentored a generation of atomic physicists, including Nir Davidson.
