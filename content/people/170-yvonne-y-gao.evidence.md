## Research focus summary

Yvonne Y. Gao leads the qcrew group at NUS/CQT, specializing in bosonic quantum error correction using superconducting microwave circuits. Her work spans cat qubit encodings, grid-state preparation, bosonic cQED gate operations, and quantum information processing with multi-photon states of superconducting cavities. She is a leading figure in hardware-efficient bosonic QEC on the superconducting platform.
