Silke Ospelkaus is a Professor of Physics at Leibniz University Hannover, where her group produces ultracold ground-state polar molecules — sodium-potassium (NaK) and potassium-rubidium (KRb) — to study ultracold chemistry, dipolar collisions and quantum-controlled reactions.

She completed her PhD at the University of Hamburg with Klaus Sengstock and was a postdoctoral researcher at JILA/NIST in the group of Deborah S. Jin, where she helped create the first high-phase-space-density gas of polar KRb molecules and demonstrate quantum-state-controlled chemical reactions.
