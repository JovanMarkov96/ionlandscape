Rudolf Grimm is a Professor of Physics at the University of Innsbruck and the Institute for Quantum Optics and Quantum Information (IQOQI), where his groups study strongly interacting ultracold gases of lithium (Li), cesium (Cs) and erbium (Er). His work produced the first molecular Bose-Einstein condensate, fermionic superfluidity in the BEC-BCS crossover, the first observation of Efimov states, and dipolar quantum gases of erbium.

He is a foundational figure in the physics of Feshbach resonances and ultracold few- and many-body systems.
