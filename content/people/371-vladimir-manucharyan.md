Vladimir Manucharyan is a Professor of Physics at EPFL (and formerly the University of Maryland), where his group develops protected superconducting qubits. He invented the fluxonium qubit — a superconducting circuit built from a Josephson junction shunted by a large 'superinductance' that is insensitive to charge noise — and pursues high-coherence, intrinsically protected qubit designs using high-kinetic-inductance superinductors.

He created fluxonium during his PhD at Yale University with Michel Devoret, and his group has since demonstrated record coherence and high-fidelity gates with fluxonium circuits.
