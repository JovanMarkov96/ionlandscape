Sebastian Will is an Associate Professor of Physics at Columbia University, where his group creates dipolar quantum matter from ultracold polar molecules. The Will Lab assembles ground-state sodium-cesium (NaCs) molecules, including in optical tweezer arrays, to explore dipolar many-body physics, ultracold chemistry and molecular qubits.

He completed his PhD with Immanuel Bloch (Mainz and LMU Munich) on ultracold atoms as quantum simulators, then moved to MIT as a postdoctoral researcher with Martin Zwierlein, where he helped create one of the first ultracold gases of fermionic NaK molecules. He started his laboratory at Columbia in 2018.
