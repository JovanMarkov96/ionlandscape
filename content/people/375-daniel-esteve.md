Daniel Esteve is a senior physicist who led the Quantronics group at CEA Saclay, a pioneering laboratory of superconducting quantum circuits. With Denis Vion and Michel Devoret, his group built the 'quantronium' — an early superconducting qubit operated at a noise-insensitive 'sweet spot' that achieved long coherence — and made foundational studies of single-Cooper-pair and single-electron devices and of decoherence in Josephson-junction qubits.

His work helped establish the design principles for coherent superconducting quantum bits.
