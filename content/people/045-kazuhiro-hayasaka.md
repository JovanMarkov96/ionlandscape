Kazuhiro Hayasaka leads the Space-Time Standards Laboratory at NICT in Kobe, Japan. His research focuses on developing high-precision optical clocks using trapped indium and calcium ions for frequency standards and metrology. He was also a co-author on early seminal work demonstrating continuous single-photon generation in an ion-trap cavity system.

He received his PhD in Physics from Osaka University.
