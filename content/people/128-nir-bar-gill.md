Prof. Nir Bar-Gill is a Full Professor jointly appointed in the Racah Institute of Physics and the Department of Applied Physics at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. He heads the Quantum Information, Simulation and Sensing (QISS) lab.

He received his BSc and MSc in Physics from the Technion and completed his PhD at the Weizmann Institute of Science in 2010 under Prof. Nir Davidson, where he studied ultracold atoms. He then moved to Ron Walsworth's group at Harvard University for postdoctoral research, pivoting to nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centres in diamond and their applications in many-body spin physics, quantum coherence, and nanoscale sensing. He joined the Hebrew University in 2013.

His research focuses on exploiting NV colour centres in diamond for quantum information processing, quantum simulation, and ultra-sensitive magnetic sensing. Landmark achievements include demonstrating spin coherence times approaching one second in a solid-state system and developing nanoscale NMR/MRI modalities using NV ensembles.
