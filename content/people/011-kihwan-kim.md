Kihwan Kim is a leading trapped-ion quantum computing experimentalist and Director of the Center for Trapped Ion Quantum Science at the Institute for Basic Science (IBS) in South Korea, a position he assumed in late 2025. 

Prior to returning to South Korea, he was a Professor in the Department of Physics at Tsinghua University (2011–2025), where he led a highly successful trapped-ion research group. He earned his bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees in physics from Seoul National University, followed by postdoctoral research at the University of Innsbruck and the University of Maryland.

His research focuses on developing trapped-ion quantum processors, including 1D linear ion chains, dual-species architectures, and 2D ion crystals. He is particularly known for high-fidelity control, multi-qubit entangling operations (such as global entangling gates on arbitrary qubits), achieving hour-long coherence times for single-ion qubits, and groundbreaking quantum simulations of spin models and bosonic dynamics.
