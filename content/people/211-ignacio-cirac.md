J. Ignacio Cirac is a theoretical physicist and director of the Theory Division at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics. With Peter Zoller he proposed the 1995 Cirac-Zoller scheme for a trapped-ion quantum gate -- the blueprint for much of trapped-ion quantum computing -- and has made foundational contributions to quantum simulation and tensor-network methods.
