John Chiaverini leads trapped-ion research at MIT Lincoln Laboratory. His work focuses on scalable quantum computing architectures, integrated photonics for ion traps, and surface-electrode trap technology.

He received his PhD from Stanford University in 2002 working under Aharon Kapitulnik. He then joined David Wineland's group at NIST Boulder as a postdoctoral researcher, where he contributed significantly to early work on surface-electrode ion traps and trapped-ion quantum error correction.
