Stephan Schiller is a Professor of Physics at Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, where his group performs ultra-high-precision spectroscopy of trapped molecular hydrogen ions — HD+ and H2+ — to test quantum electrodynamics, determine fundamental constants such as the proton-to-electron mass ratio, and search for new physics.

His broad contributions to precision optics also include early work on squeezed light and quantum-state reconstruction and the development of optical frequency-comb spectroscopy.
