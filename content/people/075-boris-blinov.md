Boris B. Blinov leads the Trapped Ion Quantum Computing group at the University of Washington, focusing on barium ion quantum networks and ion-photon entanglement. His early seminal work included the first observation of entanglement between a single trapped atom and a single photon.

He received his PhD from the University of Michigan in 2000 working in accelerator physics, before pivoting to quantum information as a postdoctoral researcher in Christopher Monroe's group, also at the University of Michigan, from 2001 to 2004.
