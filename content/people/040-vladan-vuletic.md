Vladan Vuletić is the Lester Wolfe Professor of Physics at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT). His experimental research focuses on ultracold atoms, quantum optics, many-body quantum entanglement, cavity QED, and quantum information processing. While primarily known for his pioneering work with neutral atoms, he has also made significant contributions to trapped-ion cavity QED interfaces.

He completed his PhD at LMU Munich (1997) under Theodor W. Hänsch. Following a brief postdoctoral stay at MPQ, he moved to Stanford University as a postdoctoral fellow under Steven Chu (1997–2000). He joined the MIT faculty in 2003 and was promoted to Full Professor in 2011.

In addition to his academic roles, he is a Co-founder and the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) of QuEra Computing Inc., an industry-leading company developing neutral-atom quantum computers based on Rydberg atom array technology.
