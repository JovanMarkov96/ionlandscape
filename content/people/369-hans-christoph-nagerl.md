Hans-Christoph Nägerl is a Professor of Physics at the University of Innsbruck, where his group studies ultracold cesium (Cs) gases and Feshbach molecules, including one-dimensional quantum gases (Tonks-Girardeau and sine-Gordon physics) and the creation of ultracold ground-state Cs2 and RbCs molecules.

He earned his PhD at Innsbruck with Rainer Blatt working on trapped-ion quantum information before moving to ultracold neutral atoms and molecules with the Innsbruck cold-atom groups.
