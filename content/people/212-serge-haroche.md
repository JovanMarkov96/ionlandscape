Serge Haroche is a French physicist who shared the 2012 Nobel Prize in Physics with David Wineland for ground-breaking experimental methods that enable measuring and manipulating individual quantum systems. At the Ecole Normale Superieure and College de France he pioneered cavity quantum electrodynamics, trapping single photons and probing them non-destructively with Rydberg atoms.
