Jason Petta is a Professor of Physics at UCLA (previously Princeton) who develops semiconductor quantum-dot spin qubits in GaAs and silicon. His work established coherent control of singlet-triplet spin qubits, demonstrated a coherent spin-photon interface coupling a single spin to a microwave photon, and realized two-qubit gates in silicon.
