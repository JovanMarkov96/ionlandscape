Ben P. Lanyon leads the Distributed Quantum Systems group at the University of Innsbruck, demonstrating long-distance entanglement distribution with trapped calcium ions and quantum networking protocols. He was also the lead author on a seminal 2011 paper demonstrating universal digital quantum simulation with trapped ions.

He received his PhD from the University of Queensland in 2009 under Andrew White, before joining Rainer Blatt's group at the University of Innsbruck as a postdoctoral researcher.
