## Key Papers

### 1. Parallelized telecom quantum networking with a ytterbium-171 atom array
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41567-025-03022-4
- **Journal:** Nature Physics 21, 1826–1833 (2025)
- **Authors:** Lintao Li, Xiye Hu, Zhubing Jia, William Huie, Won Kyu Calvin Sun, Aakash, Yuhao Dong, Narisak Hiri-O-Tuppa, Jacob P. Covey
- **Role:** senior_author (Covey is last/corresponding author)
- **Source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-025-03022-4; arXiv:2502.17406
- **Significance:** First parallelized atom-photon entanglement at telecom wavelength using Yb-171 array; fidelity 0.95.

### 2. Quantum networks with neutral atom processing nodes
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41534-023-00759-9
- **Journal:** npj Quantum Information 9, 90 (2023)
- **Authors:** J. P. Covey, H. Weinfurter, H. Bernien
- **Role:** first_author (Covey is first/corresponding author)
- **Source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-023-00759-9; arXiv:2304.02088
- **Significance:** Review/perspective paper outlining the neutral-atom quantum networking architecture.

### 3. Repetitive readout and real-time control of nuclear spin qubits in 171Yb atoms
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.030337
- **Journal:** PRX Quantum 4, 030337 (2023)
- **Authors:** W. Huie, L. Li, N. Chen, X. Hu, Z. Jia, W. K. C. Sun, J. P. Covey
- **Role:** senior_author (Covey is last/corresponding author)
- **Source:** https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.030337; arXiv:2305.02926
- **Significance:** Nondestructive midcircuit measurement of Yb-171 qubits with 99% success rate; key for quantum error correction.

### 4. High-fidelity entanglement and detection of alkaline-earth Rydberg atoms
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41567-020-0903-z
- **Journal:** Nature Physics 16, 857–861 (2020)
- **Authors:** Ivaylo S. Madjarov, Jacob P. Covey, Adam L. Shaw, Joonhee Choi, Anant Kale, Alexandre Cooper, Hannes Pichler, Vladimir Schkolnik, Jason R. Williams, Manuel Endres
- **Role:** co_author (Covey is second author; work done as postdoc at Caltech with Endres)
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-020-0903-z; arXiv:2001.04455
- **Significance:** Landmark demonstration of high-fidelity Rydberg entanglement in alkaline-earth (Sr) atoms.

### 5. 2000-times repeated imaging of strontium atoms in clock-magic tweezer arrays
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.173201
- **Journal:** Physical Review Letters 122, 173201 (2019)
- **Authors:** Jacob P. Covey, Ivaylo S. Madjarov, Alexandre Cooper, Manuel Endres
- **Role:** first_author
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.173201; arXiv:1811.06014
- **Significance:** Early demonstration of clock-magic tweezer arrays with repeated nondestructive imaging; foundation for Yb/Sr qubit work.
