Mirko Lobino leads quantum photonics and trapped-ion research at Griffith University in Brisbane, Australia, focusing on hybrid quantum systems and interfacing single trapped ions with quantum photonic circuits.

He completed his PhD at Politecnico di Milano in 2007 under Roberta Ramponi, and subsequently held a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Calgary.
