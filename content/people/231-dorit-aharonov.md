Dorit Aharonov is a theoretical computer scientist at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem renowned for the quantum threshold theorem (fault-tolerant quantum computation), adiabatic quantum computing, and quantum complexity. She is a co-founder and chief scientific officer of the quantum-error-mitigation company Qedma.
