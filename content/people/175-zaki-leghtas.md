Zaki Leghtas is a physicist at Mines Paris-PSL and the ENS who works at the interface of theory and experiment on bosonic superconducting qubits. His work develops dissipatively stabilized 'cat' qubits — Schrödinger-cat states of a microwave cavity protected by engineered two-photon dissipation — for hardware-efficient quantum error correction.
