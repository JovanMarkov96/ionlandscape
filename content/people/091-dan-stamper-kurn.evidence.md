## Key Papers

### 1. Optical Confinement of a Bose-Einstein Condensate (1998)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.80.2027
- **Journal:** Physical Review Letters 80, 2027 (1998)
- **Authors:** D.M. Stamper-Kurn, M.R. Andrews, A.P. Chikkatur, S. Inouye, H.-J. Miesner, J. Stenger, W. Ketterle
- **Role:** first_author (lead author)
- **Significance:** First demonstration of BEC confinement in an optical dipole trap, enabling spinor condensate studies.

### 2. Spin domains in ground-state Bose–Einstein condensates (1998)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/24567
- **Journal:** Nature 396, 345–348 (1998)
- **Authors:** J. Stenger, S. Inouye, D.M. Stamper-Kurn, H.-J. Miesner, A.P. Chikkatur, W. Ketterle
- **Role:** co_author
- **Significance:** Founding paper on spinor BEC physics — first observation of spin domain formation in optically trapped sodium BEC.

### 3. Non-classical light generated by quantum-noise-driven cavity optomechanics (2012)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature11325
- **Journal:** Nature 488, 476–480 (2012)
- **Authors:** D.W.C. Brooks, T. Botter, S. Schreppler, T.P. Purdy, N. Brahms, D.M. Stamper-Kurn
- **Role:** senior_author (corresponding)
- **Significance:** First direct observation of quantum effects (ponderomotive squeezing) in an optomechanical system using ultracold atoms in a cavity.

### 4. Spinor Bose gases: Symmetries, magnetism, and quantum dynamics (2013)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/RevModPhys.85.1191
- **Journal:** Reviews of Modern Physics 85, 1191–1244 (2013)
- **Authors:** D.M. Stamper-Kurn, M. Ueda
- **Role:** first_author
- **Significance:** Comprehensive review defining the field of spinor BEC; 1000+ citations.

### 5. Coherent Magnon Optics in a Ferromagnetic Spinor Bose-Einstein Condensate (2014)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.113.155302
- **Journal:** Physical Review Letters 113, 155302 (2014)
- **Authors:** G.E. Marti, A. MacRae, R. Olf, S. Lourette, F. Fang, D.M. Stamper-Kurn
- **Role:** senior_author
- **Significance:** Demonstration of spin-wave (magnon) optics in a ferromagnetic BEC.
