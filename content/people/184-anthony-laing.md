Anthony Laing is Professor of Physics and Co-Director of QET Labs (Quantum Engineering Technology Labs) at the University of Bristol. He is a leading experimentalist in integrated photonic quantum computing and simulation. His group pioneers reconfigurable waveguide chips that implement quantum walks, boson sampling, and molecular vibrational simulations — using programmable silica-on-silicon and silicon photonic circuits to process quantum information with single photons. He invented the Reference Frame Independent Quantum Key Distribution (RFI-QKD) protocol (2010, patented with Nokia) and contributed to landmark demonstrations including the first fully reconfigurable entanglement chip (Nature Photonics 2012), the universal linear optics processor (Science 2015), and photonic molecular dynamics simulation (Nature 2018). In 2016 he was appointed Lecturer in Physics and awarded a 5-year EPSRC Quantum Technologies Fellowship; he was later promoted to full Professor. He obtained both his MSc and PhD from the University of Bristol, working in Jeremy O'Brien's Centre for Quantum Photonics. In 2020 he co-founded Duality Quantum Photonics (with Alberto Politi as CTO), a Bristol spin-out developing scalable integrated photonic quantum hardware, and serves as its CEO.
