Francesca Ferlaino is a Professor of Physics at the University of Innsbruck and a director at the Institute for Quantum Optics and Quantum Information (IQOQI), where her group creates strongly dipolar quantum gases of the magnetic lanthanides erbium (Er) and dysprosium (Dy). Her lab achieved Bose-Einstein condensation of erbium and observed dipolar quantum droplets and supersolid states of matter.

She earned her PhD at the University of Florence with Massimo Inguscio and built her dipolar-gas program in Innsbruck, where she collaborated with Rudolf Grimm.
