Frank Wilhelm-Mauch is a theoretical physicist at Saarland University and Forschungszentrum Jülich. His work develops quantum optimal control and high-fidelity gate design for superconducting qubits, the theory of decoherence in Josephson circuits, and the scientific roadmaps for European superconducting quantum processors (OpenSuperQ).
