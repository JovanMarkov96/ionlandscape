Kilian Singer leads the Trapped Ion Quantum Information and Thermodynamics group at the University of Kassel. His research focuses on single-ion heat engines, thermodynamics in the quantum regime, and scalable ion trap technologies using calcium ions.

He received his PhD from the University of Heidelberg and the Max-Planck-Institut für Kernphysik (MPIK) in 2004, working in the group of Matthias Weidemüller on ultracold Rydberg atoms.
