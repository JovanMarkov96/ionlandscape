## key_papers

- **10.1103/PhysRevA.69.062320** — Blais, Huang, Wallraff, Girvin, Schoelkopf, PRA 2004. Foundational circuit QED proposal. Confirmed via https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.69.062320 and https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0402216. Blais is first author.
- **10.1038/nature02851** — Wallraff et al. (including Blais), Nature 2004. First experimental demonstration of strong coupling in circuit QED. Confirmed via https://www.nature.com/articles/nature02851. Blais is co-author (theory role).
- **10.1103/PhysRevA.76.042319** — Koch, Yu, Gambetta, Houck, Schuster, Majer, Blais et al., PRA 2007. Transmon qubit proposal. Confirmed via https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.76.042319.
- **10.1038/nature06184** — Majer, Chow, Gambetta, Koch et al. (including Blais), Nature 2007. Coupling superconducting qubits via a cavity bus. Confirmed via https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06184.
- **10.1103/RevModPhys.93.025005** — Blais, Grimsmo, Girvin, Wallraff, Rev. Mod. Phys. 2021. 82-page review of circuit QED. arXiv:2005.12667. Blais is first author. Confirmed via https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/RevModPhys.93.025005. (Note: this is the Reviews of Modern Physics paper, not Nature Physics. The Nature Physics 2020 paper on "quantum optics with circuit QED" has DOI 10.1038/s41567-020-0806-z — also co-authored by Blais et al.)
