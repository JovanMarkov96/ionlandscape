Cheng Chin is a Professor of Physics at the University of Chicago, where his group studies ultracold cesium (Cs) gases as a tunable platform for quantum many-body physics, using Feshbach resonances to control interactions. His lab has explored Efimov few-body physics, quantum simulation of phase transitions, and analogues of cosmological and condensed-matter phenomena in cold atoms.

He earned his PhD at Stanford University with Steven Chu and was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Innsbruck with Rudolf Grimm, where he contributed to early work on the BEC-BCS crossover and Efimov states, before joining Chicago.
