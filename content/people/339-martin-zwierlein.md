Martin Zwierlein is a Professor of Physics at MIT and a member of the MIT-Harvard Center for Ultracold Atoms, where his group studies strongly interacting ultracold Fermi gases and ultracold dipolar molecules. His laboratory created ultracold gases of fermionic sodium-potassium (NaK) molecules in their absolute ground state.

He earned his PhD at MIT with Wolfgang Ketterle, where he observed Bose-Einstein condensation of molecules, fermionic superfluidity and vortices, and imbalanced Fermi superfluids — landmark experiments in the physics of the BEC-BCS crossover.
