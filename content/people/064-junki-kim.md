Junki Kim is an Assistant Professor at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), where he leads the Quantum Engineering with Trapped Ions (QuETI) lab. His research focuses on trapped-ion quantum computing and simulation with ytterbium and barium ions, particularly architecting scalable quantum computers with ion shuttling.

He completed his postdoctoral training at Seoul National University (2017-2018) and at Duke University (2018-2021) working in the groups of Jungsang Kim and Kenneth Brown, before joining SKKU in 2021.
