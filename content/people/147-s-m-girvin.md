Steven Girvin is a theoretical physicist at Yale University and a principal architect of circuit quantum electrodynamics (circuit QED) — the theory describing superconducting qubits strongly coupled to microwave photons. His work underpins dispersive qubit readout and the field of bosonic quantum error correction, including cat codes and Gottesman-Kitaev-Preskill (GKP) codes that encode logical qubits in microwave cavity modes.

He is also known for foundational contributions to the theory of the fractional quantum Hall effect.
