Alain Aspect shared the 2022 Nobel Prize in Physics for experiments with entangled photons that established the violation of Bell inequalities and pioneered quantum information science. A professor at the Institut d'Optique, he is also a co-founder of the neutral-atom quantum-computing company Pasqal.
