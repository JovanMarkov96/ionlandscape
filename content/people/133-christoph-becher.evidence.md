## Data quality notes

- **Title**: "Professor of Quantum Optics" is confirmed by institutional page; ResearchGate lists "Department of Experimental Physics" as department affiliation.
- **Since year**: April 2005 per ScholarNexus. This is after ~2001–2004 Innsbruck postdoc period.
- **PhD advisor**: Not found in publicly accessible sources. The Kaiserslautern physics dept (mid-1990s) worked on quantum optics/laser physics. Advisor left unset (null).
- **Innsbruck connection**: Becher was a researcher in Rainer Blatt's group at Innsbruck, evidenced by co-authorship on multiple 2002–2003 ion-cavity QED papers. Blatt is in database as 017-rainer-blatt but schema does not model postdoc supervisors.
- **UCSB postdoc**: Produced the landmark Science 2000 paper on quantum dot single-photon turnstile with Michler, Imamoğlu et al.
- **Research focus**: Optical spectroscopy and quantum optics with colour centres in diamond (SiV, NV, SnV); quantum photonics with fiber/diamond microcavities; quantum frequency conversion to telecom; quantum repeater networks.
- **Current initiatives**: Leads QuTe (Center for Quantum Technologies at Saarland); PI in BMBF Quantenrepeater.Net (QR.N, €20M, 2025–); DFG CRC QuCoLiMa; conducted quantum teleportation across Saarbrücken fiber network (2024).
- **SnV (tin-vacancy)**: Group has expanded to SnV centers (e.g., Görlitz et al., npj QI 2022; Resch et al., PRX 2026). Platforms list kept as nv_center, color_center (which covers SiV/SnV under the broader color_center tag).
