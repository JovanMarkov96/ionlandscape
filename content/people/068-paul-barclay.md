Paul Barclay leads a quantum photonics research group at the University of Calgary focusing on hybrid quantum systems, diamond-based quantum technologies, and nanophotonics. His work explores coupling trapped ions and solid-state defects (like NV centers) to nanophotonic cavities for enhanced light-matter interaction.

He received his PhD in Applied Physics from Caltech in 2007, working under Oskar Painter.
