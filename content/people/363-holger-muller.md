Holger Müller is a Professor of Physics at the University of California, Berkeley, where his group builds precision atom interferometers with cesium (Cs) atoms to test fundamental physics. His measurement of the fine-structure constant via the recoil of cesium atoms is among the most precise tests of the Standard Model and of quantum electrodynamics.

His work also includes matter-wave tests of the equivalence principle, searches for dark matter and dark energy, and the development of optical-cavity-enhanced atom interferometry. He was a postdoctoral researcher at Stanford with Steven Chu.
