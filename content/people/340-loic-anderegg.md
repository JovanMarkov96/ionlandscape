Loïc Anderegg is an Assistant Professor of Physics at the University of Southern California, where his group develops optical-tweezer arrays of laser-cooled diatomic and polyatomic molecules for quantum science, including calcium monofluoride (CaF) and calcium monohydroxide (CaOH).

He earned his PhD at Harvard University with John M. Doyle, where he carried out pioneering work on laser slowing, magneto-optical trapping and optical-tweezer trapping of CaF molecules.
