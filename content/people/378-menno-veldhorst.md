Menno Veldhorst is an Associate Professor at Delft University of Technology (QuTech) who develops semiconductor spin qubits in silicon and germanium quantum dots. He was first author on the first two-qubit logic gate in silicon and built a four-qubit germanium quantum processor based on hole spins, pursuing dense, scalable quantum-dot arrays.
