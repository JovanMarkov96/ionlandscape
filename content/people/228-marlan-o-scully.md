Marlan O. Scully is a theoretical physicist at Texas A&M (and affiliated with Princeton and Baylor) known for foundational work in quantum optics, including the laser phase-transition analogy, lasing without inversion, the micromaser, and quantum coherence effects. He co-authored the standard text Quantum Optics.
