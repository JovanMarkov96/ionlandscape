Andrea Morello is a Professor of Physics at UNSW Sydney who builds spin qubits from single phosphorus donor atoms implanted in silicon. His group demonstrated single-atom electron- and nuclear-spin qubits with high-fidelity readout and control, record coherence times, and small nuclear-spin quantum processors — a route to silicon-based quantum computing compatible with existing microelectronics.
