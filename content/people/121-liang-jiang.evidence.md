## Data quality notes

- **Lab URL**: The Yale Jiang Group page (jianggroup.yale.edu) still exists but is outdated; the active lab is at UChicago. A UChicago lab URL (jianggroup.uchicago.edu) is listed — verify it resolves.
- **PhD advisor confirmed**: Both the Caltech Heritage Project interview and community sources (New Quantum Era podcast) confirm Mikhail Lukin as PhD advisor at Harvard.
- **`since_year` 2019**: CQE welcome announcement and multiple profile pages confirm 2019 as the year Jiang joined UChicago PME. Note: the Caltech Heritage page says "2020-present" which may reflect a slight discrepancy; the CQE news announcement explicitly says 2019, and the PME page is consistent.
- **key_papers[0] and [1] roles**: Jiang was a PhD student (Lukin group) on these papers; "co_author" is used as he is a named middle author.
- **key_papers[4] role**: Nature 2025 paper has many authors from AWS; Jiang is a contributing co-author on the concatenated bosonic qubit architecture, not the sole senior author.
- **Bosonic codes / cat codes**: Jiang has extensive theory work on GKP codes, cat codes, and surface-GKP concatenation (Albert et al. 2018, Noh et al. 2020 etc.) which further support the 'computing' application tag but were not added as key_papers to keep the list to the most-cited and representative works.
