Tim Langen is a Professor of Physics at TU Wien, where he leads the Cold Molecules and Quantum Technologies division at the Atominstitut. His group develops laser cooling of aluminium monofluoride (AlF) molecules, a candidate for producing dense samples of ultracold molecules for quantum technology.

He earned his PhD at TU Wien with Jörg Schmiedmayer, studying thermalization and prethermalization in isolated one-dimensional quantum gases, and was a group leader at the University of Stuttgart working on dipolar quantum droplets and supersolids before returning to Vienna.
