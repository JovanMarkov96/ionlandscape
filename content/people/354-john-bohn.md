John Bohn is a Fellow of JILA and Professor Adjoint at the University of Colorado Boulder, working on the theory of ultracold molecules, dipolar collisions and cold controlled chemistry. His theoretical work has guided landmark experiments on quantum-state-controlled reactions and dipolar collisions of ultracold KRb molecules.

He is a long-standing collaborator of the JILA ultracold-molecule experiments and a co-author of influential reviews on the quantum engineering of chemistry and quantum matter with cold molecules.
