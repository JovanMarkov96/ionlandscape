Atsushi Noguchi is an Associate Professor at the University of Tokyo, where he leads research on hybrid quantum systems combining trapped ions (specifically beryllium) with superconducting circuits.

He completed his PhD at Osaka University (2015) in the group of Shinji Urabe and Kenji Toyoda. During his doctoral studies, he was a key contributor to the landmark 2015 Nature paper demonstrating Hong-Ou-Mandel interference of phonons in trapped ions.
