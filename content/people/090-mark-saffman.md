Mark Saffman is the Johannes Rydberg Professor of Physics at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, where his group builds neutral-atom quantum computers from optical-tweezer and optical-lattice arrays of cesium (Cs) and rubidium (Rb) atoms, using Rydberg-blockade interactions to implement entangling gates.

He is a leading figure in Rydberg-based quantum computing and helped translate the platform toward industry; his proposals and demonstrations of Rydberg gates underpin much of today's neutral-atom quantum-processor effort.
