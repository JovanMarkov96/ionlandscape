Kjeld S. E. Eikema leads the Ultrafast Laser Physics and Precision Metrology group at VU Amsterdam and is a Group Leader at the Advanced Research Center for Nanolithography (ARCNL). His research combines frequency combs with trapped ions and neutral atoms (such as helium) for extreme precision measurements and tests of fundamental physics and Quantum Electrodynamics (QED).

He received his PhD from the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam in 1996 working in the group of Wim Hogervorst.
