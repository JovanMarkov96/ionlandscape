Rupert Ursin (born 26 January 1973) is an Austrian experimental physicist and Senior Research Group Leader and Deputy Director at IQOQI Vienna (Institute for Quantum Optics and Quantum Information, Austrian Academy of Sciences). He completed his PhD in 2006 at the University of Vienna under Anton Zeilinger, working on quantum teleportation over long distances. He is best known for distributing entangled photons over a 144 km free-space link between the Canary Islands (La Palma–Tenerife, 2007), and for co-authoring the first experimental demonstration of free-space decoy-state QKD over 144 km (PRL, 2007). He contributed to the Micius satellite-relayed intercontinental quantum network demonstration (PRL, 2018) and to the significant-loophole-free Bell test with entangled photons (PRL, 2015). He has received the Christian-Doppler-Prize (2009) and the Gründungspreis Phönix (2023), and is also CEO of Quantum Technology Laboratories GmbH.
