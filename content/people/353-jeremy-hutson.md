Jeremy Hutson is a Professor of Chemistry at Durham University and a leading theorist of ultracold molecules and molecular collisions. His work underpins the formation of ultracold molecules by magnetoassociation and STIRAP, the theory of Feshbach resonances, and the understanding of collisions and intermolecular forces in cold molecular gases such as RbCs.

He is the author of widely used scattering and bound-state codes (MOLSCAT, BOUND and FIELD) and has collaborated closely with the experimental groups that created ultracold ground-state polar molecules.
