Winfried K. Hensinger is a Professor of Quantum Technologies at the University of Sussex, where he leads the Sussex Ion Quantum Technology Group and directs the Sussex Centre for Quantum Technologies. He is also the Co-founder, Chief Scientist, and Chairman of Universal Quantum, a full-stack quantum computing company spun out of his lab in 2018.

His research focuses on scalable trapped-ion quantum computing, specifically pioneering the use of microwave control rather than traditional laser beams to execute quantum logic gates. His group is well known for developing practical, engineering-driven, utility-scale blueprints for modular trapped-ion quantum computers.

Prior to his role at Sussex, he completed his PhD at the University of Queensland (2002) and held a FOCUS Fellowship as a postdoctoral researcher in Christopher Monroe's group at the University of Michigan (2002–2005).
