Rachel Godun is a Principal Scientist at the National Physical Laboratory (NPL), where she leads the Ytterbium Ion Optical Clock team. Her research focuses on developing and improving trapped-ion optical clocks for next-generation timekeeping and fundamental physics tests, including constraints on the time variation of fundamental constants.

She received her DPhil from the University of Oxford (2000) under Keith Burnett, studying atom interferometry. After continuing ultracold-atom research at Oxford for several years, she joined NPL in 2008.
