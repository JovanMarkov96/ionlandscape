## Notable career facts
- Entered ENSET (now ENS Paris-Saclay) in 1984; agrégation in physics 1987
- 1992: Joined Institut d'Optique as CNRS research fellow
- 1998: Became professor at ENS Paris-Saclay quantum physics laboratory
- 2012–2017: Director of Aimé Cotton Laboratory
- 2019: French Fellows visiting scholar at Churchill College, Cambridge
- 2021: Senior member of Institut Universitaire de France (IUF)
- 2024: Awarded Jaffé Prize and Berthelot Medal from Institut de France
- Co-director of coordinated research lab on quantum sensors (LuMIn/Thales R&T)
- Coordinator of EQUIPEX+ e-DIAMANT (national diamond network for quantum applications)
