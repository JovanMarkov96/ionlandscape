## key_papers

### 10.1103/PhysRevLett.86.4267
- **Title**: Generation of Continuous Variable Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen Entanglement via the Kerr Nonlinearity in an Optical Fiber
- **Authors**: Ch. Silberhorn, P. K. Lam, O. Weiß, F. König, N. Korolkova, G. Leuchs
- **Journal**: Physical Review Letters, 86, 4267 (2001)
- **Role**: Leuchs is senior/corresponding author
- **Source**: https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.86.4267; arXiv: quant-ph/0103002
- **Significance**: First generation of continuous-variable EPR entanglement using the Kerr nonlinearity in optical fiber; two squeezed beams from a Sagnac interferometer beat the quantum noise limit by 4.0 dB in both quadratures after interference at a beamsplitter. Foundational result for fiber-optical CV quantum communication.

### 10.1103/PhysRevLett.89.167901
- **Title**: Continuous Variable Quantum Cryptography — Beating the 3 dB Loss Limit
- **Authors**: Ch. Silberhorn, T. C. Ralph, N. Lütkenhaus, G. Leuchs
- **Journal**: Physical Review Letters, 89, 167901 (2002)
- **Role**: Leuchs is senior author
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0204064; https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.89.167901
- **Significance**: Showed that CV-QKD with bright squeezed states can generate a secret key even when channel loss exceeds 50% (the 3 dB limit that defeats coherent-state protocols), by exploiting nonclassical correlations. Key theoretical contribution to practical CV-QKD security.

### 10.1103/PhysRevLett.91.233901
- **Title**: Sharper Focus for a Radially Polarized Light Beam
- **Authors**: R. Dorn, S. Quabis, G. Leuchs
- **Journal**: Physical Review Letters, 91, 233901 (2003)
- **Role**: Leuchs is senior/corresponding author
- **Source**: https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.91.233901; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14683185/
- **Significance**: First experimental demonstration that radial polarization allows tighter focus (0.16λ²) than linear polarization (0.26λ²) at high NA; the longitudinal electric field component at the focus is exploited. ~2,800 citations — Leuchs's most-cited single paper; initiated global research programmes on structured light and nano-optics.

### 10.1103/PhysRevA.65.052306
- **Title**: Polarization Squeezing and Continuous-Variable Polarization Entanglement
- **Authors**: N. Korolkova, G. Leuchs, R. Loudon, T. C. Ralph, Ch. Silberhorn
- **Journal**: Physical Review A, 65, 052306 (2002)
- **Role**: Leuchs is co-author / group leader
- **Source**: https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.65.052306; arXiv: quant-ph/0108098
- **Significance**: Introduced the concept of polarization squeezing and continuous-variable polarization entanglement using Stokes-parameter operators; opened the polarization CV-QI sub-field and provided a new encoding basis for quantum communication.

### 10.1098/rsta.2016.0235
- **Title**: Quantum Communication with Coherent States of Light
- **Authors**: Imran Khan, Dominique Elser, Thomas Dirmeier, Christoph Marquardt, Gerd Leuchs
- **Journal**: Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A, 375(2099), 20160235 (2017)
- **Role**: Leuchs is senior/corresponding author
- **Source**: https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rsta.2016.0235
- **Significance**: Review of the Erlangen group's experimental programme on coherent-state CV-QKD, covering telecom-device integration, satellite quantum channel demonstrations, and perspectives on global QKD infrastructure.
