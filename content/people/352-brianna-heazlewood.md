Brianna Heazlewood is a Professor of Physical Chemistry at the University of Liverpool, where her group studies cold, controlled chemical reactions between trapped molecular ions held in Coulomb crystals and velocity-selected beams of neutral radicals produced by a Zeeman decelerator, probing reaction dynamics at low temperatures with full control over the reactants.

She earned her PhD at the University of Sydney with Scott H. Kable and was a postdoctoral researcher and group leader at the University of Oxford with Timothy P. Softley before establishing her independent laboratory.
