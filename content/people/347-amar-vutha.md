Amar Vutha is an Associate Professor of Physics at the University of Toronto, where his group pursues precision measurements with molecules to search for physics beyond the Standard Model, including a novel approach to the electron's electric dipole moment using polar molecules frozen into a solid inert-gas (neon) matrix.

He earned his PhD at Yale University in 2011 with David DeMille, contributing to the design and analysis of the ACME electron-EDM experiment based on thorium monoxide (ThO).
