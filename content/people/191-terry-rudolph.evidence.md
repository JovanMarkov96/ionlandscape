## Data quality notes

- **BSc year (1994)**: Sourced from Wikipedia only; no primary University of Queensland source found.
- **PhD**: Strongly confirmed by Mathematics Genealogy Project (primary source for academic genealogy). Advisor Helen Freedhoff was a quantum optics theorist at York University.
- **Postdoc at University of Toronto vs. lecturer**: Multiple sources describe Rudolph as "lecturing at University of Toronto" 1998–2000 before his postdoc in Vienna. This is a lecturer/teaching role, not a research postdoc, so it is not listed in `postdocs[]`.
- **Imperial College professorship**: Rudolph joined Imperial in 2003 on an Advanced Fellowship; became full professor 2011 or 2012 (sources differ slightly). He took leave in 2016 to co-found PsiQuantum. The Imperial position is secondary/on-leave; PsiQuantum is listed as primary current position.
- **Grandson of Erwin Schrödinger**: Widely reported biographical detail (Wikipedia, HandWiki, Grokipedia); Rudolph did not know this connection until after completing his physics degree.
- **OpenAlex metrics**: The OpenAlex API call for A5086614028 returned a different author (Russian researcher), suggesting a data quality issue or redirect. Google Scholar metrics used instead.
- **FBQC paper role**: The FBQC Nature Communications paper (2023) has 13 co-authors; Rudolph is listed alphabetically. He invented the FBQC concept (confirmed by PsiQuantum bio) but is not marked as "senior_author" since the paper is a large collaboration.
