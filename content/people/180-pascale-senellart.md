Pascale Senellart is a physicist at the Centre for Nanoscience and Nanotechnology (C2N, CNRS) near Paris who develops bright, highly indistinguishable single-photon sources based on semiconductor quantum dots deterministically coupled to optical microcavities — a leading resource for photonic quantum computing.
