Crystal Senko is an Associate Professor at the Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC) at the University of Waterloo, and the Canada Research Chair in Trapped Ion Quantum Computing. Her research focuses on trapped-ion quantum simulation and computing, particularly utilizing multi-level qudits in barium ions. She co-founded Open Quantum Design to create the world's first open-source, full-stack trapped-ion quantum computer.

She received her PhD from the University of Maryland (2014) working under Christopher Monroe on quantum many-body spin chains. She then completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the MIT-Harvard Center for Ultracold Atoms (CUA) under Mikhail Lukin, before joining IQC.
