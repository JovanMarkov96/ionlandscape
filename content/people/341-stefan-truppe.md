Stefan Truppe leads a research group at the Fritz Haber Institute of the Max Planck Society in Berlin, where he develops laser cooling and trapping of molecules — notably aluminium monofluoride (AlF), a promising candidate for deep laser cooling — and high-resolution molecular spectroscopy.

He earned his PhD at Imperial College London working on calcium monofluoride (CaF) and tests of fundamental constants, where he demonstrated sub-Doppler cooling of molecules, then joined the Fritz Haber Institute, working with Gerard Meijer's department.
