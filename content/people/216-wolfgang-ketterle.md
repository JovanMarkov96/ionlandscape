Wolfgang Ketterle shared the 2001 Nobel Prize in Physics for achieving Bose-Einstein condensation in dilute atomic gases and for early fundamental studies of the condensates' properties. At MIT he leads research on ultracold atoms and quantum simulation of many-body physics.
