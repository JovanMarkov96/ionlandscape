Tijs Karman is an Associate Professor of Physics at Radboud University Nijmegen and a theorist of ultracold molecular collisions. He developed the theory of microwave shielding of ultracold polar molecules — engineering repulsive shields with microwave fields to suppress lossy collisions — which enabled experiments to evaporatively cool dipolar molecules to quantum degeneracy and to a dipolar molecular Bose-Einstein condensate.

His work spans collision theory, sympathetic cooling and the engineering of interactions in dipolar molecular gases.
