Machiel Blok is Assistant Professor of Physics and Astronomy at the University of Rochester, where he leads the Blok Lab. He completed his PhD at Delft University of Technology under Ronald Hanson, working on quantum measurement and real-time feedback with NV center spin registers in diamond. Following a postdoc in Irfan Siddiqi's group at UC Berkeley and Lawrence Berkeley National Lab (2015–2019), he joined Rochester in 2020. His current research focuses on qudit quantum computing and quantum simulation with superconducting transmon circuits, pioneering the use of higher-dimensional (beyond qubit) encodings to improve quantum processor performance and error resilience.
