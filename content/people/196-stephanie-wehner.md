Stephanie Wehner is the Antoni van Leeuwenhoek Professor in Quantum Information at Delft University of Technology / QuTech, and Director of the European Quantum Internet Alliance (QIA). Her research spans quantum cryptography, quantum communication protocols, and the architectural foundations of a large-scale quantum internet.

She received her PhD from CWI Amsterdam in 2008 under Harry Buhrman, with a thesis on "Cryptography in a Quantum World." She then held a postdoctoral position at the Caltech Institute for Quantum Information (2008–2010) under John Preskill, followed by faculty positions at the National University of Singapore / Centre for Quantum Technologies (2010–2014) before joining TU Delft in 2014. She was awarded the Antoni van Leeuwenhoek professorship in late 2016.

Her most-cited work includes the quantum internet roadmap paper in Science (2018, co-authored with Elkouss and Hanson), the comprehensive review of Bell nonlocality in Reviews of Modern Physics (2014), and the first link-layer protocol for quantum networks (SIGCOMM 2019). She also co-developed NetSquid, a discrete-event quantum network simulator. She is a member of the Royal Dutch Academy of Arts and Sciences (KNAW) and a recipient of the Körber European Science Prize (2025).
