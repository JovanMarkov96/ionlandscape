Jian-Wei Pan is a physicist at the University of Science and Technology of China and a leader of photonic quantum information science. His group has produced record multiphoton entanglement, the Micius satellite for intercontinental quantum key distribution, the Jiuzhang boson-sampling photonic quantum computers demonstrating quantum computational advantage, and advances in quantum repeaters and cold-atom quantum simulation.
