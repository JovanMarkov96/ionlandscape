Michael Tarbutt is a Professor of Physics at Imperial College London and a leader of the Centre for Cold Matter. His group laser-cools, slows and magneto-optically traps calcium monofluoride (CaF) molecules, develops molecules as a platform for quantum computation and simulation, and uses laser-cooled ytterbium monofluoride (YbF) to measure the electron's electric dipole moment.

He carried out his PhD at Imperial College London in the group of Edward Hinds, with whom he has long collaborated on molecular precision measurements, and built one of the first molecular magneto-optical traps.
