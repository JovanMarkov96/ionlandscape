Monika Aidelsburger is a Professor of Physics at the Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich, where her group engineers artificial gauge fields and topological band structures for ultracold rubidium (Rb) and potassium (K) atoms in optical lattices using Floquet (periodic-driving) techniques, realizing the Hofstadter model and measuring topological invariants.

She earned her PhD with Immanuel Bloch, where she implemented strong effective magnetic fields and the Hofstadter Hamiltonian with neutral atoms.
