Kang-Kuen Ni is a Professor of Chemistry and Physics at Harvard University, where her group studies ultracold molecules for quantum information and controlled chemistry. Her lab assembled single molecules from individual atoms in optical tweezers (NaCs) and observed bimolecular reactions of ultracold KRb molecules at the state-to-state level.

During her PhD at JILA (University of Colorado Boulder) with Jun Ye and Deborah Jin, she helped create the first ultracold gas of ground-state polar KRb molecules.
