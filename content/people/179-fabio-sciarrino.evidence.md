## `key_papers`

All five papers confirmed as senior/corresponding-author works.

1. **"Integrated photonic quantum technologies"** (2020, Nature Photonics, vol. 14, pp. 273–284)
   - DOI: 10.1038/s41566-019-0532-1
   - Authors: Wang, Sciarrino, Laing, Thompson
   - *Source*: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41566-019-0532-1
   - ~1,981 citations (Google Scholar); top-cited review in integrated quantum photonics.

2. **"Integrated multimode interferometers with arbitrary designs for photonic boson sampling"** (2013, Nature Photonics, vol. 7, pp. 545–549)
   - DOI: 10.1038/nphoton.2013.112
   - Authors: Crespi, Osellame, Ramponi, Brod, Galvao, Spagnolo, Sciarrino et al.
   - *Source*: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphoton.2013.112
   - Landmark experimental boson sampling paper; ~953 citations.

3. **"Two-Particle Bosonic-Fermionic Quantum Walk via Integrated Photonics"** (2012, Physical Review Letters, vol. 108, 010502)
   - DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.108.010502
   - Authors: Sansoni, Sciarrino, Vallone, Mataloni, Crespi, Ramponi, Osellame
   - *Source*: https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.108.010502
   - First demonstration of bosonic/fermionic quantum walks in integrated photonics; ~783 citations.

4. **"Anderson localization of entangled photons in an integrated quantum walk"** (2013, Nature Photonics, vol. 7, pp. 322–328)
   - DOI: 10.1038/nphoton.2013.26
   - Authors: Crespi, Osellame, Ramponi, Giovannetti, Fazio, Sansoni, De Nicola, Sciarrino, Mataloni
   - *Source*: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphoton.2013.26
   - ~589 citations.

5. **"Integrated photonic quantum gates for polarization qubits"** (2011, Nature Communications, vol. 2, article 566)
   - DOI: 10.1038/ncomms1570
   - Authors: Crespi, Ramponi, Osellame, Sansoni, Bongioanni, Vallone, Sciarrino, Mataloni
   - *Source*: https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms1570
   - First integrated CNOT gate for polarization-encoded qubits; ~490 citations.
