Taeyoung Choi leads trapped-ion research at Ewha Womans University in Seoul, focusing on quantum computing and quantum control with ytterbium ions.

He received his PhD in Physics from The Ohio State University in 2011 under Jay Gupta, and subsequently conducted significant research in collaboration with Christopher Monroe in the field of trapped-ion quantum computing.
