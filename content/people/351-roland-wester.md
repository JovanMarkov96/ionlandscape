Roland Wester is a Professor of Physics at the University of Innsbruck, where his group studies the dynamics of cold molecular ions and anions in radiofrequency multipole ion traps, using velocity-map imaging to reveal the mechanisms of fundamental ion-molecule reactions such as nucleophilic substitution (SN2).

His work spans cold chemistry, the spectroscopy and reaction dynamics of molecular anions (including OH⁻), and laboratory astrochemistry relevant to interstellar molecular clouds.
