Waseem Bakr is a Professor of Physics at Princeton University, where his group uses quantum-gas microscopy to image and manipulate ultracold fermionic lithium (Li) and potassium (K) — and dipolar erbium (Er) — atom by atom in optical lattices and tweezers, probing the Fermi-Hubbard model and strongly correlated quantum matter.

He developed the first quantum gas microscope during his PhD at Harvard with Markus Greiner, a technique that resolves single atoms on individual lattice sites and now underpins much of quantum simulation with neutral atoms.
