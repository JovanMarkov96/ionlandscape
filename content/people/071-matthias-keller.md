Matthias Keller leads trapped-ion research at the University of Sussex focusing on ion-photon interfaces and cavity QED for quantum networks.

He received his PhD under Herbert Walther at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics in 2004, where he worked on single-photon generation with trapped calcium ions.
