Seigo Tarucha is a team leader at the RIKEN Center for Quantum Computing and Professor Emeritus at the University of Tokyo who pioneered the physics of few-electron semiconductor quantum dots (revealing atom-like shell structure) and now develops high-fidelity spin qubits in gallium-arsenide and silicon quantum dots, reaching gate fidelities above the fault-tolerance threshold.
