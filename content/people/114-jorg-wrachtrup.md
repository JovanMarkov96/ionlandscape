Jörg Wrachtrup is a physicist at the University of Stuttgart and a founder of single-spin quantum science with nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centres in diamond. His group develops NV-based quantum sensing and imaging, nanoscale NMR, and defect-based quantum information.
