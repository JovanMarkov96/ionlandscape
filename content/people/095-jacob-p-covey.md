Jacob Covey is an Assistant Professor of Physics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where his group builds optical-tweezer arrays of alkaline-earth(-like) atoms — ytterbium (Yb) and strontium (Sr) — for quantum computing, optical-clock metrology and atom-photon quantum networking.

He developed alkaline-earth tweezer-array techniques as a postdoctoral researcher at Caltech and previously created ultracold ground-state polar molecules during his PhD at JILA.
