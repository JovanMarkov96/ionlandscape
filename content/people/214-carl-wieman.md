Carl Wieman shared the 2001 Nobel Prize in Physics with Eric Cornell and Wolfgang Ketterle for creating the first Bose-Einstein condensate in a dilute gas of rubidium atoms (1995) at JILA. He is now a professor at Stanford, also renowned for his work on science education.
