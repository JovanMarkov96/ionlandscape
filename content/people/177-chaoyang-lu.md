Chao-Yang Lu is a physicist at the University of Science and Technology of China working on photonic quantum technologies. He develops high-efficiency solid-state (quantum-dot) single-photon sources, multiphoton entanglement, and the Jiuzhang boson-sampling machines that demonstrated quantum computational advantage with light.
