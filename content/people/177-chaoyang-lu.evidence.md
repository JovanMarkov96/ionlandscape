## Data quality notes

- **PhD advisor ambiguity**: Lu's Cambridge thesis was co-supervised by Mete Atatüre (Cavendish, quantum dot photonics) and Pan Jianwei (visiting). The USTC profile specifically names both. Pan is recorded as `advisor_id` because he is the quantum information/photonics lineage mentor and Lu's subsequent career was built in Pan's group at USTC. Atatüre was the resident Cambridge supervisor for quantum-dot physics.
- **Since_year 2011 vs. 2022**: Lu joined USTC as Professor of Physics in 2011 directly after his PhD. He was promoted to Chair Professor in 2022. `since_year: 2022` reflects the current rank (Chair Professor); his continuous USTC tenure dates to 2011.
- **Jiuzhang 4.0 (2026)**: Xinhua reported Jiuzhang 4.0 in May 2026 (3,050 photons), but no peer-reviewed DOI was available at time of writing. Not included in key_papers.
- **graduate student at USTC 2004–2007**: Profile page lists "Graduate student, Department of Modern Physics, USTC (2004–2007)" before Cambridge. This is a master's-level graduate enrollment; no separate MS degree listed in most sources, so it is omitted from education to avoid ambiguity.
