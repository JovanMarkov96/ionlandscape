Nathalie de Leon is a physicist at Princeton University working on solid-state qubits and their materials. Her group develops nitrogen-vacancy (NV) and group-IV (tin-vacancy, SnV) colour centres in diamond for quantum networks and sensing, and investigates the materials limits of superconducting-qubit coherence.
