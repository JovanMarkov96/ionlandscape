Thomas Monz is an experimental quantum physicist serving as a Senior Scientist at the University of Innsbruck and is the Co-founder and CEO of Alpine Quantum Technologies (AQT), an Austrian spin-out developing general-purpose trapped-ion quantum computers.

He completed his PhD at the University of Innsbruck (2011) under Rainer Blatt, focusing on creating and characterizing multi-qubit entangled states (e.g., 14-qubit GHZ states) and transitioned directly into a Senior Scientist role within the institution. 

His research spans trapped-ion quantum computing hardware, ion-photon interfaces, and system-level validation. He played a central role in the realization of key quantum algorithms on trapped-ion architectures, including the first scalable realization of Shor's algorithm, and is also engaged in research utilizing "qudits" to enhance computational capacity.
