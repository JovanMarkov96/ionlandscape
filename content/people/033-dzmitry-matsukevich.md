Dzmitry Matsukevich is a Principal Investigator at the Centre for Quantum Technologies (CQT) at the National University of Singapore. His group focuses on trapped ion quantum information processing, quantum thermodynamics, and utilizing trapped molecular ions (such as SiO+) for quantum networking and precision measurements.

He completed his PhD at the Georgia Institute of Technology (2006) under Alex Kuzmich, and subsequently conducted postdoctoral research in the group of Christopher Monroe at the University of Maryland (Joint Quantum Institute) from 2006 to 2010, where he made significant contributions to ion-photon entanglement and quantum networks.
