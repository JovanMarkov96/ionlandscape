Nikolai Kolachevsky is an experimental physicist and Director of the P. N. Lebedev Physical Institute (FIAN) of the Russian Academy of Sciences in Moscow. He co-leads the joint FIAN–Russian Quantum Center trapped-ion programme that built Russia's ytterbium-171 (171Yb+) qudit-based quantum computer, and has a long record in optical atomic clocks and precision spectroscopy.
