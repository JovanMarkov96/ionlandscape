Selim Jochim is a Professor of Physics at Heidelberg University, where his group builds strongly interacting Fermi gases of lithium (Li) atom by atom, deterministically preparing few-fermion systems to study how many-body behaviour emerges from individual particles.

He earned his PhD at the University of Innsbruck with Rudolf Grimm, where he was a lead author on the first Bose-Einstein condensate of molecules formed from a Fermi gas.
