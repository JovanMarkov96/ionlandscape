Barbara M. Terhal is a Professor at QuTech, Delft University of Technology (Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science), where she leads the Terhal Group focused on quantum error correction and fault-tolerant quantum computation theory.

She received her PhD cum laude from the University of Amsterdam in 1999 under Paul Vitányi, becoming the first person to receive a PhD in quantum computing in the Netherlands. Her thesis introduced the concept of the entanglement witness. After postdoctoral work at IBM Watson Research Center and Caltech, she joined IBM Research as a staff member (2001–2010). She then held a full professorship at RWTH Aachen University (2010–2017) with a concurrent position at Forschungszentrum Jülich (2015–2022), before moving to TU Delft / QuTech in September 2017.

Her research focuses on quantum error correction for superconducting devices, including surface codes, bosonic/GKP codes, and fault-tolerant architectures. She is a Fellow of the American Physical Society (since 2007) and a member of the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences (2020).
