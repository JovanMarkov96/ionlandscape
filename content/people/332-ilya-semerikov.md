Ilya Semerikov is an experimental physicist at the P. N. Lebedev Physical Institute (FIAN) and the Russian Quantum Center in Moscow, and a laureate of the VYZOV national prize. He led the development of Russia's ion-based quantum computer, which uses ytterbium-171 (171Yb+) ions in a linear trap with qudit (ququart) encoding and was reported at the 50-qubit scale in 2024.
