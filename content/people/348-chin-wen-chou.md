Chin-Wen (James) Chou is a staff scientist in the Ion Storage Group at NIST Boulder, where he applies quantum-logic techniques developed for trapped-ion clocks to control single molecular ions. His group prepared and coherently manipulated pure quantum states of a single calcium hydride ion (CaH+), enabling quantum-logic spectroscopy of molecular ions.

He earned his PhD at Caltech in 2006 with H. Jeff Kimble, working on quantum networks with atomic ensembles, then joined NIST, where he contributed to record-accuracy aluminium-ion (Al+) optical clocks and tests of relativity.
