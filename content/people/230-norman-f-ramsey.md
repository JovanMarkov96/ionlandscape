Norman F. Ramsey shared the 1989 Nobel Prize in Physics for the separated oscillatory fields method (Ramsey interferometry) and the invention of the hydrogen maser. His techniques are the basis of atomic clocks and precision spectroscopy. A Harvard professor, he died in 2011.
