Moonjoo Lee leads the trapped-ion quantum computing group at POSTECH (Pohang University of Science and Technology). His research focuses on quantum simulation, quantum computing, and cavity QED with ytterbium ions. 

He received his PhD from Seoul National University (2011) working with Kyungwon An on single-atom cavity QED. After his doctorate, he completed extensive postdoctoral training spanning neutral atoms, trapped ions, and superconducting circuits at ETH Zürich (with Tilman Esslinger), the University of Innsbruck (with Tracy Northup), and CEA-Saclay (Quantronics group). He joined POSTECH as a faculty member in 2020.
