## key_papers

### 10.1103/PhysRevLett.94.240503
- **Title**: Unconditional Quantum Cloning of Coherent States with Linear Optics
- **Authors**: Ulrik L. Andersen, Vincent Josse, Gerd Leuchs
- **Journal**: Physical Review Letters, 94, 240503 (2005)
- **Role**: Andersen is first author
- **Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0501005 and https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.94.240503
- **Significance**: Proposed and experimentally demonstrated optimal Gaussian cloning of optical coherent states using only linear optics and homodyne detection, achieving fidelity ~65% (approaching optimal 2/3). Published during Erlangen postdoc.

### 10.1038/nphoton.2010.197
- **Title**: A generator for unique quantum random numbers based on vacuum states
- **Authors**: C. Gabriel, C. Wittmann, D. Sych, R. Dong, W. Mauerer, U. L. Andersen, C. Marquardt, G. Leuchs
- **Journal**: Nature Photonics, 4(10), 711–715 (2010)
- **Role**: Andersen is co-author
- **Source**: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphoton.2010.197
- **Significance**: Demonstrated a QRNG using homodyne measurement of vacuum fluctuations — the principle underlying the Alea Quantum Technologies spin-out. 564 citations (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30).

### 10.1038/nphys3410
- **Title**: Hybrid discrete- and continuous-variable quantum information
- **Authors**: U. L. Andersen, J. S. Neergaard-Nielsen, P. van Loock, A. Furusawa
- **Journal**: Nature Physics, 11(9), 713–719 (2015)
- **Role**: Andersen is first/corresponding author
- **Source**: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys3410
- **Significance**: Review and perspective article establishing the conceptual framework for hybrid CV–DV quantum protocols; 554 citations (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30). Identified as a signature paper in Andersen's research profile.

### 10.1126/science.aay4354
- **Title**: Deterministic generation of a two-dimensional cluster state
- **Authors**: Mikkel V. Larsen, Xueshi Guo, Casper R. Breum, Jonas S. Neergaard-Nielsen, Ulrik L. Andersen
- **Journal**: Science, 366(6463), 369–372 (2019)
- **Role**: Andersen is senior/corresponding author
- **Source**: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aay4354
- **Significance**: First deterministic generation of a 2D photonic cluster state (>30,000 entangled modes in a cylindrical lattice) using time-multiplexed squeezed light — a scalable resource for measurement-based CV quantum computing. 578 citations (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30).

### 10.1364/AOP.361502
- **Title**: Advances in quantum cryptography
- **Authors**: S. Pirandola, U. L. Andersen, L. Banchi, et al. (18 authors)
- **Journal**: Advances in Optics and Photonics, 12(4), 1012–1236 (2020)
- **Role**: Andersen is co-author (second author)
- **Source**: https://opg.optica.org/aop/abstract.cfm?uri=aop-12-4-1012
- **Significance**: Comprehensive review of quantum cryptography (QKD, CV-QKD, device-independence); 2,861 citations (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30) — Andersen's highest-cited paper.

### 10.1126/science.adv2560
- **Title**: Quantum learning advantage on a scalable photonic platform
- **Authors**: Zhenghao Liu, Romain Brunel, ..., Ulrik L. Andersen (corresponding)
- **Journal**: Science, 389, 1332–(2025)
- **Role**: Andersen is senior/corresponding author
- **Source**: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adv2560
- **Significance**: First proven quantum advantage for a photonic system: EPR-entangled light achieves 11.8-orders-of-magnitude sample-complexity reduction in a quantum learning task. Published September 2025.
