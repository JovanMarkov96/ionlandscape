Murray D. Barrett is a Principal Investigator at the Centre for Quantum Technologies (CQT) at the National University of Singapore, where he leads the Microtraps Group. His research focuses on developing microfabricated ion traps for scalable quantum computing and quantum networking with barium and lutetium ions.

He received his PhD in Physics from Georgia Institute of Technology (2002) under Michael S. Chapman, studying all-optical approaches to Bose-Einstein condensation. He subsequently conducted postdoctoral research at NIST Boulder in the Ion Storage Group of David J. Wineland, where he was lead author on the landmark 2004 Nature paper demonstrating the first deterministic quantum teleportation of atomic qubits.
