Hendrick Bethlem is an Associate Professor of Physics at VU Amsterdam, where his group develops decelerated and trapped cold-molecule beams for precision tests of fundamental physics, including searches for a possible variation of the proton-to-electron mass ratio using methanol spectroscopy and a molecular fountain.

With Gerard Meijer, during his PhD at the University of Nijmegen, he invented the Stark decelerator and demonstrated the first electrostatic trapping of neutral polar molecules (ammonia).
