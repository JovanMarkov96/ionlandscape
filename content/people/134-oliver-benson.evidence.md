## Key papers evidence

*   **`key_papers[0]`**: "A single-photon turnstile device" — Kim, Benson, Kan, Yamamoto, Nature 397, 500-503 (1999)
    *   DOI: 10.1038/17295
    *   Role: co_author (Benson is second author)
    *   Landmark single-photon device paper from Stanford postdoc period.

*   **`key_papers[1]`**: "Regulated and entangled photons from a single quantum dot" — Benson, Santori, Pelton, Yamamoto, PRL 84, 2513-2516 (2000)
    *   DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.84.2513
    *   Role: first_author (1,353 citations per Google Scholar)
    *   Foundational quantum dot entangled photon paper.

*   **`key_papers[2]`**: "Enhancement of the zero phonon line emission from a single nitrogen vacancy center in a nanodiamond via coupling to a photonic crystal cavity" — Wolters et al. incl. Benson, APL 97, 141108 (2010)
    *   DOI: 10.1063/1.3499300
    *   Role: senior_author (Benson last author)
    *   First NV-photonic crystal cavity coupling paper from Berlin group; Purcell factor 12.1.

*   **`key_papers[3]`**: "Ultrabright and efficient single-photon generation based on nitrogen-vacancy centres in nanodiamonds on a solid immersion lens" — Schröder, Gädeke, Banholzer, Benson, NJP 13, 055017 (2011)
    *   DOI: 10.1088/1367-2630/13/5/055017
    *   Role: senior_author (Benson last/corresponding author; all Berlin-affiliated)
    *   Source: IOP Science article page.

*   **`key_papers[4]`**: "Hybrid integrated quantum photonic circuits" — Elshaari, Pernice, Srinivasan, Benson, Zwiller, Nature Photonics 14, 285-298 (2020)
    *   DOI: 10.1038/s41566-020-0609-x
    *   Role: co_author (935 citations per Google Scholar)
    *   Major review on integrated quantum photonics; widely cited.
