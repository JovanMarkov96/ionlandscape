David M. Lucas is the Hooke Professor of Experimental Physics at the University of Oxford, where he co-leads the Oxford Ion Trap Quantum Computing Group. The Oxford group is one of the world's leading centers for trapped-ion quantum computing, working with calcium and strontium ions on quantum error correction, high-fidelity quantum gates, and optical clocks.

Under his leadership, the group has set numerous world records for trapped-ion quantum operations, including the first demonstration of 99.9% fidelity two-qubit logic gates in 2016. He received his DPhil from the University of Oxford, studying parity violation in atomic samarium.
