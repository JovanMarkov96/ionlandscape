## Key Papers
1. **10.1126/science.aam5538** — "A Fermi-degenerate three-dimensional optical lattice clock", Science 358(6359):90–94, 2017.
   - Demonstrates 3D optical lattice clock with degenerate Fermi gas of Sr-87; precision 5×10⁻¹⁹ in 1 hour.
   - Source: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aam5538

2. **10.1126/science.aau7230** — "A degenerate Fermi gas of polar molecules", Science, Feb 2019.
   - First Fermi degenerate gas of ultracold polar KRb molecules; temperatures below 0.3 T_F.
   - Source: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aau7230

3. **10.1088/1681-7575/ab4089** — "JILA SrI optical lattice clock with uncertainty of 2.0×10⁻¹⁸", Metrologia 56(6):065004, 2019.
   - State-of-the-art systematic uncertainty evaluation; >20-fold improvement over 2013 evaluation.
   - Source: https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1681-7575/ab4089

4. **10.1038/s41586-023-06360-6** — "Realizing spin squeezing with Rydberg interactions in an optical clock", Nature 621:734–739, 2023.
   - First Rydberg-mediated spin squeezing in neutral-atom optical clock; ~4 dB metrological gain.
   - Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06360-6

5. **10.1126/science.ado5987** — "Coherent evolution of superexchange interaction in seconds-long optical clock spectroscopy", Science, 2024.
   - Direct observation of coherent superexchange in 3D optical lattice clock; groundwork for quantum magnetism.
   - Source: https://www.science.org/doi/abs/10.1126/science.ado5987
