Dr. Ran Finkelstein is an experimental physicist and Senior Lecturer in the School of Physics and Astronomy at Tel Aviv University, where he leads a laboratory on quantum information with atoms and photons. His research spans quantum computation, quantum metrology, and quantum simulation with arrays of ultracold neutral atoms held in optical tweezers, as well as quantum optics — the generation, storage, and interaction of single photons with atomic ensembles.

He earned his BSc and MSc at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem and his PhD at the Weizmann Institute of Science (2021), where he worked on Rydberg quantum optics in the group of Ofer Firstenberg. He then held a Troesh Postdoctoral Prize Fellowship at the California Institute of Technology (2021–2024) in the neutral-atom array group of Manuel Endres, working on arrays of alkaline-earth atoms whose long-lived metastable states enable high-fidelity control, scalability, and site-resolved readout. He joined the Tel Aviv University faculty in 2024.
