Wesley C. Campbell leads the Campbell Lab at UCLA, focusing on trapped-ion quantum simulation and quantum computing with ytterbium ions. His research also includes precision measurement and optical atomic clocks.

He received his PhD from Harvard University in 2008 working under John M. Doyle on cold molecules. He then completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Maryland (JQI) under Christopher Monroe from 2008 to 2011 before joining the faculty at UCLA.
