Chen Wang is an Associate Professor of Physics at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, where his group develops bosonic superconducting qubits — encoding error-correctable logical qubits in the states of high-quality three-dimensional microwave cavities controlled by transmon ancillas.

As a postdoctoral researcher at Yale University with Robert Schoelkopf, he created an entangled 'Schrödinger cat' state shared between two separate microwave cavities, a building block for hardware-efficient quantum error correction with cat codes.
