Christiane Koch is a Professor of Physics at Freie Universität Berlin and a leading theorist of quantum control. Her work develops optimal-control methods for steering molecular dynamics — including the control of molecular rotation, the formation and stabilization of ultracold molecules, and control of open quantum systems — with broad applications across quantum technologies.

She has shaped the European strategy for quantum optimal control and collaborates closely with cold-molecule and quantum-information experiments.
