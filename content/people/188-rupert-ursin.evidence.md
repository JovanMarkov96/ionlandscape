## key_papers

### 10.1038/430849a — Quantum teleportation across the Danube (Nature 2004)
- **authors**: Rupert Ursin, Thomas Jennewein, Markus Aspelmeyer, Rainer Kaltenbaek, Michael Lindenthal, Philip Walther, Anton Zeilinger
- **journal**: Nature 430, 849 (2004)
- **role**: first_author
- **confirmed via**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15318210/ and https://www.nature.com/articles/430849a
- **note**: First free-space quantum teleportation outside the lab — 600 m across the Danube river, Vienna.

### 10.1038/nphys629 — Entanglement-based quantum communication over 144 km (Nature Physics 2007)
- **authors**: R. Ursin, F. Tiefenbacher, T. Schmitt-Manderbach et al. (18 authors including Zeilinger)
- **journal**: Nature Physics 3, 481–486 (2007)
- **role**: first_author
- **confirmed via**: Google Scholar lookup "Nat. Phys. 3, 481–486 (2007)" and https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0607182
- **note**: Landmark demonstration of entanglement-based QKD between La Palma and Tenerife (Canary Islands, ESA Optical Ground Station). ~1,770 citations on Google Scholar.

### 10.1103/PhysRevLett.98.010504 — Experimental demonstration of free-space decoy-state QKD over 144 km (PRL 2007)
- **authors**: T. Schmitt-Manderbach, H. Weier, M. Fürst, R. Ursin et al. (including Zeilinger, Weinfurter)
- **journal**: Physical Review Letters 98, 010504 (2007)
- **role**: co_author
- **confirmed via**: https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.98.010504
- **note**: Companion paper to nphys629 using weak coherent pulses / decoy states rather than entanglement. ~1,118 citations.

### 10.1038/nature11472 — Quantum teleportation over 143 kilometres using active feed-forward (Nature 2012)
- **authors**: Xiao-Song Ma, Thomas Herbst, Thomas Scheidl, Daqing Wang, Sebastian Kropatschek, William Naylor, Bernhard Wittmann, Alexandra Mech, Johannes Kofler, Elena Anisimova, Vadim Makarov, Thomas Jennewein, Rupert Ursin & Anton Zeilinger

- **confirmed via**: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature11472 and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22951967/
- **note**: World-record free-space quantum teleportation at the time. ~958 citations.

### 10.1103/PhysRevLett.115.250401 — Significant-loophole-free test of Bell's theorem with entangled photons (PRL 2015)
- **authors**: Giustina, Versteegh, Wengerowsky, Handsteiner, Hochrainer, Phelan, Steinlechner, Kofler, Larsson, Abellan, Amaya, Pruneri, Mitchell, Beyer, Gerrits, Lita, Shalm, Nam, Scheidl, Ursin, Wittmann, Zeilinger

### 10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.030501 — Satellite-relayed intercontinental quantum network (PRL 2018)
