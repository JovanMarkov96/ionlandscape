David Allcock is an Assistant Professor of Physics at the University of Oregon, where his group develops trapped-ion quantum computing with calcium ions (40Ca+), focusing on microfabricated surface-electrode ion traps, laser-free microwave control of qubits, and integrated photonics and electronics for scalable processors.

He earned his PhD at the University of Oxford with David Lucas and was a postdoctoral researcher in the Ion Storage Group at NIST Boulder with David J. Wineland.
