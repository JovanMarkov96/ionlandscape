John Preskill is a theoretical physicist at Caltech and director of the Institute for Quantum Information and Matter. He coined the term "quantum supremacy" and the NISQ (Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum) era, and is a leading voice on quantum error correction, fault tolerance, and the theory of quantum information.
