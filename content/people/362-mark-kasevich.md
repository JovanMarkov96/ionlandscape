Mark Kasevich is a Professor of Physics at Stanford University and a pioneer of light-pulse atom interferometry, using freely falling cesium (Cs) and rubidium (Rb) atoms as ultra-precise inertial and gravitational sensors. His group develops atomic gravimeters, gyroscopes and gradiometers, very-long-baseline atom interferometers for tests of gravity and searches for dark matter and gravitational waves, and spin-squeezing for quantum-enhanced sensing.

He earned his PhD at Stanford with Steven Chu, where he developed atom interferometry based on stimulated Raman transitions.
