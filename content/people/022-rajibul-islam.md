K. Rajibul Islam is an Associate Professor at the Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC) and the Department of Physics and Astronomy at the University of Waterloo. His research focuses on experimental quantum many-body physics, particularly the quantum simulation of frustrated spin models with trapped ions. He leads the Laboratory for Quantum Information with Trapped Ions (QITI), which is developing "QuantumIon", a multi-user, open-access trapped-ion quantum computer. He also co-founded Open Quantum Design, a non-profit foundation developing open-source, full-stack trapped-ion quantum computing blueprints.

He received his PhD from the University of Maryland (2013) under the supervision of Christopher Monroe, where his thesis on quantum simulation won the university's Distinguished Dissertation Award. He then transitioned to neutral atoms and hybrid systems as a postdoctoral fellow in Markus Greiner's group at Harvard University (2012–2015), where he helped pioneer the direct measurement of entanglement entropy in many-body systems. He followed this with a postdoctoral stint in Vladan Vuletić's group at MIT (2015–2016) before joining the faculty at Waterloo.
