### Key papers

1. **Cooperative Atom-Light Interaction in a Blockaded Rydberg Ensemble** (2010)
   - DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.193603
   - Journal: Physical Review Letters 105, 193603
   - Role: **first_author** (J. D. Pritchard, D. Maxwell, A. Gauguet, K. J. Weatherill, M. P. A. Jones, C. S. Adams)
   - Citations: ~641 (Google Scholar)
   - Significance: Pioneering demonstration of cooperative optical nonlinearity via Rydberg EIT; Pritchard's defining PhD-era result

2. **Storage and Control of Optical Photons Using Rydberg Polaritons** (2013)
   - DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.103001
   - Journal: Physical Review Letters 110, 103001
   - Role: **co_author** (Maxwell, Szwer, Paredes-Barato, Busche, Pritchard, Gauguet, Weatherill, Jones, Adams)
   - Citations: ~378 (Google Scholar)
   - Significance: Microwave control of photon–photon interactions via Rydberg polaritons

3. **ARC: An open-source library for calculating properties of alkali Rydberg atoms** (2017)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.cpc.2017.06.015
   - Journal: Computer Physics Communications 220, 319–331
   - Role: **co_author** (Sibalic, Pritchard, Adams, Weatherill)
   - Citations: ~644 (Google Scholar); highest-cited paper
   - Significance: Widely-used open-source toolkit for the Rydberg community

4. **Rydberg atom quantum technologies** (2020)
   - DOI: 10.1088/1361-6455/ab52ef
   - Journal: Journal of Physics B 53(1), 012002
   - Role: **co_author** (Adams, Pritchard, Shaffer)
   - Citations: ~584 (Google Scholar)
   - Significance: Topical review covering the landscape of Rydberg quantum technologies

5. **Demonstration of a quantum gate using electromagnetically induced transparency** (2022)
   - DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.129.200501
   - Journal: Physical Review Letters 129, 200501
   - Role: **senior_author** (McDonnell, Keary, Pritchard)
   - Significance: First demonstration of a two-qubit gate in Pritchard's Strathclyde group
