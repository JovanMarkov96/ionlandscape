## Key Papers

### 1. Dipole Blockade and Quantum Information Processing (2001)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.87.037901
- **Journal:** Physical Review Letters 87, 037901
- **Authors:** M. D. Lukin, M. Fleischhauer, R. Cote, L. M. Duan, D. Jaksch, J. I. Cirac, P. Zoller
- **Role:** co_author (Lukin is first of lead authors; Jaksch/Cirac/Zoller also senior)
- **Significance:** Seminal paper proposing the Rydberg dipole blockade mechanism for scalable quantum gates
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11461592/

### 2. Long-distance quantum communication with atomic ensembles and linear optics (2001)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/35106500
- **Journal:** Nature 414, 413–418
- **Authors:** L.-M. Duan, M. D. Lukin, J. I. Cirac, P. Zoller (DLCZ protocol)
- **Role:** co_author
- **Significance:** Foundational DLCZ quantum repeater protocol; one of the most-cited quantum communication papers
- Source: https://ffden-2.phys.uaf.edu/103_fall2003.web.dir/ben_steiner/duan%20et%20al.%202001.pdf

### 3. Nanoscale magnetic sensing with an individual electronic spin in diamond (2008)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature07279
- **Journal:** Nature 455, 644–647
- **Authors:** J. R. Maze, ..., M. D. Lukin (senior author)
- **Role:** senior_author
- **Significance:** Demonstrated single NV-center magnetometry at nanoscale resolution; launched quantum sensing with NV centers
- Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature07279

### 4. Probing many-body dynamics on a 51-atom quantum simulator (2017)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature24622
- **Journal:** Nature 551, 579–584
- **Authors:** H. Bernien, S. Schwartz, ..., M. D. Lukin (senior author)
- **Role:** senior_author
- **Significance:** Landmark demonstration of programmable 51-qubit Rydberg array quantum simulator
- Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature24622

### 5. Logical quantum processor based on reconfigurable atom arrays (2023)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-023-06927-3
- **Journal:** Nature (2023)
- **Authors:** D. Bluvstein, S. Evered, ..., M. D. Lukin (senior author)
- **Role:** senior_author
- **Significance:** First logical quantum processor with transversal gates on 448-atom platform; below-threshold error correction
- Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10830422/
