Gerard Meijer is a Director at the Fritz Haber Institute of the Max Planck Society and a pioneer of the manipulation of cold molecules. With Hendrick Bethlem he invented the Stark decelerator, using time-varying electric fields to slow and trap polar molecular beams, opening the field of controlled cold-molecule physics and chemistry.

He earned his PhD at the University of Nijmegen (now Radboud University) and has led molecular-physics research programs in the Netherlands and Germany, including current work on laser cooling of aluminium monofluoride (AlF) molecules.
