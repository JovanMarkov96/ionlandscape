## Career History

- 1990–1994: Research Associate, Freie Universität Berlin; PhD 1994
- 1994–2000: Research group, TU Chemnitz (under von Borczyskowski); habilitation 1998
- 2000–present: Professor of Physics and Director, 3rd Physical Institute, University of Stuttgart
- 2010–present: Max Planck Fellow, Max Planck Institute for Solid State Research, Stuttgart

**Postdoc lineage:** Fedor Jelezko (113-fedor-jelezko) was a postdoc in Wrachtrup's group at TU Chemnitz 1998–2000, then moved to Stuttgart with him through ~2011.
