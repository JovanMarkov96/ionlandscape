Johannes Hecker Denschlag is a Professor of Physics and Head of the Institute of Quantum Matter at Ulm University. His group investigates ultracold quantum gases and pioneering hybrid systems of trapped ions immersed in neutral atom clouds, focusing on cold ion-atom collisions and quantum chemistry.

He received his PhD from the University of Innsbruck (1998) under Anton Zeilinger and Jörg Schmiedmayer. He then conducted postdoctoral research at NIST Gaithersburg as a Feodor-Lynen fellow with Nobel laureate William D. Phillips, before returning to Innsbruck and eventually joining Ulm University in 2003.
