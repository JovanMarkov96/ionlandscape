Alexandre Blais is Full Professor (Professeur titulaire) of Physics at Université de Sherbrooke and Scientific Director of the Institut quantique. He is the co-inventor of circuit quantum electrodynamics (circuit QED), the theoretical architecture — coupling superconducting qubits to microwave resonators — that underpins superconducting quantum processors at Google, IBM, and Amazon. He obtained his PhD at Sherbrooke (2002), did a postdoc with Girvin and Schoelkopf at Yale (2003–2005), then returned to Sherbrooke as faculty. He is a CIFAR Fellow (Quantum Information Science), Fellow of the American Physical Society, Member of the Royal Society of Canada, and has received the NSERC Steacie Fellowship (2010), Herzberg Medal (2011), Brockhouse Medal (2020), Rutherford Memorial Medal (2018), and Guggenheim Fellowship (2021).
