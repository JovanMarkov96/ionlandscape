## `key_papers`

### 1. "Electrically driven quantum dot-micropillar single photon source with 34% overall efficiency" (2010)
- DOI: 10.1063/1.3284514
- Journal: Applied Physics Letters 96, 011107 (4 January 2010)
- Authors: T. Heindel, C. Schneider, M. Lermer, S. H. Kwon, T. Braun, S. Reitzenstein, S. Höfling, M. Kamp, A. Forchel
- Role: `first_author`
- *Source*: https://pubs.aip.org/aip/apl/article-abstract/96/1/011107/118931
- *Significance*: Demonstrated triggered single-photon emission from a low-mode-volume electrically driven QD-micropillar cavity at repetition rates up to 220 MHz with 34% overall efficiency — a benchmark result for electrically pumped QD sources. ~258 citations (Google Scholar).

### 2. "Quantum key distribution using quantum dot single-photon emitting diodes in the red and near infrared spectral range" (2012)
- DOI: 10.1088/1367-2630/14/8/083001
- Journal: New Journal of Physics 14, 083001 (August 2012)
- Authors: T. Heindel, C. A. Kessler, M. Rau, C. Schneider, M. Fürst, F. Hargart, W.-M. Schulz, M. Eichfelder, R. Roßbach, S. Nauerth, M. Lermer, H. Weier, M. Jetter, M. Kamp, S. Reitzenstein, S. Höfling, P. Michler, H. Weinfurter, A. Forchel
- Role: `first_author`
- *Source*: https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1367-2630/14/8/083001
- *Significance*: First demonstration of QKD using electrically driven semiconductor QD single-photon sources, achieving sifted key rates of 27.2–95.0 kbit/s. A landmark for practical quantum cryptography with solid-state sources.

### 3. "Highly indistinguishable photons from deterministic quantum-dot microlenses utilizing three-dimensional in situ electron-beam lithography" (2015)
- DOI: 10.1038/ncomms8662
- Journal: Nature Communications 6, 7662 (2015)
- Authors: M. Gschrey, A. Thoma, P. Schnauber, M. Seifried, R. Schmidt, B. Wohlfeil, L. Krüger, J.-H. Schulze, T. Heindel, S. Burger, F. Schmidt, A. Strittmatter, S. Rodt, S. Reitzenstein
- Role: `co_author` (Heindel is a mid-list author contributing to quantum optics characterisation)
- *Source*: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4518279/
- *Significance*: Showed QD microlenses fabricated by deterministic 3D in situ EBL achieve photon indistinguishability up to (80±7)% — highest value for non-cavity-coupled QD sources at the time. ~381 citations (Google Scholar, highest-cited paper on his profile).

### 4. "Enhancing quantum cryptography with quantum dot single-photon sources" (2022)
- DOI: 10.1038/s41534-022-00626-z
- Journal: npj Quantum Information 8, 104 (2022)
- Authors: M. Bozzio, M. Vyvlecka, M. Cosacchi, C. Javerzac-Galy, T. Heindel, et al.
- Role: `co_author` (Heindel is a corresponding/senior contributor)
- *Source*: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-022-00626-z
- *Significance*: Showed that phonon-assisted and two-photon excitation of QD sources provides tunable photon-number coherence that enhances security margins for QKD and other quantum-cryptographic primitives beyond what Poissonian (laser) sources can achieve.

### 5. "Quantum dots for photonic quantum information technology" (2023)
- DOI: 10.1364/AOP.490091
- Journal: Advances in Optics and Photonics 15(3), 613–738 (2023)
- Authors: T. Heindel, J.-H. Kim, N. Gregersen, A. Rastelli, S. Reitzenstein
- Role: `first_author`
- *Source*: https://opg.optica.org/aop/abstract.cfm?uri=aop-15-3-613
- *Significance*: Comprehensive 126-page review covering epitaxial growth, deterministic nanofabrication, quantum light source device concepts, and photonic circuit integration with QDs; immediately became the field's primary reference. ~210 citations within two years of publication.
