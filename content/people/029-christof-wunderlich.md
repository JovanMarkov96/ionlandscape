Christof Wunderlich leads the Quantum Optics Research Group at the University of Siegen. His group is known for pioneering work on microwave-driven quantum logic with trapped ytterbium ions and magnetic gradient traps (MAGIC) for scalable quantum information processing. He is also a co-founder of the quantum computing startup eleQtron.

He received his PhD from LMU Munich in 1996 under Theodor W. Hänsch.
