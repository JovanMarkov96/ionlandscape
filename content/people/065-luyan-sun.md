Luyan Sun leads quantum information research at Tsinghua University focusing on hybrid quantum systems and trapped-ion quantum computing.

He received his PhD from the University of Maryland in 2008. From 2009 to 2013, he worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Yale University with Robert Schoelkopf and Michel Devoret, focusing on quantum computation utilizing superconducting circuits.
