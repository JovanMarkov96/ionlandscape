Markus Hennrich leads the Trapped Rydberg Ion Group at Stockholm University, pioneering work on Rydberg excitation of trapped ions (such as strontium) for fast quantum computing operations and quantum simulation.

He completed his PhD at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics (MPQ) and TU Munich in 2004, working on the generation of single photons in atom-cavity systems. He subsequently joined Rainer Blatt's group at the University of Innsbruck / IQOQI as a postdoctoral researcher, contributing significantly to trapped-ion quantum information, before starting his own group in Sweden.
