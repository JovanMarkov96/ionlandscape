Jonathan Pritchard is a Professor of Physics at the University of Strathclyde, where his group works on Rydberg atoms in cesium (Cs) and rubidium (Rb) for quantum computing, quantum optics and microwave-to-optical conversion, using optical-tweezer arrays and atomic ensembles.

His research includes Rydberg-blockade gates for neutral-atom quantum processors and Rydberg-atom electrometry for microwave sensing.
