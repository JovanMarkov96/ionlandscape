Monika Schleier-Smith is an Associate Professor of Physics at Stanford University, where her group engineers programmable interactions among rubidium (Rb) atoms coupled to optical cavities, using cavity quantum electrodynamics to generate spin-squeezed and entangled states for quantum metrology and to build tunable-range quantum simulators.

She earned her PhD at MIT with Vladan Vuletić, pioneering cavity-based spin squeezing of atomic ensembles.
