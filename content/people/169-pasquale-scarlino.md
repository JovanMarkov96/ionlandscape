Pasquale Scarlino is an experimental physicist at EPFL working on hybrid circuit QED. His group couples superconducting microwave resonators to semiconductor quantum-dot spin and Andreev qubits, using superconducting circuits as a quantum bus to mediate long-range interactions between disparate qubit platforms.
