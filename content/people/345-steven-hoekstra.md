Steven Hoekstra is a Professor of Physics at the University of Groningen, where he develops decelerated and slow beams of heavy polar molecules — strontium monofluoride (SrF) and barium monofluoride (BaF) — for precision tests of fundamental symmetries.

He is a leader of the NL-eEDM collaboration, which uses cold BaF molecules to search for the electron's electric dipole moment, and works toward measurements of molecular parity violation with species such as radium monofluoride (RaF).
