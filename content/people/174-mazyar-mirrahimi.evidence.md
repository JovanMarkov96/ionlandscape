## Key Papers

1. **Dynamically protected cat-qubits: a new paradigm for universal quantum computation**
   - Authors: Mirrahimi, Leghtas, Albert, et al.
   - Journal: New Journal of Physics 16, 045014 (2014)
   - DOI: 10.1088/1367-2630/16/4/045014
   - arXiv: 1312.2017
   - Significance: Foundational cat-qubit theory paper; proposed encoding in Schrödinger cat states with multi-photon driven dissipation

2. **Confining the state of light to a quantum manifold by engineered two-photon loss**
   - Authors: Leghtas, Touzard, Pop, Kou, Vlastakis, Petrenko, Sliwa, Narla, Shankar, Hatridge, Reagor, Frunzio, Schoelkopf, Mirrahimi, Devoret
   - Journal: Science 347(6224), 853–857 (2015)
   - DOI: 10.1126/science.aaa2085
   - arXiv: 1412.4633
   - Significance: Experimental demonstration of two-photon driven-dissipative confinement

3. **Hardware-Efficient Autonomous Quantum Memory Protection**
   - Authors: Leghtas, Kirchmair, Vlastakis, Schoelkopf, Devoret, Mirrahimi
   - Journal: Physical Review Letters 111, 120501 (2013)
   - DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.111.120501
   - Significance: Proposed cat-code encoding for autonomous quantum error correction

4. **Repetition Cat Qubits for Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computation**
   - Authors: Guillaud, Mirrahimi
   - Journal: Physical Review X 9, 041053 (2019)
   - DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevX.9.041053
   - arXiv: 1904.09474
   - Significance: Hardware-efficient fault-tolerant computation using repetition code on cat qubits

5. **Real-time quantum feedback prepares and stabilizes photon number states**
   - Authors: Sayrin, Dotsenko, Zhou, Peaudecerf, Rybarczyk, Gleyzes, Rouchon, Mirrahimi, Amini, Brune, Raimond, Haroche
   - Journal: Nature 477, 73–77 (2011)
   - DOI: 10.1038/nature10376
   - arXiv: 1107.4027
   - Significance: First real-time quantum feedback experiment stabilizing Fock states; group includes Nobel laureate S. Haroche
