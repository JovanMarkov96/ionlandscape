Kater Murch is a Professor of Physics at Washington University in St. Louis, where his group studies the quantum measurement and control of superconducting qubits. His experiments tracked the individual quantum trajectories of a continuously monitored transmon and stabilized its dynamics with real-time feedback, and his group explores non-Hermitian physics and exceptional points in driven dissipative quantum circuits.

He earned his PhD at the University of California, Berkeley with Dan Stamper-Kurn and was a postdoctoral researcher with Irfan Siddiqi.
