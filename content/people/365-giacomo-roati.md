Giacomo Roati is a CNR-INO senior researcher at the LENS laboratory of the University of Florence, where his group studies ultracold fermionic lithium (Li) and potassium (K) gases as quantum simulators of superfluidity, transport and disorder. His lab has observed Josephson dynamics and superfluidity in strongly interacting Fermi gases and Anderson localization of matter waves.

He earned his PhD at the University of Trento with Massimo Inguscio and helped create some of the first quantum-degenerate Fermi-Bose mixtures.
