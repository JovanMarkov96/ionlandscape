Ana Maria Rey is a Fellow of JILA and Professor Adjoint at the University of Colorado Boulder, and a theorist of ultracold matter. Her work connects atomic, molecular and optical physics with condensed-matter and quantum-information science, including spin models with trapped ions and polar molecules, optical-lattice clocks, and the use of entanglement for quantum-enhanced metrology.
