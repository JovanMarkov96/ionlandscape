Jungsang Kim leads the Multifunctional Integrated Systems Technology (MIST) group at Duke University. He is the Schiciano Family Distinguished Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering and Professor of Physics, and serves as Associate Director of the Duke Quantum Center.

His research focuses on trapped-ion quantum information systems, with a strong emphasis on engineering and integration: microfabricated surface traps, optical micro-electromechanical systems (MEMS), advanced single-photon detectors, compact cryogenics/vacuum technologies, and system-scale architectures. He is a prominent figure in the co-design of scalable quantum computers and the use of photonic interconnects for modular quantum computing and quantum communication networks.

Prior to his focus on trapped ions, he earned his PhD from Stanford University (1999) under Yoshihisa Yamamoto, where his thesis work included the development of single-photon turnstile devices. He is a Fellow of the American Physical Society (2021).
