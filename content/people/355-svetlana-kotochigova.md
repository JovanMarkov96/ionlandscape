Svetlana Kotochigova is a Professor of Physics at Temple University and a theorist of the electronic structure, interactions and collisions of ultracold atoms and molecules. Her calculations of molecular potentials, transition dipole moments and Feshbach resonances guide experiments that create and control ultracold polar molecules such as KRb and RbCs.

Her work also addresses highly magnetic lanthanide atoms, quantum chaos in ultracold collisions, and the sensitivity of molecular spectra to a possible variation of fundamental constants.
