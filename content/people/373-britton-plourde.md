Britton Plourde is a Professor of Physics at the University of Wisconsin-Madison (previously at Syracuse University, 2005-2024), working on superconducting qubits and microwave circuits. His group studies coherence-limiting materials defects, quasiparticles and superconducting vortices, develops transmon and flux qubits and resonators, and pursues single-flux-quantum (SFQ) digital control for scalable superconducting processors.

He earned his PhD at the University of California, Berkeley with John Clarke.
