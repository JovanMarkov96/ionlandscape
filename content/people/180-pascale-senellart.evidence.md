## `key_papers`

### 1. "Nonlinear Emission of Microcavity Polaritons in the Low Density Regime" (1999)
- DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.82.1233
- Authors: P. Senellart and J. Bloch
- Published: Physical Review Letters 82, 1233 (8 February 1999)
- Role: `first_author` (Senellart is first and corresponding author)
- *Source*: https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.82.1233
- Significance: Senellart's debut paper; first observation of nonlinear polariton emission in a III-V microcavity stimulated by final-state population.

### 2. "Exciton-photon strong-coupling regime for a single quantum dot embedded in a microcavity" (2005)
- DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.95.067401
- Authors: E. Peter, P. Senellart, D. Martrou, A. Lemaître, J. Hours, J. M. Gérard, J. Bloch
- Published: Physical Review Letters 95, 067401 (2005)
- Role: `co_author` (Senellart is second author)
- *Source*: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16090987/; https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.95.067401
- Significance: First observation of vacuum Rabi splitting (strong coupling) for a single quantum dot in a semiconductor microcavity; vacuum Rabi splitting of 400 μeV.

### 3. "Ultrabright source of entangled photon pairs" (2010)
- DOI: 10.1038/nature09148
- Authors: A. Dousse, J. Suffczyński, A. Beveratos, O. Krebs, A. Lemaître, I. Sagnes, J. Bloch, P. Voisin, P. Senellart
- Published: Nature 466, 217–220 (8 July 2010)
- Role: `senior_author` (Senellart is last/corresponding author)
- *Source*: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature09148
- Significance: Demonstrated a photonic-molecule microcavity coupled to a single quantum dot producing entangled photon pairs with 80% overall efficiency — twenty times brighter than prior systems.

### 4. "Near-optimal single-photon sources in the solid state" (2016)
- DOI: 10.1038/nphoton.2016.23
- Authors: N. Somaschi, V. Giesz, L. De Santis, J. C. Loredo, M. P. Almeida, G. Hornecker, S. L. Portalupi, T. Grange, C. Antón, J. Demory, C. Gómez, I. Sagnes, N. D. Lanzillotti-Kimura, A. Lemaître, A. Auffeves, A. G. White, L. Lanco, P. Senellart
- Published: Nature Photonics 10, 340–345 (2016)
- Role: `corresponding` (Senellart is the corresponding/senior author)
- *Source*: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphoton.2016.23
- Significance: Reported semiconductor QD single-photon source with near-unity indistinguishability (~99%) and high brightness using an electrically controlled cavity; >1,370 citations; underpinned Quandela's commercial product line.

### 5. "High-performance semiconductor quantum-dot single-photon sources" (2017)
- DOI: 10.1038/nnano.2017.218
- Authors: P. Senellart, G. Solomon, A. White
- Published: Nature Nanotechnology 12, 1026–1039 (November 2017)
- Role: `first_author` (Senellart is first and corresponding author)
- *Source*: https://www.nature.com/articles/nnano.2017.218
- Significance: Definitive invited review article covering state-of-the-art quantum-dot single-photon technology, challenges, and roadmap; widely cited reference in the field.
