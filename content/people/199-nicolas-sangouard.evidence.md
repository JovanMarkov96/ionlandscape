## Key Papers

### 1. Quantum repeaters based on atomic ensembles and linear optics
- **Authors:** Nicolas Sangouard, Christoph Simon, Hugues de Riedmatten, Nicolas Gisin
- **Journal:** Rev. Mod. Phys. 83, 33 (2011)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/RevModPhys.83.33
- **arXiv:** 0906.2699
- **Citations:** ~2,755 (Google Scholar); canonical review of DLCZ-type quantum repeaters
- **Role:** First author; foundational review paper for the entire subfield
- **Source:** https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/RevModPhys.83.33

### 2. Experimental quantum key distribution certified by Bell's theorem
- **Authors:** D. P. Nadlinger, P. Drmota, B. C. Nichol, G. Araneda, D. Main, R. Srinivas, D. M. Lucas, C. J. Ballance, K. Ivanov, E. Y-Z. Tan, P. Sekatski, R. L. Urbanke, R. Renner, N. Sangouard, J-D. Bancal
- **Journal:** Nature 607, 682–686 (2022)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-022-04941-5
- **arXiv:** 2109.14600
- **Citations:** ~395 (Google Scholar); first and only complete device-independent QKD experiment at time of publication
- **Role:** Co-author (theory); provided DI-QKD security framework
- **Source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04941-5

### 3. Proposal for Implementing Device-Independent Quantum Key Distribution based on a Heralded Qubit Amplification
- **Authors:** Nicolas Gisin, Stefano Pironio, Nicolas Sangouard
- **Journal:** Phys. Rev. Lett. 105, 070501 (2010)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.070501
- **arXiv:** 1003.0635
- **Citations:** ~383 (Google Scholar); introduced heralded qubit amplifier concept for DI-QKD
- **Role:** Co-author
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.0635

### 4. Quantum storage of photonic entanglement in a crystal
- **Authors:** Christoph Clausen, Imam Usmani, Félix Bussières, Nicolas Sangouard, Mikael Afzelius, Hugues de Riedmatten, Nicolas Gisin
- **Journal:** Nature 469, 508–511 (2011)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature09662
- **arXiv:** 1009.0489
- **Citations:** ~645 (Google Scholar); first demonstration of entanglement between telecom photon and solid-state memory
- **Role:** Co-author (theory); provided framework for crystal-based quantum memory in repeater context
- **Source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/nature09662

### 5. Loophole-free Bell inequality violation with superconducting circuits
- **Authors:** Simon Storz, Josua Schär, Anatoly Kulikov, et al., Jean-Daniel Bancal, Nicolas Sangouard, Baptiste Royer, Alexandre Blais, Andreas Wallraff
- **Journal:** Nature 617, 265–270 (2023)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-023-05885-0
- **Citations:** recent high-impact paper; loophole-free Bell test with superconducting qubits
- **Role:** Co-author (theory)
- **Source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-05885-0
