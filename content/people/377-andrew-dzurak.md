Andrew Dzurak is a Professor of Physics at UNSW Sydney who develops silicon spin qubits in metal-oxide-semiconductor (MOS) quantum dots, fabricated with CMOS-compatible processes for scalability. His group demonstrated the first two-qubit logic gate in silicon and high-fidelity addressable spin qubits.
