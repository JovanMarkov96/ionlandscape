Kenji Toyoda conducts trapped-ion research at Osaka University, focusing on quantum simulation, quantum computing, and motional phonon dynamics with calcium ions.

He received his PhD from Kyoto University (2002) working under Shinji Urabe, a pioneer of trapped ion research in Japan. He was the senior author on the landmark 2015 Nature paper demonstrating Hong-Ou-Mandel interference of phonons in trapped ions.
