Tilman Esslinger is a Professor of Physics at ETH Zürich, where he leads the Quantum Optics Group. His group is renowned for pioneering experiments with ultracold quantum gases in optical lattices and cavities, and more recently for hybrid atom-ion systems.

He received his PhD in Physics from Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (1995) under Nobel laureate Theodor W. Hänsch, where his doctoral research focused on subrecoil laser cooling and optical lattices at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics. He was a co-author on the seminal 2002 Nature paper demonstrating the quantum phase transition from a superfluid to a Mott insulator in ultracold atoms — one of the most cited experiments in cold-atom physics.

He joined the ETH Zürich faculty in 2001 and has since expanded his research into cavity QED with quantum gases and hybrid atom-ion experiments using lithium ions.
