Ksenia Khabarova is an experimental physicist at the P. N. Lebedev Physical Institute (FIAN) and the Russian Quantum Center in Moscow, working on trapped-ion quantum computing with ytterbium-171 (171Yb+) ions and on optical atomic clocks and precision frequency metrology. She is part of the joint FIAN–RQC group that built Russia's ion-based quantum computer using qudit (ququart) encoding.
