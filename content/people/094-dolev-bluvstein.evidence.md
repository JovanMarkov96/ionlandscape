## Key papers

### 1. Controlling quantum many-body dynamics in driven Rydberg atom arrays (2021)
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.abg2530
- **Journal:** Science 371, 1355–1359 (2021)
- **Role:** first_author (D. Bluvstein is first author; M. D. Lukin is senior/last author)
- **Significance:** Demonstrates quantum many-body scars and discrete time-crystal-like dynamics in driven Rydberg arrays of up to 200 atoms; first major first-author paper from Bluvstein's PhD
- **Source:** https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abg2530
- **Authors:** D. Bluvstein, A. Omran, H. Levine, A. Keesling, G. Semeghini, S. Ebadi, T. T. Wang, A. A. Michailidis, N. Maskara, W. W. Ho, S. Choi, M. Serbyn, M. Greiner, V. Vuletic, M. D. Lukin

### 2. A quantum processor based on coherent transport of entangled atom arrays (2022)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-022-04592-6
- **Journal:** Nature 604, 451–456 (2022)
- **Role:** first_author (D. Bluvstein is first author; M. D. Lukin is senior/last author)
- **Significance:** Introduces reconfigurable atom-array architecture with coherent qubit transport; demonstrates parallel two-qubit gates with arbitrary connectivity across two spatial dimensions; foundational paper for Bluvstein's PhD thesis direction
- **Source:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35444318/
- **Authors:** D. Bluvstein, H. Levine, G. Semeghini, T. T. Wang, S. Ebadi, M. Kalinowski, A. Keesling, N. Maskara, H. Pichler, M. Greiner, V. Vuletić, M. D. Lukin

### 3. High-fidelity parallel entangling gates on a neutral-atom quantum computer (2023)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-023-06481-y
- **Journal:** Nature 622, 268–272 (2023)
- **Role:** co_author (S. J. Evered is first author; Bluvstein is second author; M. D. Lukin is senior/last author)
- **Significance:** Achieves 99.5% two-qubit gate fidelity on up to 60 atoms in parallel, surpassing the surface-code error-correction threshold; essential enabling result for the logical qubit work
- **Source:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37821591/
- **Authors:** S. J. Evered, D. Bluvstein, M. Kalinowski, S. Ebadi, T. Manovitz, H. Zhou, S. H. Li, A. A. Geim, T. T. Wang, N. Maskara, H. Levine, G. Semeghini, M. Greiner, V. Vuletic, M. D. Lukin

### 4. Logical quantum processor based on reconfigurable atom arrays (2024)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-023-06927-3
- **Journal:** Nature 626, 58–65 (2024)
- **Role:** first_author (D. Bluvstein is first author; M. D. Lukin is senior/last author)
- **Significance:** First programmable logical quantum processor; demonstrates up to 48 logical qubits using surface and color codes with transversal gates; awarded Physics World 2024 Breakthrough of the Year
- **Source:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38056497/
- **Authors:** D. Bluvstein, S. J. Evered, A. A. Geim, S. H. Li, H. Zhou, T. Manovitz, S. Ebadi, M. Cain, M. Kalinowski, D. Hangleiter, J. P. Bonilla Ataides, N. Maskara, I. Cong, X. Gao, P. Sales Rodriguez, T. Karolyshyn, G. Semeghini, M. J. Gullans, M. Greiner, V. Vuletić, M. D. Lukin

### 5. A fault-tolerant neutral-atom architecture for universal quantum computation (2026)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-025-09848-5
- **Journal:** Nature 649, 39–46 (2026)
- **Role:** first_author (D. Bluvstein is first author; M. D. Lukin and M. Cain are senior/last authors)
