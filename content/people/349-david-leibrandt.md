David Leibrandt is a Professor of Physics at UCLA, where his group develops next-generation optical atomic clocks and precision measurements, including aluminium-ion (Al+) quantum-logic clocks, quantum-logic spectroscopy of molecular ions, and proposals for many-ion Coulomb-crystal clocks.

He earned his PhD at MIT and led trapped-ion optical-clock experiments in the Ion Storage Group at NIST Boulder, where he helped demonstrate an Al+ clock with systematic uncertainty below one part in 10^18, before joining the UCLA faculty in 2022.
