## Data quality notes

- **BS degree year**: Sources disagree (1992 vs. 1996). Given 1987 entry year and a known MS in 1995, a 1992 BS fits the standard USTC 5-year program. The 1996 date in some sources is likely erroneous.
- **Since_year 2001 vs. 2008**: Pan started splitting time between USTC and Vienna postdoc around 2001; became Distinguished Chair Professor at USTC in 2008; became CAS Center director in 2014. `since_year: 2001` reflects his first formal USTC appointment per Wikipedia.
- **Heidelberg postdoc years**: Wikipedia states he built a group at Heidelberg ~2003 while retaining a USTC affiliation. Exact end date at Heidelberg is not confirmed; 2008 (Chair Prof appointment at USTC) used as an approximation.
- **h-index**: Not confirmed from a primary source (Google Scholar is access-gated). Omitted from metrics.
- **NV center / color_center platform**: Pan's group does quantum memory work with rare-earth atomic ensembles and trapped-ion interfaces; no direct NV-center experiments found under Pan's personal authorship. Platform excluded to avoid over-attribution.
- **quantum_dot platform**: Confirmed via USTC group page and 2025 Nature Photonics paper on semiconductor QD single-photon source by Pan, Lu, Hu.
