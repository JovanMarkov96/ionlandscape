## Key Papers

### 1. Strongly Correlated Gases of Rydberg-Dressed Atoms: Quantum and Classical Dynamics
- **Authors:** G. Pupillo, A. Micheli, M. Boninsegni, I. Lesanovsky, P. Zoller
- **Journal:** Phys. Rev. Lett. 104, 223002 (2010)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.104.223002
- **Source:** https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.104.223002
- **Role:** first_author (Pupillo is first author)
- **Citations:** ~422 (Google Scholar)
- **Notes:** Foundational paper introducing Rydberg-dressing concept for generating tunable long-range interactions in ground-state atom gases.

### 2. Supersolid Droplet Crystal in a Dipole-Blockaded Gas
- **Authors:** F. Cinti, P. Jain, M. Boninsegni, A. Micheli, P. Zoller, G. Pupillo
- **Journal:** Phys. Rev. Lett. 105, 135301 (2010)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.135301
- **Source:** https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.105.135301
- **Role:** senior_author (Pupillo is last/corresponding author)
- **Notes:** Predicts novel supersolid phase for Rydberg atoms in the dipole-blockade regime; a crystal of mesoscopic superfluid droplets.

### 3. Condensed Matter Theory of Dipolar Quantum Gases
- **Authors:** M. A. Baranov, M. Dalmonte, G. Pupillo, P. Zoller
- **Journal:** Chemical Reviews 112, 5012 (2012)
- **DOI:** 10.1021/cr2003568
- **Source:** https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/cr2003568
- **Role:** co_author (Pupillo is 3rd of 4 authors)
- **Citations:** ~890 (Google Scholar)
- **Notes:** Comprehensive review of dipolar quantum gases covering bosonic, fermionic, and strongly correlated regimes.

### 4. Cluster Luttinger Liquids of Rydberg-Dressed Atoms in Optical Lattices
- **Authors:** M. Mattioli, M. Dalmonte, W. Lechner, G. Pupillo
- **Journal:** Phys. Rev. Lett. 111, 165302 (2013)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.111.165302
- **Source:** https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.111.165302
- **Role:** senior_author (Pupillo is last/corresponding author)
- **Notes:** Demonstrates stabilization of exotic critical quantum liquids (cluster Luttinger liquids) in Rydberg-dressed atoms.

### 5. Strongly Correlated 2D Quantum Phases with Cold Polar Molecules
- **Authors:** H. P. Büchler, E. Demler, M. Lukin, A. Micheli, N. Prokof'ev, G. Pupillo, P. Zoller
- **Journal:** Phys. Rev. Lett. 98, 060404 (2007)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.98.060404
- **Source:** https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.98.060404
- **Role:** co_author (Pupillo is 6th of 7 authors)
- **Citations:** ~686 (Google Scholar)
- **Notes:** Key early paper on engineering strongly correlated quantum phases using polar molecules; co-authored during Innsbruck postdoc.
