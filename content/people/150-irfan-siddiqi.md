Irfan Siddiqi is an experimental physicist at the University of California, Berkeley and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, where he leads the Advanced Quantum Testbed. His group pioneered quantum-limited Josephson parametric amplifiers and the continuous monitoring and feedback control of superconducting transmon qubits, tracking individual quantum trajectories, and builds multi-qubit superconducting processors.
