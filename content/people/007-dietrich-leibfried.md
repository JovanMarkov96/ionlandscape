Dietrich Leibfried is a leading experimental physicist in trapped-ion quantum information science at NIST (Boulder). He received his PhD (Dr. rer. nat., summa cum laude) from Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München in 1995 under Theodor W. Hänsch, working on precision laser spectroscopy of hydrogen at the Max Planck Institute for Quantum Optics.

After postdoctoral work with David Wineland at NIST (1995–1998), he held an assistant professor position at the University of Innsbruck collaborating with Rainer Blatt (1998–2001), earning his Habilitation in 2005. He joined NIST as a staff physicist in 2001 and serves as co-leader of the group working on quantum information processing, quantum networking, and quantum control with trapped ions. He has published more than 160 peer-reviewed articles.

His key contributions include the creation of multi-particle entangled states for quantum-enhanced metrology, foundational work on trapped-ion quantum logic, and recent work on quantum control of molecular ions and ion-photon interfaces for quantum networking.
