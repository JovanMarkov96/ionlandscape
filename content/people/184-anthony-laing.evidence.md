## key_papers

### 10.1063/1.3497087
- **Title**: High-fidelity operation of quantum photonic circuits
- **Authors**: Laing, A., Peruzzo, A., Politi, A., Verde, M.R., Halder, M., Ralph, T.C., Thompson, M.G., O'Brien, J.L.
- **Journal**: Applied Physics Letters, 97(21), 211109 (2010)
- **Role**: Laing is first author
- **Source**: https://pubs.aip.org/aip/apl/article-abstract/97/21/211109/120516/ and https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.0326
- **Significance**: Early demonstration of near-unit fidelity photonic quantum circuits on silica-on-silicon waveguides; foundational for the Bristol photonics programme.

### 10.1103/PhysRevA.82.012304
- **Title**: Reference-frame-independent quantum key distribution
- **Authors**: Laing, A., Scarani, V., Rarity, J.G., O'Brien, J.L.
- **Journal**: Physical Review A, 82, 012304 (2010)
- **Role**: Laing is first author
- **Source**: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.82.012304 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.1050
- **Significance**: Invented the RFI-QKD protocol; subsequently patented with Nokia. Described in his institutional biography as a signature achievement.

### 10.1038/nphoton.2011.283
- **Title**: Generating, manipulating and measuring entanglement and mixture with a reconfigurable photonic circuit
- **Authors**: Shadbolt, P.J., Verde, M.R., Peruzzo, A., Politi, A., Laing, A., Lobino, M., Matthews, J.C.F., Thompson, M.G., O'Brien, J.L.
- **Journal**: Nature Photonics, 6, 45–49 (2012)
- **Role**: Laing is co-author
- **Source**: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphoton.2011.283
- **Significance**: First reconfigurable integrated photonic circuit with 8 voltage-controlled phase shifters for arbitrary two-photon state generation and characterization.

### 10.1126/science.aab3642
- **Title**: Universal linear optics
- **Authors**: Carolan, J., Harrold, C., Sparrow, C., Martín-López, E., Russell, N.J., Silverstone, J.W., Shadbolt, P.J., Matsuda, N., Oguma, M., Itoh, M., Marshall, G.D., Thompson, M.G., Matthews, J.C.F., Hashimoto, T., O'Brien, J.L., Laing, A.
- **Journal**: Science, 349(6249), 711–716 (2015)
- **Role**: Laing is co-author/senior author (last author)
- **Source**: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aab3642
- **Significance**: First fully reprogrammable linear optical circuit sufficient for all linear optical protocols; 15 MZIs, 30 phase shifters on a single chip. 1,437 citations (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30).

### 10.1038/s41586-018-0152-9
- **Title**: Simulating the vibrational quantum dynamics of molecules using photonics
- **Authors**: Sparrow, C., Martín-López, E., Maraviglia, N., Neville, A., Harrold, C., Carolan, J., Joglekar, Y.N., Hashimoto, T., Matsuda, N., O'Brien, J.L., Tew, D.P., Laing, A.
- **Journal**: Nature, 557(7707), 660–667 (2018)
- **Role**: Laing is senior/corresponding author
- **Source**: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-018-0152-9 and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29849155/
- **Significance**: Reprogrammable photonic chip simulates vibrational quantum dynamics of SO₂, O₃, H₂O, and other molecules; demonstrates variational quantum eigensolver-type photonic molecular simulation.

### 10.1038/s41566-019-0532-1
- **Title**: Integrated photonic quantum technologies
- **Authors**: Wang, J., Sciarrino, F., Laing, A., Thompson, M.G.
- **Journal**: Nature Photonics, 14, 273–284 (2020)
- **Role**: Laing is co-author

- **Significance**: Major review covering materials, devices and applications of integrated quantum photonics; 1,981 citations (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30), most-cited paper in Laing's corpus.

### 10.1126/sciadv.abl9236
- **Title**: The boundary for quantum advantage in Gaussian boson sampling
- **Authors**: Bulmer, J.F.F., Bell, B.A., Chadwick, R.S., Jones, A.E., Moise, D., Rigazzi, A., Thorbecke, J., Haus, U.-U., Van Vaerenbergh, T., Patel, R.B., Walmsley, I.A., Laing, A.
