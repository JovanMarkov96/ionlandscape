## Key papers

### 1. A High Phase-Space-Density Gas of Polar Molecules (2008)
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.1163861
- **Journal:** Science 322, 231–235 (2008)
- **Role:** first_author (K.-K. Ni is first author; advisors Jin and Ye are senior authors)
- **Significance:** Landmark paper creating the first ultracold KRb polar molecular gas in the rovibrational ground state via STIRAP
- **Source:** https://www.science.org/doi/abs/10.1126/science.1163861

### 2. Building one molecule from a reservoir of two atoms (2018)
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aar7797
- **Journal:** Science 360, 900–903 (2018)
- **Role:** senior_author (Ni is corresponding/senior; first independent Harvard work)
- **Significance:** Demonstrates optical-tweezer assembly of a single NaCs molecule atom by atom; Science cover paper
- **Source:** https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aar7797
- **Source:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29650700/

### 3. An optical tweezer array of ground-state polar molecules (2022)
- **DOI:** 10.1088/2058-9565/ac676c
- **Journal:** Quantum Science and Technology 7, 035006 (2022)
- **Role:** senior_author
- **Significance:** Extends optical tweezer technique to arrays of five NaCs molecules; key step toward multi-qubit experiments
- **Source:** https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2058-9565/ac676c/meta

### 4. Dipolar spin-exchange and entanglement between molecules in an optical tweezer array (2023)
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.adf8999
- **Journal:** Science 382, 1138–1143 (2023)
- **Role:** senior_author (co-senior author alongside John Doyle and Wolfgang Ketterle)
- **Significance:** First demonstration of coherent dipolar spin-exchange and entanglement between pairs of CaF molecules; key step toward molecular quantum computing
- **Source:** https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adf8999

### 5. Entanglement and iSWAP Gate between Molecular Qubits (2025)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-024-08177-3
- **Journal:** Nature 637, (2025); epub Nov 2024
- **Role:** senior_author
- **Significance:** First high-fidelity iSWAP entangling gate between molecular (NaCs) qubits using dipolar interactions in optical tweezers
- **Source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08177-3
- **Source:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39537926/
