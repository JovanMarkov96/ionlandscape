Frédéric Merkt is a Professor of Physical Chemistry at ETH Zurich, known for high-resolution spectroscopy of molecules and Rydberg states. His group performs precision measurements of the ionization and dissociation energies of molecular hydrogen and its ions (H2, H2+, HD+) as stringent tests of quantum electrodynamics, and developed Rydberg-Stark deceleration to produce slow, controlled beams of atoms and molecules.

He was the PhD advisor of Stefan Willitsch.
