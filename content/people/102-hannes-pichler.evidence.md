## Key papers

All DOIs confirmed via IQOQI publications page (https://iqoqi.at/en/people/workgroup-staff/pichler-gruppe/publications/hannes-pichler) and Google Scholar.

1. **Probing many-body dynamics on a 51-atom quantum simulator** — Bernien et al., *Nature* 551, 2017. DOI: 10.1038/nature24622. Pichler is co-author (Lukin/Harvard group). 3,354 citations.
2. **Photonic tensor networks produced by a single quantum emitter** — Pichler, Choi, Zoller, Lukin. *PNAS* 114, 2017. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1711003114. First-author theory paper.
3. **Quantum Kibble-Zurek mechanism and critical dynamics on a programmable Rydberg simulator** — Keesling et al., *Nature* 568, 2019. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-019-1070-1. Pichler co-author.
4. **Quantum phases of matter on a 256-atom programmable quantum simulator** — Ebadi et al., *Nature* 595, 2021. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-021-03582-4. Pichler co-author. 1,517 citations.
5. **Probing topological spin liquids on a programmable quantum simulator** — Semeghini et al., *Science* 374, 2021. DOI: 10.1126/science.abi8794. Pichler co-author. 1,060 citations.
6. **Universal Quantum Computation in Globally Driven Rydberg Atom Arrays** — Cesa & Pichler. *Physical Review Letters* 131, 2023. DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.131.170601. Pichler is senior/corresponding author.
7. **Fermionic quantum processing with programmable neutral atom arrays** — González-Cuadra et al., *PNAS* 120, 2023. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2304294120. Pichler senior author.
