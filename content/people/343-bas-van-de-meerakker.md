Bas (Sebastiaan) van de Meerakker is a Professor of Physics at Radboud University Nijmegen, where his group uses Stark-decelerated molecular beams to study cold molecular collisions with full quantum-state resolution, revealing scattering resonances in benchmark systems such as OH, NO and NH3.

He earned his PhD at Radboud University with Gerard Meijer, developing the deceleration and electrostatic trapping of OH radicals, and has become a leader in velocity-controlled crossed-beam scattering experiments.
