Utako Tanaka is an Associate Professor at Osaka University. Her research group focuses on the design, fabrication, and operation of microfabricated surface-electrode ion traps, primarily using calcium ions for quantum computing and quantum simulation applications. She is a key participant in the international TRiAC collaboration for advanced trap development.

She received her PhD from Kyoto University.
