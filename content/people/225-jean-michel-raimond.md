Jean-Michel Raimond is a French physicist at Sorbonne Universite and the Laboratoire Kastler Brossel, where, with Serge Haroche, he developed cavity quantum electrodynamics experiments probing the quantum-to-classical transition with Rydberg atoms and microwave photons.
