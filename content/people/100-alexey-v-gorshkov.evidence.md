## Key Papers

### 1. Two-orbital SU(N) magnetism with ultracold alkaline-earth atoms (2010)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nphys1535
- **Journal:** Nature Physics 6, 289–295 (2010)
- **Authors:** A. V. Gorshkov, M. Hermele, V. Gurarie, C. Xu, P. S. Julienne, J. Ye, P. Zoller, E. Demler, M. D. Lukin, A. M. Rey
- **Role:** first_author
- **Significance:** Proposed using alkaline-earth atoms in optical lattices as quantum simulators of SU(N) many-body phenomena with N up to 10; highly influential in cold-atom quantum simulation.
- Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys1535; arXiv:0905.2610

### 2. Photon-Photon Interactions via Rydberg Blockade (2011)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.107.133602
- **Journal:** Physical Review Letters 107, 133602 (2011)
- **Authors:** A. V. Gorshkov, J. Otterbach, M. Fleischhauer, T. Pohl, M. D. Lukin
- **Role:** first_author
- **Significance:** Theoretical framework for implementing single-photon-level nonlinearities using Rydberg-EIT; foundational for quantum nonlinear optics with Rydberg atoms.
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.3700

### 3. Tunable Superfluidity and Quantum Magnetism with Ultracold Polar Molecules (2011)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevLett.107.115301
- **Journal:** Physical Review Letters 107, 115301 (2011)
- **Authors:** A. V. Gorshkov, S. R. Manmana, G. Chen, J. Ye, E. Demler, M. D. Lukin, A. M. Rey
- **Role:** first_author
- **Significance:** Showed that dressed polar molecules in optical lattices realize a highly tunable t-J-V-W model; opened the field of quantum magnetism with polar molecules.
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1644; https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.107.115301

### 4. Attractive photons in a quantum nonlinear medium (2013)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature12512
- **Journal:** Nature 502, 71–75 (2013)
- **Authors:** O. Firstenberg, T. Peyronel, Q.-Y. Liang, A. V. Gorshkov, M. D. Lukin, V. Vuletic
- **Role:** co_author (Gorshkov contributed theory)
- **Significance:** Demonstrated photon-photon interactions via Rydberg-EIT creating two-photon bound states; named one of Physics World's ten breakthroughs of 2013.
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24067613/

### 5. Topological phases in ultracold polar-molecule quantum magnets (2013)
- **DOI:** 10.1103/PhysRevB.87.081106
- **Journal:** Physical Review B 87, 081106(R) (2013)
- **Authors:** S. R. Manmana, E. M. Stoudenmire, K. R. A. Hazzard, A. M. Rey, A. V. Gorshkov
- **Role:** co_author
- **Significance:** Demonstrated symmetry-protected topological phases in polar-molecule spin ladders; bridged polar-molecule quantum simulation and topological physics.
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.5518
