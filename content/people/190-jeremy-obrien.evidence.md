## `key_papers`

### 1. A variational eigenvalue solver on a photonic quantum processor (2014)
- **DOI**: 10.1038/ncomms5213
- **Journal**: Nature Communications, vol. 5, article 4213
- **Authors**: Peruzzo, McClean, Shadbolt, Yung, Zhou, Love, Aspuru-Guzik, O'Brien (senior/corresponding)
- **Role**: senior_author
- **Citations**: ~6,835 (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30)
- *Source*: https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms5213
- *Note*: Original proposal and experimental demonstration of the Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) algorithm on a photonic chip; most-cited paper on O'Brien's scholar profile

### 2. Demonstration of an all-optical quantum controlled-NOT gate (2003)
- **DOI**: 10.1038/nature02054
- **Journal**: Nature, vol. 426, pp. 264–267
- **Authors**: O'Brien, Pryde, White, Ralph, Branning
- **Role**: first_author
- **Citations**: ~1,375 (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30)
- *Source*: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_O'Brien — cites Nature 426(6964):264–267
- *Note*: First experimental demonstration of a two-photon optical CNOT gate; landmark paper that launched optical quantum computing

### 3. Optical quantum computing (2007)
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.1142892
- **Journal**: Science, vol. 318, pp. 1567–1570
- **Authors**: O'Brien, J. L. (sole author review)
- **Role**: sole_author
- **Citations**: ~1,811 (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30)
- *Source*: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1142892
- *Note*: Comprehensive review of optical quantum computing; widely assigned in quantum optics courses

### 4. Silica-on-silicon waveguide quantum circuits (2008)
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.1155441
- **Journal**: Science, vol. 320, pp. 646–649
- **Authors**: Politi, Cryan, Rarity, Yu, O'Brien (senior)
- **Role**: senior_author
- **Citations**: ~1,431 (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30)
- *Source*: https://www.science.org/doi/abs/10.1126/science.1155441
- *Note*: Founded the field of integrated quantum photonics; first demonstration of quantum gates on a silica-on-silicon waveguide chip

### 5. Universal linear optics (2015)
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.aab3642
- **Journal**: Science, vol. 349, pp. 711–716
- **Authors**: Carolan, Harrold, Sparrow, Martin-Lopez, Russell, Silverstone, Shadbolt, Matsuda, Oguma, Itoh, Marshall, Thompson, Matthews, Hashimoto, O'Brien, Laing
- **Role**: senior_author
- **Citations**: ~1,437 (Google Scholar, 2026-05-30)
- *Source*: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aab3642
- *Note*: First single reprogrammable optical circuit implementing all possible linear optical protocols; 15 MZIs, 30 phase shifters on-chip
