### Key papers

1. **An atom-by-atom assembler of defect-free arbitrary two-dimensional atomic arrays** (2016)
   - DOI: 10.1126/science.aah3778
   - Journal: Science 354(6315), 1021–1023
   - Authors: D. Barredo, S. de Léséleuc, V. Lienhard, T. Lahaye, A. Browaeys
   - Role: **first_author**
   - Citations: ~1,238 (Google Scholar)
   - Significance: Demonstrated real-time atom-by-atom assembly of defect-free 2D arrays of single atoms in optical tweezers; foundational result in the tweezer-assembly field

2. **Synthetic three-dimensional atomic structures assembled atom by atom** (2018)
   - DOI: 10.1038/s41586-018-0450-2
   - Journal: Nature 561, 79–82
   - Authors: D. Barredo, V. Lienhard, S. de Léséleuc, T. Lahaye, A. Browaeys
   - Role: **first_author**
   - Citations: ~809 (Google Scholar)
   - Significance: Extended atom-by-atom assembly to 3D arbitrary geometries (up to 72 atoms); demonstrated holographic methods for plane-by-plane assembly

3. **Tunable two-dimensional arrays of single Rydberg atoms for realizing quantum Ising models** (2016)
   - DOI: 10.1038/nature18274
   - Journal: Nature 534, 667–670
   - Authors: H. Labuhn, D. Barredo, S. Ravets, S. de Léséleuc, T. Macrì, T. Lahaye, A. Browaeys
   - Role: **co_author** (Barredo 2nd author)
   - Citations: ~1,055 (Google Scholar)
   - Significance: First demonstration of quantum Ising simulation with tunable 2D Rydberg arrays up to 30 spins

4. **Observation of a symmetry-protected topological phase of interacting bosons with Rydberg atoms** (2019)
   - DOI: 10.1126/science.aav9105
   - Journal: Science 365(6455), 775–780
   - Authors: S. de Léséleuc, V. Lienhard, P. Scholl, D. Barredo, S. Weber, N. Lang, H. P. Büchler, T. Lahaye, A. Browaeys
   - Role: **co_author**
   - Citations: ~735 (Google Scholar)
   - Significance: Experimental realization of a symmetry-protected topological phase using Rydberg-dressed hard-core bosons in 1D optical tweezer arrays

5. **Quantum simulation of 2D antiferromagnets with hundreds of Rydberg atoms** (2021)
   - DOI: 10.1038/s41586-021-03585-1
   - Journal: Nature 595, 233–238
   - Authors: P. Scholl, M. Schuler, H. J. Williams, A. A. Eberharter, D. Barredo, K.-N. Schymik, V. Lienhard, L.-P. Henry, T. C. Lang, T. Lahaye, A. M. Läuchli, A. Browaeys
   - Role: **co_author**
   - Citations: ~998 (Google Scholar)
   - Significance: Scaled Rydberg tweezer platform to ~196 atoms; programmable simulation of 2D transverse-field Ising antiferromagnet
