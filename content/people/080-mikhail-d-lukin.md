Mikhail Lukin is a Professor of Physics at Harvard University and a co-director of the Harvard Quantum Initiative, working at the interface of quantum optics, quantum information and many-body physics. His group pioneered programmable quantum simulators and quantum processors built from reconfigurable optical-tweezer arrays of rubidium (Rb) atoms excited to Rydberg states, demonstrating 51-atom quantum simulations, hundreds-of-qubit arrays and logical qubits.

His broader work spans single-photon nonlinear optics and Rydberg polaritons, quantum networks and quantum memories, and quantum sensing with nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centres in diamond.
