Andrew M. Steane is a Professor of Physics at the University of Oxford and a fellow of Exeter College. He co-discovered quantum error correction — the CSS/"Steane" codes — and initiated Oxford's trapped-ion quantum-computing experiments, which he later co-led with David Lucas using calcium-ion (⁴⁰Ca⁺ and ⁴³Ca⁺) qubits. He received the Institute of Physics Maxwell Medal and Prize in 2000 and has written several physics textbooks. His work spans both the theory and experimental implementation of quantum information with trapped ions.
