Kenneth R. Brown is the Michael J. Fitzpatrick Distinguished Professor of Engineering at Duke University and the Director of the Duke Quantum Center. He holds appointments in the Departments of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Physics, and Chemistry. 

His research group focuses heavily on practical quantum error correction, fault-tolerant quantum computing, and advanced quantum control techniques. Working closely with experimental setups utilizing calcium, ytterbium, and novel boron ions, his group has made landmark contributions to the realization of logical qubits and fault-tolerant operations in trapped-ion hardware. In addition to his academic roles, he serves as a Scientific Advisor for IonQ.

He received his PhD in Theoretical Chemistry from UC Berkeley (2003) under K. Birgitta Whaley, exploring theoretical issues in quantum information. Following his PhD, he transitioned further into experimental and applied quantum computing during his postdoctoral training at MIT under Isaac L. Chuang.
