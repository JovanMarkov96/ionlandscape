Olivier Dulieu is a CNRS Senior Researcher at the Laboratoire Aimé Cotton (Université Paris-Saclay) and a theorist of cold and ultracold molecules. His work established the theoretical foundations for forming cold molecules by photoassociation and for transferring them to their absolute ground state, and provides the molecular structure and interaction data underpinning ultracold-molecule experiments worldwide.

He has been a central figure in the European cold-molecules community, collaborating with many experimental groups on species ranging from Cs2 to heteronuclear alkali dimers such as RbCs.
