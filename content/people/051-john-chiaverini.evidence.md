# Evidence — 051-john-chiaverini
Verified: 2026-05-04. PhD Stanford 2002 (Aharon Kapitulnik). Postdoc NIST (David Wineland).

## Key papers
- 10.1038/nature03074 — Chiaverini et al., Nature 2004; first experimental demonstration of quantum error correction with trapped ions (9Be+). Chiaverini is first author.
- 10.26421/QIC5.6-1 — Chiaverini et al., QIC 2005; foundational surface-electrode architecture proposal for scalable ion-trap QIP. Chiaverini is first author.
- 10.1038/nnano.2016.139 — Mehta, Bruzewicz, McConnell, Ram, Sage, Chiaverini, Nature Nanotechnology 2016; first integrated nanophotonic waveguides in a surface-electrode trap for ion addressing. Chiaverini is corresponding/senior author (MIT Lincoln Lab).
- 10.1038/s41586-020-2811-x — Niffenegger, Stuart et al. incl. Chiaverini, Nature 2020; integrated multi-wavelength photonic chip for full ion qubit control (Sr+). Chiaverini is senior author.
