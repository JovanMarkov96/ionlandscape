Yasunobu Nakamura is an experimental physicist at the University of Tokyo and the director of the RIKEN Center for Quantum Computing. In 1999 he demonstrated the first coherent control of a superconducting qubit (a Cooper-pair-box charge qubit), launching the field of superconducting quantum computing. His work spans microwave quantum optics, quantum acoustics, hybrid quantum systems, and large-scale superconducting quantum processors.
