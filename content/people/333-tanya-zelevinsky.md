Tanya Zelevinsky is a Professor of Physics at Columbia University, where her ZLab uses high-precision spectroscopy of ultracold molecules to test fundamental physics. She built a molecular lattice clock based on quantized vibrations of ultracold strontium dimers (Sr2) and studies ultracold molecular photodissociation and quantum-state control of diatomic molecules.

She earned her PhD in physics from Harvard University in 2004 with Gerald Gabrielse, then performed seminal work on the strontium optical lattice clock as a postdoctoral researcher in Jun Ye's group at JILA, where she was the first to create ultracold Sr2 molecules in an optical lattice. She joined Columbia in 2008 to start the university's modern atomic, molecular and optical physics program.
