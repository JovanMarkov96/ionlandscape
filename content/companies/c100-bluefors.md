Bluefors is a Helsinki, Finland company founded in 2008 by physicists Rob Blaauwgeers and Pieter Vorselman (the name combines their surnames). It is the world's leading maker of dilution refrigerators — the systems that cool quantum processors to a few thousandths of a degree above absolute zero, colder than deep space.

The company's origins lie at the Low Temperature Laboratory in Helsinki, where Blaauwgeers developed one of the first cryogen-free ("dry") dilution refrigerators together with a fully automated gas-handling system. Cryogen-free systems proved dramatically simpler and more reliable to operate than conventional "wet" refrigerators, and as superconducting and spin quantum computers took off, Bluefors' refrigerators became the de facto industry standard cooling platform for them.

Bluefors has grown into a global business with more than 700 employees, having delivered over 1,700 cryogenic systems and many thousands of cryocoolers to leading research institutions and quantum companies, and it continues to develop higher-cooling-power and ultra-compact systems to support ever-larger quantum machines.
