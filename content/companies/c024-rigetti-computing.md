short_summary: 'Rigetti Computing (founded 2013, Berkeley CA; NASDAQ: RGTI) is a publicly
  traded superconducting quantum computing company that designs and fabricates its
  own processors at its Fab-1 facility in Fremont and delivers them via Quantum Cloud
  Services (QCS) and cloud marketplaces. It pioneered the Quil quantum instruction
  language and the Forest open-source SDK, went public via SPAC in March 2022, and
  has advanced through Aspen and Ankaa processor generations to the 108-qubit Cepheus-1
  system (April 2026, 99.1% two-qubit CZ fidelity). In May 2026, Rigetti signed a
  letter of intent for up to $100 million in CHIPS Act funding from the U.S. Department
  of Commerce.'
