Qruise is a Saarbrücken, Germany software company founded in 2021 that applies machine learning to the development of quantum computers. It grew out of the quantum-control research community around Tommaso Calarco, with Shai Machnes and Frank Wilhelm-Mauch as managing directors. Its mission is to let scientists and engineers use sophisticated machine-learning and optimal-control techniques in their everyday workflow without having to become machine-learning experts themselves.

The company's software addresses one of the most time-consuming parts of building a quantum computer: characterising, calibrating and controlling the hardware. Its flagship QruiseOS performs rapid, in-depth characterization, calibration and optimal control of quantum devices, shipping with more than thirty pre-defined experiments. Its companion product, QruiseML, uses model learning to generate highly accurate "digital twins" of quantum devices by inferring system parameters directly from experimental data, which in turn improves gate fidelities and accelerates device bring-up.

Qruise's tools are hardware-agnostic, serving superconducting, trapped-ion and neutral-atom platforms, and the company is an active partner in German and European quantum initiatives such as the Munich Quantum Valley.
