Welinq is a Paris quantum-networking company building the interconnects needed to link quantum processors into larger, distributed machines. Founded in 2022 as a spin-off of Sorbonne Université and the Laboratoire Kastler-Brossel, its founding team combines quantum-hardware and quantum-protocol expertise: CEO Tom Darras, hardware scientific director Julien Laurat (a professor at LKB), protocols scientific director Eleni Diamanti (a CNRS research director at LIP6), and Jean Lautier-Gaud.

The company's core technology is a neutral-atom (cold-atom) quantum memory that can store and synchronise quantum information with high performance, paired with a hardware-agnostic, full-stack "quantum link" that connects quantum processing units regardless of their underlying qubit technology. This is aimed squarely at the scaling problem in quantum computing: rather than building one ever-larger processor, Welinq's memories and links let many processors be networked into a distributed quantum computer or quantum data centre.

Welinq raised a EUR 5 million seed round in 2023, including deep-tech investor Quantonation, and in 2025 began commercialising its hardware — launching what it describes as the first commercial quantum memory for distributed quantum architectures and selling its first rack-mounted entangled photon-pair source for quantum networks, with units being deployed across Europe.
