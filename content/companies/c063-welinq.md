Welinq is a Paris company spun out of Sorbonne Université / Laboratoire Kastler-Brossel in 2022 by Tom Darras, Julien Laurat, Eleni Diamanti and Jean Lautier-Gaud. It develops high-performance quantum memories and a full-stack quantum-link solution to connect quantum processing units.
