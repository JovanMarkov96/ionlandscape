Qblox is a Delft, Netherlands company founded in 2019 as a spin-out of QuTech / TU Delft by Niels Bultink (CEO) and Jules van Oven. It builds the control electronics that operate quantum computers — the systems that send precisely timed microwave and baseband signals to qubits and read out their state.

Its flagship product, the Cluster, is a scalable and modular quantum control stack that integrates qubit control and readout in one system and grows with the size of the processor. Designed for low latency and tight synchronisation, it supports the fast feedback needed for error correction and works across a wide range of qubit platforms — superconducting, spin, NV-centre and trapped-ion — which has helped Qblox build a customer base of more than one hundred research groups and companies.

Qblox secured a EUR 4.8 million seed grant from the European Innovation Council and subsequently a roughly $26 million (EUR 24.2 million) Series A led by Quantonation and the Invest-NL Deep Tech Fund, with QDNL Participations and the EIC, to advance its control stack toward industrial-scale quantum computing.
