Quantum Source Labs, located in Ness Ziona, Israel, approaches scalable quantum computing by addressing the fundamental inefficiencies of pure photonic architectures. While photons are superb carriers of quantum information for computation and networking without significant decoherence, interacting them deterministically (for two-qubit logic gates) is notoriously difficult. 

To solve this, Quantum Source utilizes a hybrid architecture integrating single atoms with photonic circuits inside optical cavities (Cavity-QED). The atom acts as a deterministic, nonlinear medium mediating interactions between flying photonic qubits. This enables highly efficient error-resistant entangling gates and circumvents the massive resource overhead typical of linear optical quantum computing (LOQC). By leveraging mature semiconductor manufacturing paired with these unique photon-atom elements, the company aims for practical fault-tolerant capability scalable to millions of qubits.
