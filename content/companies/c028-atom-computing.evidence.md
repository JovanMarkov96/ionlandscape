## founding
- **source**: Source 1 (Wikipedia), Source 4 (Moor Insights)
- **note**: Founded 2018 by Ben Bloom and Jonathan King in Berkeley, CA. Bloom had previously been a senior quantum engineer at Rigetti Quantum Computing. $5M seed funding from Venrock in May 2018.

## acquisition_status
- **source**: Sources 6, 7, 8
- **note**: NOT acquired by Microsoft. The Microsoft relationship is a commercial technology partnership. Atom Computing remains an independent, privately held company. Microsoft has integrated Atom's hardware into the Azure Quantum platform and co-designed quantum systems, but there is no public record of an acquisition or equity investment by Microsoft.

## funding_note
- **note**: Total raised stated as ~$101M across multiple sources (Crunchbase, Quantum Insider, Tracxn). The EIFO ($10.2M) and PensionDanmark ($10M) rounds in 2024 are classified as strategic investments/Series B extensions rather than a new Series C. No formal Series C has been announced. The discrepancy between $101M and larger figures (e.g., PitchBook $272M) is unexplained by public sources; the $101M figure is used as the conservative, cross-verified total.

## technology
- **source**: Source 2 (AC1000 product page), Source 5 (1,180-qubit announcement)
- **note**: Uses nuclear-spin qubits in ytterbium-171 (Yb-171) atoms. Coherence times demonstrated at 40 seconds — among the longest for any qubit technology. All-to-all connectivity due to ability to physically rearrange atoms in the optical tweezer array. Supports both digital (gate-based) and analog modes.

## ceo_history
- **source**: Source 3, Source 8
- **note**: Ben Bloom was founding CEO (2018–2021). Rob Hays appointed CEO July 2021 (Series A). Hays stepped down July 2024; Ben Bloom returned as CEO.

## microsoft_partnership_scope
- **source**: Sources 6, 7, 12
- **note**: Partnership announced September 10, 2024. Microsoft contributes qubit-virtualization (error-correction) software stack; Atom Computing contributes the physical hardware (1,200+ qubit neutral-atom system). The combined system is offered via Azure Quantum. Milestone: 24 entangled logical qubits (November 2024, largest on record). On-premises system with 50 logical qubits launched early 2025. "Magne" co-build for Denmark anticipated 2026–2027.
