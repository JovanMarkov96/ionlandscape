JoS QUANTUM is a Frankfurt, Germany software company founded in 2018 that develops quantum and quantum-inspired algorithms for the financial sector and adjacent industries. Founded by Niklas Hegemann and Markus Braun and based at Frankfurt's TechQuartier, it focuses on the computational problems that matter most to banks, insurers and energy companies: risk analysis, simulation and optimisation.

The company combines algorithm development with practical engagement: alongside its quantum algorithms it offers quantum-readiness assessments, data-driven business intelligence and decision-support tooling, helping financial institutions understand where quantum computing can deliver value and prepare to adopt it. Its positioning as "algorithms made in Germany" reflects a focus on regulated, enterprise-grade use cases rather than hardware.
