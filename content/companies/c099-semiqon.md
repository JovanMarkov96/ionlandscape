SemiQon is an Espoo, Finland quantum-hardware company developing silicon-based quantum processors using standard semiconductor manufacturing. It spun out of VTT, one of Europe's largest research and technology organisations, in 2023, and is led by co-founder and CEO Himadri Majumdar with co-founders Janne Lehtinen (CSO), Mika Prunnila (CRO) and Markku Kainlauri (fabrication lead). The company operates from the Micronova nano- and microtechnology research facility.

SemiQon's thesis is that quantum computing will scale most affordably if it rides the existing CMOS semiconductor supply chain rather than building entirely new manufacturing. It develops silicon spin qubits that are CMOS-compatible, and — crucially — cryogenic transistors designed to operate efficiently at 1 kelvin and below, tackling the control-electronics bottleneck of placing classical control close to the qubits. In 2024 it announced these cryogenic transistors, and it has since tested and shipped a silicon-based 4-qubit chip.

The company's work has drawn external recognition: in October 2025, SemiQon and VTT won first prize in the European Association of Research and Technology Organisations (EARTO) "Impact Expected" Innovation Prize category for their cryogenic CMOS chip. SemiQon positions its technology as a route toward the affordable, scalable, and sustainable quantum computers of the "million-qubit era."
