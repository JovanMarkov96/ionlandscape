Nu Quantum is a Cambridge, UK company spun out of the University of Cambridge Cavendish Laboratory in 2018 by Carmen Palacios-Berraquero. It develops photonic quantum-networking hardware to link quantum processors and build scalable, distributed quantum computing systems.
