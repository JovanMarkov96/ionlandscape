Nu Quantum is a Cambridge, UK company founded in 2018 as a spin-out of the University of Cambridge's Cavendish Laboratory by quantum physicist Dr Carmen Palacios-Berraquero. It builds the networking layer for quantum computing — the hardware needed to connect many quantum processors together so their combined power can scale, much as classical data centres network thousands of servers.

When the company started, few were treating networked or distributed quantum computing as a serious strategy for scaling; Nu Quantum bet that it would become one of the industry's most urgent problems. Its technology, centred on a Quantum Networking Unit, distributes entanglement between quantum processing units in real time, allowing multiple quantum computers to act as a larger, more capable whole.

After an GBP 8.5 million pre-Series A from investors including Amadeus Capital Partners, IQ Capital and the National Security Strategic Investment Fund, Nu Quantum closed an oversubscribed $60 million Series A in December 2025 — described as the largest financing round ever raised by a pure-play quantum-networking company and the largest quantum Series A in the UK. In early 2026 it announced a Spanish subsidiary and quantum-networking hub backed by a EUR 9.75 million investment from the Spanish government.
