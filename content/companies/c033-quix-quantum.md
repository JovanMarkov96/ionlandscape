  differentiators:
  - Builds photonic quantum processors on the ultra-low-loss TriPleX silicon nitride
    (Si3N4/SiO2) waveguide platform, originally developed at the University of Twente
    and industrialised by LioniX International, enabling record-large, fully reconfigurable
    multimode interferometers with best-in-class optical loss (2.9 dB averaged across
    20 modes)
  - Room-temperature, data-centre-compatible operation; photonic chips require no
    cryogenic cooling, reducing energy consumption and allowing direct deployment alongside
    classical HPC hardware
  - Fabless model; core IP and design owned by QuiX Quantum, manufacturing outsourced
    to LioniX International; enables rapid scaling without capital-intensive fab investment
  - First company globally to sell Universal Quantum Computers based on photonics;
    first commercial sale to DLR (German Aerospace Center) in September 2022, a
    €14M contract for 8-qumode and 64-qumode systems
  - Demonstrated world-record 20-mode universal photonic processor (Quantum, 2023)
    with 97.4% amplitude fidelity and 98% quantum interference visibility; Qmode
    project targets >50 input/output modes
  elevator_pitch: QuiX Quantum (founded 2019, Enschede; University of Twente spin-out)
    designs and sells photonic quantum processors and full-stack universal quantum
    computers based on ultra-low-loss silicon nitride (TriPleX) waveguides. Its room-temperature,
    data-centre-compatible systems are Europe's first commercially sold universal photonic
    quantum computers. Flagship milestones include the world-record 20-mode processor
    (2022), a €14M DLR contract (2022), and a €15M Series A (2025). With offices
    in Enschede, Amsterdam, Ulm, and Stuttgart and 35+ employees, QuiX is the European
    market leader for photonic quantum computing hardware.
