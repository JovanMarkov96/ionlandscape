Quobly is a Grenoble, France company (founded 2022 as Siquance), spun out of CEA-Leti and CNRS, building fault-tolerant silicon-spin-qubit processors co-designed with STMicroelectronics. It raised a €115M Series A in June 2026 to industrialise its technology.
