Phasecraft is a quantum-algorithms company based in London and Bristol, founded in 2019 as a spin-out of University College London and the University of Bristol. Its founders are Professor Toby Cubitt and Professor John Morton of UCL and Professor Ashley Montanaro of the University of Bristol — among the UK's leading quantum-information theorists.

The company builds the mathematical foundations needed to make quantum computing useful, designing highly efficient algorithms that can extract value from today's noisy, near-term hardware as well as future machines. Its work concentrates on simulating quantum materials and molecules — problems where quantum computers are expected to have a decisive advantage — alongside optimisation. Phasecraft is also a competitor in the $5 million XPRIZE Quantum Applications competition backed by Google.

Phasecraft raised a GBP 13 million Series A in 2023 led by Playground Global, and in 2025 raised a further $34 million co-led by Plural, returning investor Playground Global and Novo Holdings' Quantum Fund — the latter's first direct investment in quantum software — to push its algorithms closer to solving real-world challenges.
