Phasecraft is a UK quantum-algorithms company (London and Bristol) spun out of UCL and the University of Bristol in 2019 by Toby Cubitt, Ashley Montanaro and John Morton. It focuses on algorithms that extract useful results from today's noisy quantum processors, particularly for materials and chemistry simulation.
