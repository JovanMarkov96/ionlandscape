## coaxmon_architecture
- **source**: Source 2 (oqc.tech/tech)
- **quote**: "In a Coaxmon, the qubit sits on one side of a sapphire substrate while the readout resonator and control wiring interface from the opposite side, with a via through the substrate to couple them — eliminating the crowded in-plane wiring of conventional 2D transmons and reducing cross-talk."
