Pixel Photonics is a Münster, Germany company founded in 2021 as a spin-off of the University of Münster, building advanced single-photon detectors. Its five founders include CEO Nicolai Walter and CTO Dr Wladick Hartmann, and the company commercialises waveguide-integrated superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors (WI-SNSPDs) through its proprietary ARCTIC platform.

Single-photon detectors are an essential component of photonic quantum technologies. Pixel Photonics' distinguishing approach is to integrate the detector directly onto a photonic waveguide, which makes it well suited to the scalable, on-chip integration that future photonic quantum computers and quantum-communication systems will require. Its detectors target applications across quantum computing, quantum key distribution, medical diagnostics, defence and advanced microscopy.

The company has been supported by Germany's Federal Agency for Disruptive Innovation (SPRIND), including a EUR 1 million grant for multi-mode detection, and in 2026 raised EUR 13.5 million — a EUR 5 million seed round led by Futury Capital plus EUR 8.5 million from the European Innovation Council Accelerator — to accelerate its move from lab to market.
