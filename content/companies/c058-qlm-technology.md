QLM Technology is a Bristol, UK company founded in 2017 by researchers from the University of Bristol, developing emissions-measurement technology based on quantum photonics. Its core product is a patented Quantum Gas Lidar that combines single-photon detection with tunable telecom lasers and lidar ranging.

The result is an instrument that can produce detailed, 360-degree, range-resolved images of equipment up to around 200 metres away and detect and quantify any associated methane emissions with high accuracy. Because the system images in 3D and runs continuously, it transforms gas-leak detection from periodic manual surveys into automated, always-on monitoring — important for oil-and-gas operators and others under growing pressure to find and cut methane emissions.

QLM offers its Quantum Gas Lidar hardware together with the QLM Cloud analytics platform as a software-as-a-service, letting users set custom alerts and generate reports when emissions exceed chosen thresholds. The company raised around GBP 3 million in seed funding to commercialise the technology.
