## Notes
- **Website domain**: The old domain `orca.co.uk` now redirects elsewhere; the canonical company website is `orcacomputing.com`.
- **Founders**: Pre-seed announcement (Source 3) names five co-founders — Murray, Walmsley, Nunn, Cristina Escoda, and Kris Kaczmarek. The current "About Us" and "Team" pages foreground Murray, Walmsley, and Nunn as the three main founders; Escoda and Kaczmarek appear to have departed the company. The profile lists the three publicly acknowledged co-founders.
- **Ian Walmsley role**: Walmsley was Co-founder and Chair per early materials. The team page lists Erik Langaker as current Board Chairman, with Walmsley's current title there as "Co-founder and Chairman" (likely Chairman Emeritus / Founding Chairman). His day-to-day role is as Director of the Oxford Quantum Institute (per his person profile 181-ian-walmsley). The profile records him with role "Co-founder and Chairman" in the founders block per instruction.
- **"Steve Murray" clarification**: The user brief mentioned "Steve Murray (CEO)?" — the actual CEO name is **Richard Murray** (not Steve). No person named Steve Murray is associated with ORCA Computing.
- **Funding total**: Confirmed VC funding is £2.9M pre-seed ($3.7M) + $15M Series A = ~$18.7M VC (~$19M rounded). The £11.6M Innovate UK grant (~$14.5M) is listed separately as a grant round. Crunchbase reports $20.7M total over 7 rounds, and $25.4M per other aggregators; the discrepancy likely reflects undisclosed Innovate UK sub-grants or bridge notes. The profile uses $19M for the VC total and lists the Innovate UK grant separately.
- **PT-2 qumode count**: The NQCC product page and QCR coverage both cite "40 qumodes at industry-relevant clock cycles." The "~90 qubits" figure cited by some secondary sources (e.g., DCD) likely refers to a different counting convention or preliminary estimate; the 40 qumodes from the ORCA product page is used here as the primary specification.
- **GXC acquisition**: ORCA acquired the Integrated Photonics Division of GXC (Austin, TX) on January 30, 2024; terms undisclosed. This explains the Austin office and Thien-An Nguyen's CTO role (he was formerly VP R&D Strategy at GXC's photonics division).
- **HSBC**: A search for ORCA–HSBC partnership returned no concrete evidence of a formal HSBC partnership. HSBC appears in broader quantum industry context (wanting quantum readiness) but not in any ORCA-specific press release or deployment announcement.
- **Rolls-Royce**: Rolls-Royce (specifically Rolls-Royce Submarines) used ORCA PT-1 hardware through Digital Catapult's QTAP programme (early 2024), not a direct bilateral partnership. No separate Rolls-Royce–ORCA partnership agreement was found.
- **`modality = hardware`**: ORCA provides full-stack photonic quantum hardware (PT Series) with bundled software (SDKs, Python/PyTorch/CUDA-Q integration), but the primary product is hardware. The `hardware` classification is appropriate; no standalone software product is sold.
- **`total_raised_usd`**: Set to $19,000,000 (VC rounds only: $3.7M + $15M). The Innovate UK grant (~$14.5M) is recorded as a separate `grant` round. Combined with the grant the total is ~$33.5M; the field is intentionally kept to private equity only for comparability.
