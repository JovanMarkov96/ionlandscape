## founding
- **source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Element_Six
- **note**: Founded 1946 as Industrial Distributors Ltd., a De Beers entity under Sir Ernest Oppenheimer focused on industrial uses of natural diamond. De Beers Diamond Research Laboratory synthesised its first synthetic diamond in 1958 using HPHT process. Shannon (Ireland) plant opened May 1963. Company renamed Element Six in 2002. CVD technology breakthrough in 1992.

## quantum_products_dnv
- **source**: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210920005612/en/Element-Six-Launches-DNV-B14-a-New-Grade-of-Diamond-for-Quantum-Technology-Applications
- **note**: DNV-B1 — general-purpose quantum grade; controlled NV-centre density; SPIE Prism Award 2021 winner. DNV-B14 — >10x NV density vs DNV-B1; uniform density across substrate; sensing + maser + gyroscope applications. Both are CVD single-crystal diamonds with engineered NV-centre populations; not gem-quality but quantum-functional materials.
- **key spec**: NV-centre density is the critical differentiator between grades. Higher density = stronger ensemble signal for sensing. Lower density = longer coherence times per spin for qubit applications.

## quantum_networking_strategy
- **source**: https://www.e6.com/about/news/aws_element_six_quantum_collaboration

IonQ collaboration (September 2025) achieved foundry-compatible diamond films, enabling mass production of diamond-based quantum memories and photonic interconnects.

## lightsynq_investment
- **source**: https://www.e6.com/en/about/news/element-six-investment-lightsynq

E6 described the acquisition as a "strategic milestone" in quantum networking and computing — the investment evidences E6's role as both a materials supplier and strategic venture participant in the quantum ecosystem.

## ionq_collaboration_2025
- **source**: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250904390259/en/IonQ-Breakthrough-in-Synthetic-Diamond-Materials-Accelerates-Quantum-Networking-Scale-and-Production
- **note**: Announced September 4, 2025. E6 and IonQ jointly produced quantum-grade synthetic diamond films compatible with standard semiconductor foundries. Technique: bonding diamond films onto silicon and silicon nitride substrates. Enables fabrication of quantum memory nodes and photonic interconnects using existing fab infrastructure. IonQ described this as a key enabler for their 2030 roadmap to 2 million qubits.

## walsworth_partnership
