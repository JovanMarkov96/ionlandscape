Zurich Instruments is a Zurich, Switzerland company founded in April 2008 as a spin-off of ETH Zurich by Sadik Hafizovic (CEO), Flavio Heer and Beat Hofstetter. It began in precision measurement — its first products were high-performance lock-in amplifiers — and has become a leading supplier of the control and measurement electronics used to operate quantum computers.

Its Quantum Computing Control System (QCCS), first commercialised in 2018, integrates a multi-channel arbitrary waveform generator, a quantum analyzer for readout and a programmable system controller into a coordinated stack for controlling qubits. Later generations added direct microwave signal generation at superconducting-qubit operating frequencies up to around 8.5 GHz and scalability up to hundreds of channels, making the QCCS a widely used backbone for superconducting and spin-qubit experiments.

In June 2021, Zurich Instruments was wholly acquired by the German test-and-measurement group Rohde & Schwarz, which retained its development, production, marketing and sales functions and strengthened its position in the quantum-technology market.
