Kipu Quantum is a German quantum-software company, based in Berlin and Karlsruhe and founded in 2021 by quantum-computing pioneer Enrique Solano (chief visionary officer), Tobias Grab (chief strategy officer) and Daniel Volz (CEO). It develops the algorithms needed to make today's quantum computers useful for industry.

Its distinctive approach is to design algorithms that are specific to both an application and the particular quantum chip they will run on. By compressing circuits — reducing their depth and the number of qubits and operations required — Kipu aims to extract industrial value from existing, noisy hardware rather than waiting for large fault-tolerant machines. Its target customers come from the pharmaceutical, chemical, logistics and financial sectors, and its algorithms are designed to be hardware-agnostic across trapped-ion, neutral-atom and superconducting platforms.

Kipu Quantum emerged from stealth in 2022 with a EUR 3 million round and joined deep-tech investor Quantonation's portfolio, then in December 2023 announced a EUR 10.5 million seed round led by HV Capital and DTCF.
