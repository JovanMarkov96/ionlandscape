Multiverse Computing is a San Sebastián (Donostia), Spain software company founded in 2019 by Enrique Lizaso (CEO), Román Orús (chief scientific officer), Alfonso Rubio-Manzanares and Sam Mugel (CTO). It applies quantum and quantum-inspired algorithms — particularly tensor networks, an approach pioneered by Orús — to hard computational problems.

The company first made its name in finance and optimisation with its Singularity platform, but its breakthrough product is CompactifAI, which uses tensor-network methods to compress large language models by up to 95% while largely preserving their performance. Because the cost of running modern AI is dominated by model size, this has the potential to radically lower the cost and energy of deploying large models — a capability that has attracted strong commercial interest.

In June 2025 Multiverse Computing raised a EUR 189 million (~$215 million) Series B led by Bullhound Capital, with backers including HP Tech Ventures, Forgepoint Capital, CDP Venture Capital, Santander Climate VC, Quantonation and Toshiba — one of the largest funding rounds for a European quantum-software company.
