  differentiators:
  - Only commercial quantum computing platform that operates at room temperature and
    atmospheric pressure using nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in synthetic diamond,
    eliminating the need for dilution refrigerators, vacuum systems, or cryogenics
  - Smart Diamond architecture integrates NV qubits with electronic, magnetic, and
    optical interfaces in a 19-inch rack-mountable form factor, enabling deployment
    in standard data centres, HPC facilities, and edge environments
  - Vertically integrated technology stack spanning diamond materials growth, device
    fabrication, quantum control electronics, and software, with Australia's first
    quantum diamond foundry under development
  - Targets edge quantum computing use cases (satellites, vehicles, field deployments)
    where cryogenic systems are impractical — a differentiated market segment not
    addressed by superconducting or trapped-ion platforms
