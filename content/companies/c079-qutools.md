qutools is a Munich, Germany company that develops instrumentation for experimental quantum optics and quantum technologies. Founded by physicists from the Munich quantum-optics community, it has long supplied the practical tools that quantum experiments depend on.

Its product range spans precise time-to-digital converters and time taggers — its quTAG family — used for time-correlated single-photon counting; single-photon and entangled-photon source systems; and quantum key distribution demonstrators. qutools is also widely known for its quantum-education kits, such as the quED entangled-photon system, which let universities and schools teach the foundations of quantum mechanics with real hardware.

By serving research groups, industrial R&D and education alike, qutools occupies an important enabling niche in the quantum ecosystem, providing the measurement instruments and demonstrators that underpin work in quantum communication, sensing and fundamental physics.
