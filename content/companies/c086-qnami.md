Qnami is a Basel, Switzerland company founded in 2017 as a spin-off of the University of Basel, where co-founder and chief scientific officer Patrick Maletinsky leads the quantum-sensing group; co-founder Mathieu Munsch is CEO. The company builds quantum sensors and microscopes based on nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centres in diamond.

An NV centre is an engineered "flaw" in the diamond lattice that hosts a single electron spin, which behaves as an extraordinarily sensitive, atom-scale magnetometer. Qnami's flagship product, ProteusQ, is a scanning probe microscope that places such a spin at the tip of a patented Quantilever diamond probe and scans it across a surface, producing non-invasive images of magnetic fields at the nanoscale. Launched in 2019 as the first commercial scanning NV magnetometer, it is used to study spintronics, two-dimensional materials, magnetic memories and semiconductor devices.

Qnami closed a CHF 4 million (~$4.4 million) Series A led by Runa Capital and SIT Capital, building on early recognition such as winning the Venture Kick startup competition, and has become one of Europe's notable quantum-sensing companies.
