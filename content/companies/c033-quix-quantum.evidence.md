## Notes
- **Founders vs academic co-founders**: The legal founders on founding documents are Hans van den Vlekkert and Jelmer Renema (Source 2, Inside Quantum Technology). The COPS group announcement (Source 1) names Ad Lagendijk, Willem Vos, Pepijn Pinkse, Jelmer Renema (COPS), and Klaus Boller (LPNO) as the scientists who "founded" QuiX — this likely reflects academic co-founders and initial scientific advisors rather than legal company founders. The profile lists van den Vlekkert and Renema as primary co-founders, and Pinkse and Boller as academic co-founders, consistent with both sources.
- **TriPleX technology**: QuiX uses silicon nitride (Si3N4/SiO2) waveguide technology branded TriPleX, originally developed at University of Twente and commercialised by LioniX International (also based in Enschede). QuiX is fabless — chips are manufactured by LioniX; QuiX owns the design IP and integration.
- **56-mode processor**: The known task brief mentioned a "56-mode processor." Research found the Qmode project targets ">50 input/output modes" but no specific 56-mode product announcement was confirmed. The profile uses the verified figures: 12-mode (2020), 20-mode (2022, peer-reviewed 2023), and >50-mode (Qmode project, in development).
- **Funding total**: Confirmed VC rounds: €5.5M seed (2022) + €15M Series A (2025) ≈ €20.5M ≈ $22.6M USD. The 2020 seed (FORWARD.one/Oost NL) amount was undisclosed. EIC Accelerator blended finance (up to €17.5M) is listed separately as grant/equity and not added to the VC total. Profile sets `total_raised_usd: 22600000` representing confirmed VC rounds only.
- **Employees**: EIC Scaling Club interview (eicscalingclub.eu) states 35 employees as of 2024/2025. The DLR contract press release (2022) and Hengesbach appointment (2022) mentioned 15 employees at that time, consistent with rapid growth.
- **DLR contract "8-qubit and 64-qubit"**: Reporting used both "qubit" and "qumode" terminology inconsistently. The correct term for QuiX's photonic platform is qumodes (optical modes). The profile uses "qumode" throughout for accuracy.
- **Amsterdam office**: Opened in 2022 at Startup Village, Amsterdam Science Park 608; near QuSoft, University of Amsterdam, and CWI. Confirms ongoing academic ties. Van den Vlekkert's connection to LioniX International is relevant — LioniX co-founder and serial photonics entrepreneur, he brought industrial manufacturing expertise to the academic founders.
