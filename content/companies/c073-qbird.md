Q*Bird is a Delft, Netherlands company developing quantum-secure communication technology, founded in 2022 as a spin-off of QuTech and TU Delft by Joshua Slater (CEO), Ingrid Romijn and Remon Berrevoets. It addresses the threat that future quantum computers pose to today's encryption by distributing cryptographic keys whose security is guaranteed by the laws of physics.

The company's distinguishing technology is measurement-device-independent quantum key distribution (MDI-QKD). Unlike conventional point-to-point QKD, MDI-QKD removes detector-side vulnerabilities and places the measurement at a central, untrusted node — which means many users can share quantum-secure connections over a single network rather than needing a dedicated link for every pair. Q*Bird's commercial product, Falqon, implements this scalable architecture, and it has launched a Quantum Domain Controller for managing quantum-secured networks. Its technology has been tested in collaboration with industry leaders including Cisco, Juniper and the fibre operator Eurofiber.

Q*Bird has been backed by European deep-tech funding, including a EUR 1 million grant with Eurofiber and, in 2025, EUR 7.5 million (~$8.75 million) in blended finance from the European Innovation Council's EIC Accelerator to scale its quantum-secure networking technology.
