ID Quantique (IDQ) is a Geneva, Switzerland company founded in October 2001 as a spin-off of the University of Geneva by Grégoire Ribordy (CEO), Nicolas Gisin, Olivier Guinnard and Hugo Zbinden. One of the longest-established companies in the field, it has pioneered the commercial application of quantum technologies for more than two decades.

IDQ is a world leader in quantum-safe security. Its quantum key distribution (QKD) systems exchange encryption keys whose security is guaranteed by the laws of physics, protecting network communications for financial institutions, enterprises and governments. Alongside QKD, it makes quantum random number generators — including chip-scale Quantis devices embedded in consumer products such as smartphones — and high-performance single-photon detectors used in quantum and scientific applications.

The company's strategic backing has reflected the growing importance of quantum security: South Korea's SK Telecom took a majority stake in 2018, accelerating IDQ's QRNG and telecom work, and in May 2025 the US trapped-ion quantum-computing leader IonQ acquired ID Quantique, folding its quantum-networking and security technology into IonQ's portfolio.
