Algorithmiq is a quantum-software company founded in Helsinki in 2020 by Professor Sabrina Maniscalco (CEO), Guillermo García-Pérez (CSO), Matteo Rossi (CTO) and Boris Sokolov, with deep roots in the physics of open quantum systems. It develops quantum algorithms aimed at the life sciences, particularly quantum chemistry and drug discovery.

The company's central focus is extracting useful results from today's noisy quantum hardware. It controls the full computational pipeline — from quantum-state initialization through its error-mitigation techniques and measurement to chemistry-specific optimisation — and has developed error-mitigation methods designed to make near-term quantum processors deliver meaningful answers despite their imperfections. Its applications include quantum-enabled discovery of light-sensitive cancer drugs, for which it won a EUR 1.7 million prize.

Algorithmiq has raised around EUR 36 million in total, including a $4 million seed round backed by prominent angels, a EUR 13.7 million Series A led by Inventure, and a EUR 18 million round in 2026 led by United Ventures and Italy's Cassa Depositi e Prestiti — at which point the company moved its global headquarters from Helsinki to Milan while retaining its Finnish roots.
