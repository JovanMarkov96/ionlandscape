Cerca Magnetics is a Nottingham, UK company founded in 2020 as a spin-out from the University of Nottingham's Sir Peter Mansfield Imaging Centre, commercialising a new generation of brain-imaging technology based on quantum sensing. It builds wearable magnetoencephalography (MEG) systems that use optically pumped magnetometers (OPMs).

Magnetoencephalography detects the tiny magnetic fields produced by neural activity. Conventional MEG relies on cryogenically cooled SQUID sensors fixed in a bulky helmet, which requires subjects to keep still. Cerca's OPM-MEG instead uses small, lightweight quantum-enabled sensors that can be mounted in a wearable helmet and image brain activity even while the wearer moves — a significant advantage for studying children, movement disorders and naturalistic behaviour.

The company's systems are used in research and clinical applications including epilepsy localisation, dementia studies and developmental neuroscience, building on imaging IP developed at the University of Nottingham's School of Physics and Astronomy.
