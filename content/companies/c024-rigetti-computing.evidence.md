## founding
- **source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigetti_Computing
- **quote**: "Rigetti Computing was established in 2013 by Chad Rigetti, a physicist formerly at IBM who studied under Michel Devoret." Founded July 8, 2013, Berkeley, CA. Y Combinator "spaceshot" cohort 2014.

## fab1
- **source**: https://spectrum.ieee.org/rigetti-launches-fullstack-quantum-computing-service-and-quantum-ic-fab
- **note**: Fab-1 in Fremont CA commissioned ~2016; described as the first dedicated quantum IC fabrication facility operated by a private company; enables ~2-week design-to-chip cycles.

## forest_quil_launch
- **source**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigetti_Computing
- **note**: Forest 1.0 released June 2017; Quil (Quantum Instruction Language) specification first published as arXiv:1608.03355 (2016). Platform enabled 36-qubit cloud simulation by June 2017.

## qcs_launch
- **source**: https://techcrunch.com/2019/01/30/rigetti-launches-the-public-beta-of-its-quantum-cloud-services/
- **quote**: "Rigetti Computing announced the public beta of its Quantum Cloud Services (QCS) today." January 30, 2019. QCS = quantum-first cloud, hybrid classical-quantum, sub-1 ms latency. Internal launch was 2018 per Wikipedia.

- **source**: https://www.rigetti.com/what-we-build
- **note**: Cepheus-1-108Q (108 qubits; 99.9% 1Q fidelity; 99.1% 2Q CZ fidelity) deployed April 7, 2026, on QCS and Amazon Braket. Chiplet-based multi-die architecture.
