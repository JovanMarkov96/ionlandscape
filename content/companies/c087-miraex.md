Miraex is a Lausanne, Switzerland company founded in 2019 at the EPFL Innovation Park, developing photonic-integrated-circuit technology for both industrial sensing and quantum applications. The idea originated when founder Clément Javerzac-Galy was researching microwave-to-optical transduction at EPFL; the company was established with Nicolas Abelé (CEO) and Karel Dumon.

Miraex pursues two connected directions on a single photonic platform. On the sensing side, it builds optical-fibre sensors that operate reliably in harsh industrial environments — high temperatures and strong electromagnetic interference — for condition monitoring and predictive maintenance. On the quantum side, it develops microwave-to-optical quantum transduction, the technology needed to convert the microwave signals of quantum processors into optical photons so they can be linked over fibre, enabling distributed quantum computing and entangled quantum networks.

The company is a member of the IBM Quantum Network and has been recognised among Switzerland's notable deep-tech ventures for its work bridging photonic sensing and quantum technology.
