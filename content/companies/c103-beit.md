Beit is a Kraków, Poland quantum-software company founded in 2016 by former Google engineers Paulina Mazurek (CEO), Wojciech Burkot (chief physics officer) and Witold Jarnicki (chief mathematics officer). One of Poland's pioneering quantum-computing companies, it develops proprietary quantum algorithms aimed at solving hard problems on the limited hardware available today.

Beit's research has concentrated on quantum search and related NP-hard problems, designing families of algorithms specifically constructed for NISQ (noisy intermediate-scale quantum) machines — promising better results at the qubit counts and circuit depths currently achievable than generic textbook approaches. More recently the company has applied its quantum-compressed methods to computational drug discovery, targeting small-molecule and covalent-binding problems in chemistry.

The company has raised around $1.4 million in seed funding from a consortium led by Kindred Capital, alongside roughly $2.6 million in EU grants. Its co-founder and chief physics officer Wojciech Burkot, a respected physicist and technology leader, passed away in 2024.
