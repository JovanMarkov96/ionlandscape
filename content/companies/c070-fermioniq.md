Fermioniq is an Amsterdam software company founded in 2021 as a spin-out of the University of Amsterdam, the Dutch quantum-software centre QuSoft and the national research institute for mathematics and computer science CWI. Its founders include CEO Jörgen Sandig and Professor Harry Buhrman, a leading figure in quantum complexity theory, alongside Ido Niesen, Chris Cade and Boris Ponsioen.

The company develops tensor-network emulation software — its Ava engine — that simulates the behaviour of quantum circuits on classical CPU and GPU hardware. Because many quantum circuits can be approximated efficiently with tensor networks, Ava lets users run, test and benchmark quantum applications today, bridging the gap between existing high-performance computing and still-maturing quantum hardware. In 2024 Ava became the first third-party tensor-network emulation product available on NVIDIA's CUDA-Q platform.

Fermioniq raised a seed round from investors including AMC Ventures, Innovatiefonds Noord-Holland, QDNL Participations and UvA Ventures Holding, and counts quantum-computing pioneer Chad Rigetti among its board members.
