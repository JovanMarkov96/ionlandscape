HQS Quantum Simulations is a Karlsruhe, Germany software company founded in 2017 as a spin-off of the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology by Iris Schwenk, Sebastian Zanker, Jan Reiner and Michael Marthaler, who had worked together at KIT before setting out to bridge academia and industry.

The company develops software that predicts the properties of molecules and materials at the quantum level. Its tools combine quantum-inspired methods that run on today's classical high-performance computers with algorithms designed to exploit quantum hardware as it matures, serving customers in specialty chemicals, pharmaceuticals and materials. Its HQSpectrum platform, for example, predicts and analyses Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) spectra, reducing manual effort and speeding up molecular characterisation.

HQS has raised around $16 million, including a EUR 2.3 million seed round in 2019 led by UVC Partners, High-Tech Gründerfonds and btov Partners, along with European Innovation Council grants, and it has established collaborations with major industrial companies including BASF, Bosch and Merck.
