VeriQloud is a Paris software company founded in 2017 that focuses on the software and protocol layer of quantum communication networks, as well as quantum-safe security applications. It was founded by Marc Kaplan, Elham Kashefi and Josh Nunn, bringing together expertise from the quantum-information research community.

Rather than building photonic hardware itself, VeriQloud develops the architectures and software that let organisations use quantum networks securely. Its Qline product is a quantum-communication access architecture that connects end-users into quantum networks, and its Qasmat product is quantum-safe software for secure distributed cloud storage. Together these target the practical deployment of quantum-secure communication and the protection of data against future quantum attacks.

The company raised EUR 1.9 million from deep-tech investor Quantonation and WinEquity in 2023 to advance its quantum-network software, and participates in European quantum-internet initiatives.
