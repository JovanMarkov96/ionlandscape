Single Quantum is a Delft, Netherlands company founded in 2012 by Sander Dorenbos (CEO) and Val Zwiller (CSO), spun out of TU Delft. It is one of the longest-established quantum-photonics hardware companies, manufacturing superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors (SNSPDs).

SNSPDs are among the best single-photon detectors available, combining near-unity detection efficiency, very high detection rates and excellent timing resolution. These properties make them indispensable across a wide range of quantum and photonic applications: quantum key distribution and quantum networks, photonic quantum computing, LIDAR, deep-space optical communication, and fundamental quantum-optics experiments. Single Quantum delivers complete detector systems, including the cryogenics needed to operate the superconducting nanowires.

With co-founder Val Zwiller also a professor of applied physics at the KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm, the company maintains close ties to cutting-edge detector research while supplying systems to laboratories and companies worldwide.
