## sycamore_supremacy
- **source**: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1666-5
- **quote**: "Quantum supremacy using a programmable superconducting processor" — Arute et al., *Nature* 574, 505–510 (October 23, 2019). 53-qubit Sycamore completed random circuit sampling in 200 seconds; estimated 10,000 years on classical Summit supercomputer.

## bristlecone
- **source**: https://research.google/blog/a-preview-of-bristlecone-googles-new-quantum-processor/
- **quote**: Google unveiled Bristlecone, a 72-qubit superconducting processor, at the American Physical Society meeting on March 5, 2018.

## cirq_release
- **source**: https://research.google/blog/announcing-cirq-an-open-source-framework-for-nisq-algorithms/
- **quote**: Cirq public alpha announced July 18, 2018 at the International Workshop on Quantum Software and Quantum Machine Learning; stable Cirq 1.0 released July 2022.

## error_correction_milestone2
- **source**: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05434-1
- **quote**: "Suppressing quantum errors by scaling a surface code logical qubit" — *Nature* (Feb 22, 2023). First demonstration that increasing surface code size (17 → 49 physical qubits) reduced logical qubit error rate, fulfilling Milestone 2 of roadmap.

## willow_chip
- **source**: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08449-y
- **quote**: "Quantum error correction below the surface code threshold" — *Nature* (December 9, 2024). Willow (105-qubit transmon) achieved below-threshold error correction and completed RCS benchmark in 5 minutes vs. 10^25 years classically.

## quantum_echoes
- **source**: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/quantum-echoes-willow-verifiable-quantum-advantage/
- **quote**: "Our Quantum Echoes algorithm is a big step toward real-world applications for quantum computing." Announced October 22, 2025. Willow ran the OTOC (Quantum Echoes) algorithm 13,000× faster than the best classical algorithm on a world-class supercomputer; results are independently verifiable.
