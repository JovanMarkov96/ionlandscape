Quantum X Labs (Nasdaq: QXL) is a Tel Aviv–based company that brands itself as the first Israeli company for "full-spectrum" quantum technologies, spanning three pillars: quantum computing, quantum software & simulation, and quantum sensing.

Its flagship hardware effort is a neutral-atom quantum computer. In May 2026 the company launched a system with 50+ physical qubits built on proprietary laser cooling and dynamically reconfigurable optical-tweezer arrays, supporting rapid high-fidelity loading of large qubit registers, extended coherence times, and high-fidelity Rydberg-mediated two-qubit gates. The platform is paired with a patented AI-driven error-correction architecture (US Patent 12294387B2) — a deep transformer decoder using syndrome-guided decoding for low-latency feedback and real-time fault-tolerant logical-qubit operation — and the company has set a roadmap to reach thousands of physical qubits by the end of H1 2027. Beyond computing, its sensing portfolio includes a quantum gyroscope (a GPS-independent navigation solution for aerospace, defense and commercial use) and a miniaturized quantum atomic clock, while its software/simulation work targets clinical-trial data analysis, quantum cyber security, and nuclear-reactor simulation.

The company reached the public markets through a corporate transformation: Nasdaq-listed Viewbix Inc. acquired the Israeli quantum company "Quantum Israel" (now Quantum X Labs Ltd.) in March 2026 and rebranded as Quantum X Labs Inc. It is run by co-CEOs Amihay Hadad (CEO since February 2020) and Yakov Baranes (co-CEO effective May 2026, having led the Israeli quantum subsidiary from March 2025), with Prof. Nir Sharon as Chief Quantum Technology Scientist. Its scientific advisory board reflects close ties to Israeli academia, with appointments including the Weizmann Institute's Prof. Oren Raz and the Technion's Prof. Oren Kurland.
