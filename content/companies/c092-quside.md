Quside is a Barcelona, Spain company founded in 2017 as a spin-off of ICFO, the Institute of Photonic Sciences, building on more than a decade of research into quantum and photonic technologies. Its founding team includes CEO Carlos Abellán — who co-invented the quantum-randomness technology transferred from ICFO — along with Domenico Tulli (CTO), Waldimar Amaya and ICFO professors Valerio Pruneri and Morgan Mitchell.

The company designs and manufactures quantum random number generators (QRNGs), which use photonic quantum processes to produce genuinely unpredictable randomness — a foundational ingredient for strong cryptography. Quside has extended this into computation as well as security, unveiling what it describes as the world's first Randomness Processing Unit (RPU), aimed at probabilistic and high-performance computing workloads that consume large amounts of randomness.

In 2023 Quside extended its Series A to exceed EUR 10 million, with backers including TRUMPF Ventures, Bullnet Capital, the Catalan Institute of Finance, Demium Capital and TechVision Capital, alongside an EIC Accelerator grant, to internationalise its quantum technology.
