ParityQC is an Innsbruck, Austria company founded in 2019 as a spin-off of the University of Innsbruck, co-led by physicist Wolfgang Lechner and entrepreneur Magdalena Hauser as co-CEOs. It designs the architectures — the fundamental blueprints — for quantum computers, rather than building the hardware itself.

The company is built on the parity architecture (also known as the LHZ scheme) developed by Lechner together with Philipp Hauke and Peter Zoller, which reformulates hard optimisation problems so that they can be solved with only local connectivity between qubits, sidestepping a key limitation of many quantum-hardware designs. ParityQC's business is to license this ParityQC Architecture together with its ParityOS operating system; the resulting chip designs are intended to enable highly scalable, error-corrected quantum computers and efficient optimisation, and are hardware-agnostic across superconducting, neutral-atom and trapped-ion platforms.

Backed early on by prominent investors including Hermann Hauser and Herbert Gartner — with the University of Innsbruck and the Austrian Academy of Sciences also holding stakes — ParityQC has grown into a significant player in the quantum-architecture space and raised further venture funding from B&C Innovation Investments in 2024.
