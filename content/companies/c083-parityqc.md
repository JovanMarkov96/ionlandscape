ParityQC is an Innsbruck, Austria company spun out of the University of Innsbruck in 2020 by Wolfgang Lechner and Magdalena Hauser, providing architecture and software blueprints — based on the LHZ/parity approach — for optimisation and fault-tolerant quantum computers.
