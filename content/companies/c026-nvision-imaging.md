short_summary: NVision Imaging Technologies (founded 2015, Ulm, Germany) is a University
  of Ulm spin-out co-founded by quantum physicists Fedor Jelezko (113-fedor-jelezko),
  Martin Plenio, Ilai Schwartz, and Alex Retzker. The company applies quantum spin-control
  technology — originating from NV-center research on diamond surfaces — to produce
  hyperpolarized carbon-13 metabolic contrast agents for MRI. Its POLARIS platform
  uses parahydrogen-induced polarization (PHIP) to boost MRI signals by over 10,000x
  in under three minutes, enabling real-time visualization of tumour metabolism on
  standard MRI systems. By end-2025, POLARIS Preclinical was deployed at approximately
  20 centres including Memorial Sloan Kettering and Technical University of Munich.
  Having raised USD $120 million total (Series A led by Playground Global, Series
  B anchored by Abbott with EIB venture loan), NVision rebranded as NVision Quantum
  Technologies GmbH in May 2026 and announced expansion into photonic quantum computing
  (PIQC platform) for drug discovery.
