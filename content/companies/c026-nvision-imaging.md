- description: Quantum-enhanced MRI platform that uses parahydrogen-induced polarization
    (PHIP) to amplify the MRI signal of [1-13C] pyruvate and other metabolic agents
    by over 10,000x in under three minutes. Enables real-time metabolic imaging (Warburg
    effect visualization) on standard preclinical MRI systems. Specifications — dose

    H × 87 cm W × 80 cm D. Deployed at Memorial Sloan Kettering, Technical University
    of Munich, University of Cambridge, and ~20 centers globally (as of end 2025).
    Clinical version under development.
