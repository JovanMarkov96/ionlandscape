  - PennyLane open-source framework has 35,000+ active users and 200,000+ monthly

    machine-learning toolkit

    photonic quantum computing public company (Mar 2026)
  elevator_pitch: Xanadu (founded 2016, Toronto) is building fault-tolerant, room-temperature
    photonic quantum computers using continuous-variable GKP qubits in squeezed light
    and a modular, fibre-networked architecture. Its Aurora system (Nature, 2025)
    demonstrated 12 logical qubits across four photonic server racks; its cloud-accessible
    Borealis was the first photonic quantum advantage demonstration (Nature, 2022).
    Xanadu also develops PennyLane, the world's most widely used open-source quantum
    ML framework. The company went public on Nasdaq and TSX (XNDU) in March 2026 and
    is targeting a fault-tolerant quantum data centre by 2029.
