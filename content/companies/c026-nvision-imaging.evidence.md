## technology_note
- POLARIS uses parahydrogen-induced polarization (PHIP-SAH: para-hydrogen by side-arm hydrogenation) to hyperpolarize [1-13C] pyruvate. Polarization enhances 13C MRI signal by >10,000x for ~60-second imaging window (T1 decay). NV-center diamond research is the intellectual origin of the quantum spin-control methods, though the product itself is a liquid-state contrast agent system, not a diamond sensor.
- The company's core IP traces to Plenio-group diamond NV-center work (2012 onward) on polarizing nuclear spins, transferred to soluble substrates for clinical use. This is a biomimetic application of NV-center spin physics — the quantum sensing platform type, not the diamond sensor hardware itself.

## applications_note
- Application coded as `sensing_metrology` because the core commercial product (POLARIS) is a quantum-sensing-derived MRI enhancement system for biomedical measurement. Although the PIQC platform (2026) targets quantum computing for drug discovery, the founding and primary revenue-generating product is sensing-domain.
