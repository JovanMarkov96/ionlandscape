## technology_note
- NV centers in synthetic diamond provide long coherence times at room temperature; diamond's hardness prevents thermal vibrations disrupting qubit coherence (per ORNL/QB technology Q&A, September 2025).
- First-generation QPU: 5 qubits (per IEEE Spectrum); Pawsey deployment was 2-qubit (per CSIRO); QB-QDK2.0 qubit count not publicly disclosed.
- Smart Diamond architecture: layered chip combining NV qubits with electronic, magnetic, and optical interfaces; compatible with semiconductor manufacturing processes.
