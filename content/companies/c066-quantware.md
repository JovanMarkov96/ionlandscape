QuantWare is a Delft, Netherlands company founded in 2021 as a spin-out of QuTech / TU Delft by Matthijs Rijlaarsdam (CEO) and Alessandro Bruno (CTO). It operates as a foundry for superconducting quantum processors, designing, fabricating and integrating QPUs both off-the-shelf and to customer specification — an approach that dramatically lowers the barrier for others to build quantum computers. The company says it is the world's largest commercial QPU supplier by volume, having shipped to more than fifty customers across twenty countries.

At the centre of its technology is VIO, a modular quantum-processor architecture that uses 3D integration to route signals to large numbers of qubits while maximising compute-per-watt. QuantWare positions VIO as an open platform that can also scale third parties' qubit chiplets and designs, and has announced a VIO-40K variant aimed at around 10,000 qubits — roughly 100x the scale of today's typical processors. To meet demand it is building KiloFab, described as the world's largest dedicated quantum open-architecture fab, to expand production roughly twentyfold.

In May 2026 QuantWare raised a heavily oversubscribed $178 million (EUR 152 million) Series B — the largest private round raised by a dedicated quantum-processor company — with new investors including Intel Capital, IQT and ETF Partners alongside existing backers such as FORWARD.one and the Invest-NL Deep Tech Fund.
