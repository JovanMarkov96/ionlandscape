QTI — Quantum Telecommunications Italy — is a Florence-based company founded in October 2020 and described as the first Italian quantum key distribution (QKD) company. It was created by a group of entrepreneurs, scientists and researchers associated with the National Institute of Optics of Italy's National Research Council (CNR-INO), and it develops, engineers and produces industrial-grade QKD systems for ultra-secure communications.

QTI's products are reconfigurable, fully made-in-Italy QKD architectures built on proprietary, patented expertise and a European supply chain. Its portfolio includes the established Quell-X system and the newer Ekate system, which the company has showcased — for example at OFC 2026 — with a focus on improving the scalability of quantum-secured networks.

In 2021, Telsy S.p.A., the cybersecurity and cryptography competence centre of the TIM Group, acquired part of QTI's shares, giving the company a strategic industrial partner as it brings quantum-secure communication systems to telecom and government customers.
