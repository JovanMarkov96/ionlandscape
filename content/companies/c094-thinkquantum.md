ThinkQuantum is a Padova, Italy company providing complete solutions for cybersecurity and communications based on quantum technologies. It was founded in 2021 as a spin-off of the University of Padua, established together with the aerospace-optics company Officina Stellare, and draws on the university's quantum-communications research group.

The company covers the full value chain of quantum key distribution (QKD) and quantum random number generation (QRNG). Its QKD systems exchange cryptographic keys with security guaranteed by quantum physics, and — leveraging Officina Stellare's heritage in precision optics and telescopes — ThinkQuantum develops not only fibre-based links but also free-space and satellite quantum communication. Its QRNG devices supply the high-quality randomness that underpins secure cryptography.

Positioned as a fully Italian provider of quantum-secure communication hardware and systems, ThinkQuantum participates in European quantum-communication initiatives and targets customers in defence, government and critical infrastructure.
