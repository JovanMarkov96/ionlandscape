Wave Photonics is a Cambridge, UK integrated-photonics company founded in 2021 by James Lee (CEO), Matthew Anderson and Mateusz Kubica (CTO). It offers photonic intellectual property, components and design services, helping customers build photonic integrated circuits (PICs) for quantum technologies, sensing and data-centre applications.

A core part of Wave Photonics' offering is design automation and process design kits (PDKs) that make on-chip photonics easier and faster to develop, including a quantum-photonics PDK and a silicon-nitride process (SiNQ) tailored for quantum systems and single-photon emitters. The company has led Innovate UK projects to build integrated photonic components, including work aimed at trapped-ion quantum computers.

In 2024-2025 the company raised around GBP 4.5 million (~$5.8 million) to develop its on-chip photonics designs and launched a quantum PIC packaging service, expanding from design into the supply of packaged photonic chips for quantum and adjacent markets.
