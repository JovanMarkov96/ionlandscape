Kvantify is a Danish quantum-and-high-performance-computing software company, headquartered in Copenhagen with its origins in Aarhus. Founded in 2022 by Aarhus University researchers Hans Henrik Knudsen (CEO), Professor Nikolaj Thomas Zinner and Allan Grønlund, it set out to combine advanced computing with practical applications in the life sciences.

The company develops software that runs across classical CPUs and GPUs as well as quantum computers, applying whichever combination best solves a given problem in chemistry and biology — ultimately with the aim of discovering molecules that cure diseases. Its Qrunch engine brings quantum-chemistry calculations to quantum hardware, and its broader platform targets the prediction of molecular properties relevant to drug discovery.

Kvantify has built a highly interdisciplinary team — spanning quantum computing, algorithm development, artificial intelligence, quantum chemistry, mathematics and bioinformatics — and operates from offices in Copenhagen, Aarhus and London.
