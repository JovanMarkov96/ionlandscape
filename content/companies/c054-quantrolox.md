QuantrolOx is an Oxford, UK software company founded in 2021 to automate the control of quantum computers using machine learning. Its founders are Professor Andrew Briggs and Professor Natalia Ares of the University of Oxford, together with deep-tech entrepreneur Vishal Chatrath (CEO) and Dominic Lennon.

Tuning qubits is one of the most time-consuming parts of operating a quantum computer: each qubit must be characterised, calibrated and stabilised, and doing this by hand does not scale to large machines. QuantrolOx's software uses machine learning to perform this tuning automatically, keeping qubits operating at their best and freeing scientists from painstaking manual calibration. The approach is designed to be technology-agnostic, applicable to spin, superconducting and other qubit platforms.

The company raised a GBP 1.4 million seed round in 2022 led by Nielsen Ventures and Hoxton Ventures — with Voima Ventures, Remus Capital and Amadeus/ARM co-founder Hermann Hauser among the investors — and a further EUR 3.5 million seed round led by Voima Ventures in 2023. It operates across Oxford and Finland.
