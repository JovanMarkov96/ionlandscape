Qilimanjaro Quantum Tech is a Barcelona, Spain company founded in 2019 as a spin-out of the Institute for High Energy Physics (IFAE/BIST), the University of Barcelona and the Barcelona Supercomputing Center. Its founders include Víctor Canivell (chief business officer), José Ignacio Latorre, Pol Forn-Díaz (chief hardware architect) and Artur García Sáez (chief software architect).

The company takes a distinctive path to quantum hardware: rather than gate-based machines, it builds analog quantum computers and coherent quantum annealers. Analog quantum computing performs computations in a continuous way that requires less fine-grained interaction with the qubits, which Qilimanjaro argues is a more practical route to near-term, application-specific quantum advantage. It develops the full stack — from superconducting hardware to software and cloud access via its SpeQtrum platform — and in 2026 inaugurated an analog quantum computer at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center.

Unusually, Qilimanjaro funded its early years largely through international client contracts rather than venture capital, and it has won European support including an EIC Transition grant for the RoCCQeT project and a role in the EuroQCS-Spain initiative co-financed by the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking.
