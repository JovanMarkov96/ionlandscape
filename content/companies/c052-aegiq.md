Aegiq is a Sheffield, UK company founded in December 2019 as a spin-out of the University of Sheffield, commercialising roughly two decades of research led by Professors Maurice Skolnick and Jon Heffernan into single-photon technology. It is led by co-founder and CEO Dr Maksym (Max) Sich with CTO Dr Scott Dufferwiel.

The company's foundational technology is proprietary deterministic single-photon sources — a long-standing bottleneck for photonic quantum technologies, where many applications need reliable, on-demand single photons. Around this, Aegiq is building an integrated quantum-photonics platform that targets both quantum networking, including quantum key distribution and even free-space and satellite links, and large-scale photonic quantum computing.

Aegiq won the Institute of Physics Business Start-Up Award and has raised over GBP 3.5 million, including a GBP 1.8 million seed round led by High-Tech Gründerfonds with Deepbridge Capital and angel investors, alongside support from Innovate UK's Quantum Technologies Challenge.
