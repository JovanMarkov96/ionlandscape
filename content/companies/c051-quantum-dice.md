Quantum Dice is an Oxford, UK company founded in 2020 as a spin-out of the University of Oxford's Department of Physics, by a student team that won the inaugural Student Entrepreneurs' Programme: Ramy Shelbaya (CEO), Wenmiao Yu, George Dunlop, Marko Mayr and Zhanet Zaharieva. It develops quantum random number generators (QRNGs) for trustworthy cryptographic randomness.

The company's differentiator is trust. Many QRNGs require users to assume the device is behaving correctly; Quantum Dice's patented source-device-independent self-certification (DISC) protocol — originally developed in Oxford's Quantum Optics group led by Professor Ian Walmsley — continuously verifies, in hardware, that the randomness being produced is genuinely quantum. Combined with integrated photonics, this yields a compact, high-throughput generator suitable for embedding in security products.

Quantum Dice raised GBP 2 million (~$2.7 million) in pre-seed funding in 2021, comprising a GBP 1 million venture consortium led by Elaia Partners with IP Group and the UK Innovation and Science Seed Fund, plus GBP 1 million in non-dilutive grant funding from the IP Group and Innovate UK Quantum Investment Accelerator.
