QMware is a hybrid quantum-cloud company headquartered in Rorschach, Switzerland, established as a joint venture between Terra Quantum (Switzerland) and Novarion Systems (Austria). It builds and operates data centres that combine classical high-performance computing with quantum processors, aiming to bridge the gap between today's HPC and emerging quantum hardware for commercial use.

Announced in 2020 by Terra Quantum founder and CEO Markus Pflitsch together with Novarion CEO Georg Gesek, QMware positions itself as a market leader in hybrid quantum computing (HQC). Rather than simply brokering remote access to a third-party quantum machine, it runs its own integrated infrastructure so that classical and quantum resources can work together with low latency on enterprise workloads.

The company has built partnerships across the quantum ecosystem to expand the hardware available through its platform, including an agreement with the Netherlands' QuiX Quantum to establish a fully integrated hybrid quantum-computing data centre and a collaboration with the neutral-atom company QuEra.
