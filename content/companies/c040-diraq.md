Diraq is an Australian quantum-computing company spun out of UNSW Sydney in 2022 and led by Prof. Andrew Dzurak, a pioneer of CMOS-compatible silicon spin qubits. Its qubits are electron spins confined in silicon quantum dots, fabricated with the same standard semiconductor manufacturing used for conventional chips — an approach Diraq argues is uniquely suited to integrating the millions of qubits a fault-tolerant machine will need.

Diraq has raised more than A$150M from investors including Main Sequence and Quantonation, signed a CHIPS Act letter of intent worth up to US$38M with the U.S. Department of Commerce, and is a Stage A participant in DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative.
