Sparrow Quantum is a Copenhagen, Denmark company founded in 2016 as a spin-out of the Niels Bohr Institute, built around the quantum-photonics research of Professor Peter Lodahl (its chief quantum officer) together with co-founders including Søren Stobbe. It develops deterministic, on-chip single-photon sources.

Single photons emitted one at a time, and as indistinguishable from one another as possible, are an essential building block for photonic quantum computing and for quantum communication networks. Sparrow Quantum's chip-based sources — using quantum dots embedded in photonic-crystal waveguides — are among the most precise in the world, and the company supplies them as a key enabling component to others building photonic quantum systems.

Sparrow Quantum has raised about EUR 27.5 million, including a EUR 21.5 million Series A led by the Danish pension fund PensionDanmark with Novo Holdings and Denmark's export and investment fund EIFO, and has been recognised as a key technology partner under the EU Chips Act, reinforcing Denmark's position in the global quantum race.
