Quantopticon is a London software company founded in 2017 by the mother-and-daughter physicist team of Dr Gabriela (Gaby) Slavcheva, its chief scientific officer, and Dr Mirella Koleva, its CEO. It develops simulation software for designing and optimising quantum-photonic devices.

Its product, Quantillion, models the behaviour of quantum-photonic devices driven by light pulses with high accuracy — including quantum systems with an arbitrary number of energy levels — and is aimed at components such as single-photon sources and spin-photon interfaces that underpin quantum computing and ultra-secure quantum communications. The methodology, which Slavcheva developed over more than twenty years of research into light-matter interactions in quantum nanostructures, is designed to replace slow, expensive fabricate-and-test cycles with accurate up-front simulation, dramatically shortening optimisation and cutting costs.

Quantopticon participated in the Duality quantum accelerator and was awarded a EUR 175,000 grant from the European Space Agency to support work on secure quantum-communication channels.
