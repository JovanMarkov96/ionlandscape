ColibriTD is a Paris software company founded in 2019 by Dr Laurent Guiraud (CEO), a physicist with prior product roles at Google and AWS, and Hacene Goudjil. It develops end-to-end quantum software designed to integrate with classical computing, with a mission to make quantum computing accessible to industries seeking solutions to real-world engineering problems.

Its flagship technology is H-DES, a hybrid quantum-classical differential-equation solver built on a variational quantum algorithm and applicable to both current and future quantum hardware. Differential equations sit at the heart of engineering simulation — from fluid dynamics to electromagnetics — so a practical quantum solver has broad industrial relevance. ColibriTD has demonstrated H-DES solving a partial differential equation on IBM's 156-qubit Heron R2 processor, which it describes as a first for a variational quantum algorithm on real hardware.

The company raised a EUR 1 million seed round led by Earlybird Venture Capital's Earlybird-X fund to develop its hardware-agnostic Quantum-as-a-Service platform.
