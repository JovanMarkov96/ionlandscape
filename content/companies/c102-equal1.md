Equal1 is a Dublin, Ireland quantum-semiconductor company founded in 2018 as a spin-out of University College Dublin. Led by CEO Jason Lynch, it builds silicon-based quantum computers that integrate the qubits and their control electronics together on a single CMOS chip — a "quantum system-on-chip" approach that reuses the existing semiconductor manufacturing ecosystem.

In March 2025 the company launched Bell-1, which it describes as the world's first silicon-based quantum computer server. Named after the Belfast-born physicist John Stewart Bell, it packages silicon spin qubits and cryogenic control electronics into a self-contained, rack-mountable form factor that can be installed in a standard data centre rather than requiring a specialised laboratory — a step toward making quantum computing deployable alongside classical servers.

Equal1 has raised more than $85 million in total, including a EUR 13.7 million Disruptive Technologies Innovation Fund award in 2025 and a $60 million (EUR 51.5 million) round in January 2026 led by the Ireland Strategic Investment Fund, with Atlantic Bridge, the European Innovation Council Fund, Matterwave Ventures, Enterprise Ireland, Elkstone and TNO Ventures. It employs around 45 people with operations spanning Ireland, the US, Canada, Romania and the Netherlands.
