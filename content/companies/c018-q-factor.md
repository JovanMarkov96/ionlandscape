Q-Factor is an Israeli quantum-computing company developing a scalable neutral-atom
quantum computer with the explicit goal of reaching the million-qubit scale. The
company emerged from stealth in April 2026 with a $24M seed round led by NFX and
TPY Capital, with participation from Intel Capital, Korea Investment Partners and
others, plus a grant from the Israel Innovation Authority.

Its founding team brings together neutral-atom and quantum-optics expertise:
Prof. Nir Davidson and Prof. Ofer Firstenberg of the Weizmann Institute of Science,
Prof. Yoav Sagi of the Technion, and Dr. Guy Raz. The company builds on foundational
atomic-physics research from the Weizmann Institute and the Technion, both of which
are shareholders.
