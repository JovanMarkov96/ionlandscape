Crypta Labs is a London hardware company, founded in 2014, that specialises in quantum random number generators (QRNG). Its devices use the quantum properties of light — detecting individual photons — to generate a quantum seed of genuine, physically unpredictable randomness, which underpins strong cryptography and secure communications.

The company designs its QRNGs specifically for integration by security OEM hardware manufacturers, with a patented portfolio of solutions. Its applications span conventional cybersecurity as well as more demanding environments: Crypta Labs has worked on automotive security (for example with Coventry University on protecting connected and autonomous vehicles) and on space, where it developed what it describes as the world's first space-compliant quantum random number generator for UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) and the UK Space Agency.
