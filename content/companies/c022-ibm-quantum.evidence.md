- **source**: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ibm-unveils-worlds-first-integrated-quantum-computing-system-for-commercial-use-300774332.html
- **quote**: IBM Q System One unveiled at CES January 8, 2019. "World's first integrated universal approximate quantum computing system designed for scientific and commercial use." 20-qubit processor in 9-foot borosilicate glass enclosure.

## quantum_volume_64
- **source**: https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2058-9565/abe519
- **quote**: "Demonstration of quantum volume 64 on a superconducting quantum computing system" — Quantum Science and Technology (2021). 27-qubit system, QV doubled from 32 to 64 via dynamical decoupling and compiler improvements; announced August 2020.

## eagle_processor
- **source**: https://newsroom.ibm.com/2021-11-16-IBM-Unveils-Breakthrough-127-Qubit-Quantum-Processor
- **quote**: "IBM unveiled its first quantum processor with over 100 operational and functional qubits." 127 qubits, multi-level wiring heavy-hexagonal lattice. Announced November 16, 2021 at IBM Quantum Summit.

## osprey_processor
- **source**: https://newsroom.ibm.com/2022-11-09-IBM-Unveils-400-Qubit-Plus-Quantum-Processor-and-Next-Generation-IBM-Quantum-System-Two
- **quote**: IBM Osprey features 433 qubits — "more than triple the 127 qubits of the previous IBM Eagle model." Announced November 9, 2022. IBM Quantum Network had 200+ organizations and 450,000+ users.

## quantum_utility_paper
- **source**: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06096-3
- **quote**: "Evidence for the utility of quantum computing before fault tolerance." *Nature* 618, 500–505 (June 14, 2023). 127-qubit Eagle executed 2D transverse-field Ising model circuits at depths beyond brute-force classical simulation verified by UC Berkeley on LBNL/Purdue supercomputers.

## condor_heron_system_two
- **source**: https://newsroom.ibm.com/2023-12-04-IBM-Debuts-Next-Generation-Quantum-Processor-IBM-Quantum-System-Two,-Extends-Roadmap-to-Advance-Era-of-Quantum-Utility
- **quote**: "IBM Quantum Heron" released — 133 fixed-frequency qubits with tunable couplers, "up to five-fold improvement in error reduction over IBM Quantum Eagle." IBM Quantum System Two operational at Yorktown Heights with three Heron processors. Roadmap extended to 2033. Announced December 4, 2023.
- **note**: Condor (1,121 qubits) confirmed via HPCwire/IEEE Spectrum coverage of the same summit and arXiv 2410.00916.
