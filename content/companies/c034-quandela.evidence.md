## Notes
- **Round naming inconsistency**: The 2021 €15M round is consistently labelled "seed" (Bpifrance/AID language). The 2023 €53.5M round is labelled "Series A" by the company itself (FrenchWeb headline says "SERIE A"), but Quantonation (Source 11) labels it "Series B" — likely because Quantonation counts the 2021 seed as Round A. The profile follows the company's own label: Series A for the 2023 round.
- **€53.5M vs €50M**: The FrenchWeb article headline says "50 millions d'euros" but the LinkedIn page states "€53.5M in Series B funding (December 2023)." The LinkedIn figure is more precise; the profile uses €53.5M (~$57.8M USD) as the canonical amount.
- **Jean Senellart as CTO**: LinkedIn identifies Jean Senellart (likely a relative of Pascale Senellart) in leadership; the exact role is listed as CTO in company context. This requires verification — the profile includes him with source: LinkedIn.
- **Pascale Senellart's role**: The Serena portfolio and company context consistently describe Senellart as CSO / Chief Scientific Officer. Her official CNRS position is Research Director at C2N; she continues academic work while co-founding Quandela.
- **Quandela Cloud users**: The about page shows 2,392 users; LinkedIn states 2,500+. The discrepancy likely reflects different snapshot dates. The profile uses 2,392 from the official about page (Source 5) for headcount context.
- **Massy vs Palaiseau**: C2N (the source lab) is in Palaiseau; Quandela's HQ and manufacturing is in Massy (7, Rue Léonard de Vinci). Both are in the Paris-Saclay cluster. The profile lists Massy as HQ and Palaiseau as R&D office.
- **Lucy product name**: Lucy is the proper name given to the EuroHPC-deployed system; technically it is a Belenos (MosaiQ 12) variant with attocube cryogenic modules added. The "world's most powerful photonic quantum computer" descriptor comes from OVHcloud and EuroHPC press materials (April 2026 inauguration).
- **eDelight / Prometheus**: eDelight is Quandela's proprietary quantum-dot photon source technology; Prometheus is the commercial product name for the benchtop single-photon source. Earlier commercial products included a device called Fresnel (a linearity-optimised source).
- **SPOQC**: Spin-Optical Quantum Computing is Quandela's long-term fault-tolerance architecture, combining spin qubits with photonic circuits. The 100,000x component reduction claim vs. all-photonic error correction is from Quandela's technical roadmap materials.
