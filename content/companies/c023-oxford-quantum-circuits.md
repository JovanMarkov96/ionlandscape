  - Coaxmon architecture places qubits and control wiring on opposite sides of a sapphire

  - First European quantum company on Amazon Braket (February 2022, Lucy) and first
    to deploy quantum computers directly into commercial data centres worldwide
  - Dual-rail Dimon qubit technology achieves error suppression and detection at the
    individual physical-qubit level, targeting a ~10x reduction in the physical-to-logical
    qubit overhead versus conventional surface codes
  - OQC GENESIS (2025) is the first quantum computer deployed inside a New York City
    data centre, integrated with NVIDIA Grace Hopper Superchips for hybrid quantum-AI
    workloads

short_summary: Oxford Quantum Circuits (OQC; founded 2017, Reading, UK) is a University
  of Oxford spin-out that builds and operates superconducting quantum computers for
  cloud delivery under a Quantum-Compute-as-a-Service model. Its patented Coaxmon
  architecture places qubit control on the reverse face of a sapphire substrate, enabling
  compact, low-crosstalk scaling. OQC was the first European quantum processor on
  Amazon Braket (Lucy, 2022), launched the 32-qubit Toshiko enterprise platform in
  2023 alongside a $100 M Series B led by SBI Investment, and deployed its GENESIS
  logical-era system in a New York City data centre with NVIDIA in September 2025.
