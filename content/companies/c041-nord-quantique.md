Nord Quantique is a Canadian quantum-computing company founded in 2020 in Sherbrooke, Quebec, building fault-tolerant quantum computers around superconducting bosonic codes. Rather than spreading one logical qubit across many noisy physical qubits, it embeds error correction directly into each qubit using multimode bosonic encoding in superconducting cavities — an approach it argues dramatically reduces the hardware overhead of fault tolerance, demonstrated through its Tesseract code sustaining information across many error-correction cycles.

The company advanced to Stage B of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative in late 2025 and reached a reported US$1.4 billion valuation in 2026 following an investment backed by Fidelity, targeting more than 100 logical qubits by 2029.
