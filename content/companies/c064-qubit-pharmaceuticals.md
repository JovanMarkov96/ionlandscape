Qubit Pharmaceuticals is a drug-discovery company founded in 2020, with teams in Paris and Boston, that uses computing to accelerate the search for new medicines. It was founded on the work of five academic scientists — Louis Lagardère and Jean-Philip Piquemal (Sorbonne University and CNRS), Matthieu Montes (CNAM), Jay Ponder (Washington University in St. Louis) and Pengyu Ren (University of Texas at Austin) — and is led today by CEO Robert Marino with Piquemal as chief scientist.

Its Atlas platform combines high-performance GPU computing, advanced physics-based molecular simulation and force fields, and quantum-computing methods to build accurate models of how candidate molecules bind to their targets — identifying and optimising promising drug candidates faster and at lower cost than laboratory screening alone. The company also pursues fundamental quantum-algorithm research: with Sorbonne University it has demonstrated methods that drastically reduce the number of qubits needed to simulate molecules, work associated with its Hyperion-1 effort.

Qubit Pharmaceuticals has raised a EUR 16 million seed round (from Xange, Omnes Capital, Octave Klaba and Quantonation) alongside EUR 7.5 million in European Innovation Council grants and France 2030 support, and partners with quantum and computing leaders including Pasqal, NVIDIA and AWS.
