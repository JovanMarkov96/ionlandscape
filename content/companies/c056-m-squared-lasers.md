M Squared is a Glasgow, UK photonics company founded in 2006 by Dr Graeme Malcolm and Dr Gareth Maker (its name encodes the founding date, the founders' initials and the laser beam-quality parameter M-squared). It is best known for high-purity, widely tunable lasers — such as its SolsTiS Ti:sapphire system — that are used across quantum-technology laboratories worldwide to cool, trap and manipulate atoms and ions.

Building on that photonics expertise, M Squared has moved up the stack into quantum systems. Its Maxwell platform is a neutral-atom (Rydberg) quantum computer, described as the UK's first commercial neutral-atom quantum computer prototype when it was revealed in November 2022 at the UK National Quantum Technologies Showcase. The core architecture was developed in collaboration with the University of Strathclyde, combining M Squared's industrial-grade lasers and system integration with Strathclyde's Rydberg-atom and quantum-algorithm expertise.

Alongside computing, M Squared develops quantum sensors — including gravimeters and atomic clocks — and has opened a dedicated quantum centre in the Glasgow City Innovation District, positioning itself as a vertically integrated quantum and photonics company.
