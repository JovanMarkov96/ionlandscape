Planckian is a Pisa, Italy quantum-hardware company founded in 2023 as the first joint spin-off of the University of Pisa and Scuola Normale Superiore. It is led by co-founder and CEO Michele Dallari together with co-founder Marco Polini, and is building the foundational technology required for large-scale quantum computers.

The company's processor integrates superconducting circuits with what it describes as a groundbreaking approach to qubit control, intended to remove some of the wiring and control bottlenecks that limit how large superconducting quantum computers can grow. The goal is a highly scalable architecture that can support the qubit counts needed for practical quantum computing.

Since launching with a pre-seed round backed by Italian and some international investors, Planckian has built research collaborations to accelerate its roadmap, including a 2025 partnership with the University of Naples and work with QTLab on scalable processor designs.
