Atlantic Quantum was a superconducting quantum-computing company spun out of Prof. William D. Oliver's Engineering Quantum Systems group at MIT in 2022, with sites in Cambridge, Massachusetts (systems integration) and Gothenburg, Sweden (chip design). Led by co-founders Bharath Kannan (CEO) and Simon Gustavsson (CTO), it pursued fluxonium qubits — a superconducting qubit with larger anharmonicity and longer coherence than the conventional transmon — integrated with co-located cryogenic control electronics to ease scaling.

The company raised a $9M seed round led by The Engine, won AFWERX contracts with MIT for the U.S. Air Force, and was selected for Stage A of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative. In 2025 it was acquired by Google Quantum AI, which folded its fluxonium and modular-chip expertise into Google's fault-tolerance roadmap.
