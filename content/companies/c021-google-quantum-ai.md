short_summary: Google Quantum AI (founded 2012, Santa Barbara CA) is Alphabet's superconducting
  quantum computing division. It achieved quantum supremacy with the 53-qubit Sycamore
  processor in 2019, demonstrated below-threshold quantum error correction with the
  105-qubit Willow chip in December 2024, and claimed verifiable quantum advantage
  with the Quantum Echoes algorithm in October 2025. The division operates its own
  qubit fabrication facility and publishes the open-source Cirq framework, pursuing
  a five-milestone roadmap toward commercially useful error-corrected quantum computation
  by 2029.
