- `status.operating_status` is `public` (not `private`): Xanadu completed its Nasdaq/TSX listing (XNDU) on March 27, 2026 via SPAC with Crane Harbor Acquisition Corp. The task brief suggested `private` but the current status is definitively public.

- Aurora's Nature publication date is January 22, 2025 per the PRNewswire announcement; exact Nature DOI not retrieved but the paper is confirmed peer-reviewed in Nature.
- Headcount of ~323 comes from RocketReach (March 2026 data); consistent with Wikipedia (~290 in July 2025) and the upward trajectory.
- Nathan Killoran's role has evolved: earlier listed as "Head of Software" or "CTO—Software"; as of 2025–2026 the confirmed title is SVP, Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing Software per RocketReach/Datanyze.
- The `offices` entry for Toronto manufacturing refers specifically to the photonic packaging facility opened June 2025 — not a separate city from HQ, but a distinct functional site within the same city.
