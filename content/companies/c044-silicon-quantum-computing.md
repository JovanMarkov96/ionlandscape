Silicon Quantum Computing (SQC) is Australia's first quantum-computing company, founded in 2017 as a UNSW Sydney spin-off by Prof. Michelle Simmons, a pioneer of atomic-scale silicon fabrication and former Australian of the Year. SQC builds "precision atom qubits": individual phosphorus donor atoms positioned in silicon with atomic precision via its proprietary PAQMan (Precision Atom Qubit Manufacturing) process, which uses scanning-tunnelling-microscope lithography — a fundamentally different route from the quantum-dot spin qubits pursued by most silicon competitors.

Backed by more than A$280M from the Australian government, UNSW, Telstra, Commonwealth Bank and the National Reconstruction Fund, SQC has built a full-stack quantum computer in-house and is a Stage A participant in DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative.
