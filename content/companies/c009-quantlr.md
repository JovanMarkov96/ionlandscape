QuantLR is an Israeli quantum technology startup focused on building the foundational infrastructure for the quantum internet. Spun out of research conducted at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, the company is developing components necessary for secure, long-distance quantum communication. 

The primary challenge in quantum networking is that quantum signals (photons) degrade over long distances in optical fibers, and unlike classical signals, they cannot be simply copied and amplified due to the no-cloning theorem. QuantLR is developing deterministic quantum repeaters utilizing strong photon-atom interfaces to enable entanglement distribution over global scales, solving the critical distance limitation in quantum key distribution (QKD) and quantum networks.
