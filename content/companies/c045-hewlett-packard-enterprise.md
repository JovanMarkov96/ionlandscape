Hewlett Packard Enterprise (NYSE: HPE) is a global IT, networking and high-performance-computing company. Through Hewlett Packard Labs, it runs a quantum-supercomputing research program that was selected for Stage A of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative, where its entry is characterised as superconducting qubits with advanced fabrication coupled to HPE's supercomputing-integration expertise.

Rather than treating quantum as a standalone machine, HPE frames it as a tightly integrated accelerator inside classical HPC systems — an approach laid out in its SC24 paper "How to Build a Quantum Supercomputer." Its QBI consortium draws on superconducting-hardware expertise from figures including John Martinis of Qolab.
