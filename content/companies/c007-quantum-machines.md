Quantum Machines (QM) is a global leader in providing quantum control and orchestration systems. Founded in 2018 in Tel Aviv, Israel, their mission is to bring about the era of quantum computing by creating the operational foundation—both hardware and software—that makes practical quantum computers possible. 

Their flagship product line revolves around the **Quantum Orchestration Platform (QOP)**, a comprehensive system constructed from modular, scalable **OPX** hardware modules. These modules utilize highly specialized FPGA (Field Programmable Gate Array) architecture designed specifically for the low-latency, real-time requirements of quantum operations.

### Core Technology Strategy
Quantum Machines adopts a universal, processor-agnostic approach. While different companies build quantum processors (QPU) using diverse technologies like superconducting circuits, trapped ions, or neutral atoms, QM focuses exclusively on the complex control layer sitting between the classical computer and the quantum chip. 

By offloading complex real-time operations, error correction, and pulse sequencing to their dedicated orchestration platform, they allow quantum hardware developers to extract the maximum performance from their qubits.

Key differentiators include:
*   **Universal Compatibility**: Designed to operate with any quantum hardware modality.
*   **QUA Language**: An intuitive, open-source programming language specifically designed for quantum pulse-level operations, integrating classical control flow seamlessly with quantum operations.
*   **Real-time Operations**: Capable of executing multi-qubit feedback and error correction protocols within the strict coherence time limits of quantum states.

In February 2025, the company reinforced its position in the market by securing a substantial $170 million Series C funding round.
