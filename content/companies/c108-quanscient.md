Quanscient is a Tampere, Finland company founded in 2021 by Juha Riippi (CEO), Alexandre Halbach (CTO), Asser Lähdemäki and Valtteri Lahtinen. It develops cloud-native multiphysics simulation software and is one of the companies betting that quantum computing will eventually transform engineering simulation.

Multiphysics simulation — modelling coupled physical effects such as fluid flow, electromagnetics, heat and structural mechanics — is central to designing everything from electronics to fusion reactors, but it is computationally demanding. Quanscient's Allsolve platform attacks this by running simulations massively in parallel in the cloud, delivering large speed-ups over traditional tools, while the company simultaneously develops quantum algorithms intended to extend simulation capability beyond what classical methods can reach as quantum hardware matures.

Quanscient raised a EUR 5.2 million early round and, in 2026, a EUR 10 million Series A led by the Danish quantum fund 55 North and Austrian industrial investor B&C Group, with re-participation from Maki.vc, Crowberry Capital, QAI Ventures and First Fellow Partners, to scale its platform for the AI and quantum era.
