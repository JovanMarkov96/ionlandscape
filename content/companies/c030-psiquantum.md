  differentiators:
  - Only quantum computing company manufacturing at a leading semiconductor foundry
    (GlobalFoundries Fab 8, Malta NY) on 300mm wafers at 45nm lithography, enabling
    genuine volume production of photonic quantum chips
  - Omega chipset (announced Feb 2025, published in Nature) achieves 99.98% single-qubit
    SPAM fidelity and 99.72% chip-to-chip quantum interconnect fidelity — the first
    mass-manufacturable photonic quantum computing chipset
  - Fusion-based quantum computing (FBQC) architecture tolerates photon loss via loss-tolerant
    error-correcting codes and multiplexing, removing the need for near-perfect photon
    sources
  - Operates at >100x warmer temperatures than superconducting qubit systems (~4K
    for detectors vs. ~15mK), using compact cryogenic cabinets (~30W) instead of
    dilution refrigerators
  - Selected by DARPA US2QC program through R&D viability and into validation phase
    (Feb 2025) — only photonic approach to reach this stage
  elevator_pitch: PsiQuantum is building fault-tolerant, utility-scale quantum computers
    using silicon photonics manufactured at GlobalFoundries' high-volume semiconductor
    fab. Its fusion-based approach targets million-qubit systems deployed in dedicated
    Quantum Compute Centers in Brisbane, Australia and Chicago, IL.
