Molecular Quantum Solutions (MQS) is a Copenhagen, Denmark software company founded in 2021 that provides quantum-chemistry-as-a-service for the pharmaceutical, biotech, chemical and materials industries. Its platform calculates the thermodynamic and other properties of molecules and materials so that companies can screen candidates computationally rather than through expensive laboratory experiments.

The company's product, Cebule, is a pipeline that combines quantum chemistry, molecular dynamics and graph neural networks, running on high-performance and quantum computers. It includes a visual interface for searching quantum-chemistry data across more than 200 million molecules, an overview of HPC compute tasks, an agentic research conversation log, and a Python SDK for programmatic access to molecular data and prediction models. Target applications range from battery materials and green solvents to pharmaceutical drugs and biodegradable plastics.

MQS raised a EUR 600,000 pre-seed round to further bridge the gap between laboratories and quantum chemistry, and has positioned itself within the European deep-tech ecosystem as a cloud software provider for predicting chemical and material properties.
