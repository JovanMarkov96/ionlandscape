Terra Quantum is a St. Gallen, Switzerland company founded in 2019 by Markus Pflitsch — a former CERN physicist and financial executive — together with Karl Eckstein. It is a full-stack quantum-as-a-service (QaaS) company, aiming to make quantum advantage accessible to enterprises across algorithms, computing and security rather than focusing on a single layer.

The company develops quantum and hybrid algorithms for sectors such as aerospace, transport, pharmaceuticals and finance, and pairs them with a hybrid quantum-HPC cloud — the same hybrid approach behind its QMware joint venture with Novarion — so that classical and quantum resources can work together. On the security side, Terra Quantum's patented quantum-key-distribution protocols, published in Nature's Scientific Reports, set a world record for long-distance quantum-encrypted communication, and its TQ42 platform includes a cryptography library for secure data transmission, storage and authentication.

Terra Quantum has raised around $86 million, including a $60 million Series A led by Lakestar, with later investment from Investcorp, and has won contracts including a US Air Force study on quantum-resistant networks.
