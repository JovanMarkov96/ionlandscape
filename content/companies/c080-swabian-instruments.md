Swabian Instruments is a Stuttgart, Germany company founded in 2016 that builds high-performance time-tagging electronics for quantum and photonics research. Its Time Tagger instruments record the precise arrival times of photons and electronic events across many channels, with very low timing jitter, and perform time-correlated single-photon counting and other correlation measurements in real time.

The company's software-defined approach is a key part of its appeal: rather than building bespoke electronics for each experiment, researchers can configure complex measurement routines in software around a single instrument, with streaming analysis of the incoming time-tag data. This flexibility and precision have made Swabian's time taggers a standard tool across quantum-optics, nitrogen-vacancy-centre, single-photon-source and quantum-networking laboratories worldwide.

As an enabling-technology supplier, Swabian Instruments occupies a quiet but essential place in the quantum ecosystem, providing the measurement backbone that experiments in quantum computing, communication and sensing rely on.
