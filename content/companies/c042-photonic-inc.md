Photonic Inc. is a Vancouver-based quantum company building a distributed, fault-tolerant quantum computer from silicon spin qubits that are optically linked by photons. Its qubits are "T centre" defects in silicon — a spin qubit that natively emits and absorbs photons at telecom wavelengths, giving each qubit an optical interface for entanglement between modules and over existing fibre infrastructure. Founded in 2016 by Dr. Stephanie Simmons (Simon Fraser University), the company emerged from stealth in 2023 with an "Entanglement First" networked architecture.

Photonic has raised over US$140M, with Microsoft among its investors and additional Canadian federal funding, and advanced to Stage B of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative.
