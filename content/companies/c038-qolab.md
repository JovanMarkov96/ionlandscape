Qolab is a superconducting quantum-hardware company co-founded in 2024 by Alan Ho (CEO), physicist John M. Martinis (CTO) and Robert McDermott (Head of Hardware). The company's premise is that building a useful quantum computer is fundamentally a manufacturing problem: by bringing semiconductor-industry techniques — wafer-scale integration, advanced packaging, and proprietary fabrication developed with Applied Materials — to bear on superconducting qubits, Qolab aims to produce large numbers of high-coherence qubits rather than scaling up low-quality ones.

Martinis, who led the hardware team behind Google's 2019 quantum-supremacy result, was awarded the 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics. Qolab is a participant in DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative, and in December 2025 deployed its first superconducting-qubit devices at the Israeli Quantum Computing Center.
