TOPTICA Photonics is a Munich-area (Gräfelfing), Germany photonics company founded in February 1998 — originally as TuiOptics, renamed TOPTICA Photonics in 2001 — by Dr Wilhelm Kaenders. It develops and manufactures high-end laser systems for scientific and industrial use, with a portfolio spanning tunable diode lasers, ultrafast fiber lasers, terahertz systems and optical frequency combs.

TOPTICA's lasers are an enabling backbone of quantum technology. Cooling, trapping and coherently controlling atoms and ions — the basis of neutral-atom and trapped-ion quantum computers, atomic clocks, and many quantum sensors — requires precise, narrow-linewidth, tunable laser light, and TOPTICA's systems are used for exactly these tasks in laboratories around the world. As Kaenders, whose own background is in cold-atom physics, likes to put it, "a TOPTICA laser is not required for a Nobel prize, but it seems to help."

Now operating as TOPTICA Photonics SE with international subsidiaries, the company remains a privately held, founder-shaped business and one of Europe's most important suppliers of the laser tools underpinning quantum research and emerging quantum industry.
