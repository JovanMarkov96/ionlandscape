Quantum Base is a Lancaster, UK company founded in 2013 as a spin-out from Lancaster University, co-founded by Phillip Speed and Professor Robert Young. It applies quantum physics to security and authentication, with the aim of delivering quantum security to billions of everyday consumer products.

Its flagship technology, Q-ID, creates an optical "fingerprint" derived from atomic-scale randomness — uniqueness that, by its physical nature, cannot be copied, cloned or faked, and which can be verified with a standard smartphone. This makes it a powerful tool against counterfeiting across pharmaceuticals, electronics, luxury goods and documents. The product range extends to Q-ID Electronic, an electronic quantum physically-unclonable-function (EPUF) device, and Q-RAND, the company's quantum random number generator.

Quantum Base's patented Q-ID technology has been deployed to help prevent counterfeiting across global industries, and in 2025 the company won the World Intellectual Property Organisation (WIPO) Global Award in recognition of its innovation, business growth and social impact.
