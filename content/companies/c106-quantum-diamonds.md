Quantum Diamonds is a Munich, Germany company founded in 2022 that applies quantum sensing to one of the semiconductor industry's hardest problems: finding defects inside increasingly complex chips. It was founded by Kevin Berghoff (CEO) and Dr Fleming Bruckmaier (CTO), whose research on diamond quantum sensors for chemical analysis revealed broader applications in chip inspection; the two met at an UnternehmerTUM workshop and launched the company with support from the EXIST Founder's Grant.

The company's systems use nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centres in diamond to detect the tiny magnetic fields produced by electrical currents flowing through a chip. This lets them localise defects at the micrometer scale even within buried layers of modern 2.5D and 3D chip packages, non-destructively and across a wide range of temperatures, providing high-throughput, wide-field imaging for failure analysis and process optimisation.

Quantum Diamonds has raised early venture funding from investors including the EIC Accelerator, IQ Capital, Earlybird and First Momentum Ventures, and in December 2025 announced a facility worth more than EUR 150 million to build quantum chip-inspection systems in Munich, backed by the German federal and Bavarian governments under the European Chips Act.
